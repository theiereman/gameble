# Any season's prestige scores can be read in the back-office, published or not

## Description

The back-office showed the prestige score panel only for the draft season. Every season now has its own read-only back-office page with that panel: a published season's tiers and sets come from what was stored at publication, and the page lists the formula versions that scored the season.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: an administrator opens any season's page by its number and reads its panel and versions; a moderator gets 403, anyone else and an unknown number a 404.
