# Only Legendary cards shine, the glare appears under the pointer

## Description

In the booster, Rare and Épique cards also got the holo sheen and the foil, so a shine no longer marked a Légendaire, and the glare appeared at the centre of a card then slid to the pointer. Only Légendaire cards get holo and foil now, and the glare appears under the pointer and follows it.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: only a Légendaire shows holo and foil, in the booster and elsewhere; on every card the glare appears under the pointer when the pointer starts driving the tilt.
