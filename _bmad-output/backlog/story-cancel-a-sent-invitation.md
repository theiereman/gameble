# Cancel a sent invitation

## Description

A player who sent a friend invitation, by code or by link, takes it back with an « Annuler » button on its row. The invitation disappears for both players, live on the other player's open Friends screen, and the sender may invite that player again, even one who had ignored it.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: cancelling a pending or an ignored invitation removes it from both Friends screens and lets the sender invite again; an invitation already answered, or posted by anyone but its sender, is refused with « Cette invitation n'existe plus. ».
