# The rarity legend counts distinct games in its overall figures

## Description

The rarity legend's "All seasons" figures count a game once per rarity, however many seasons it was drawn in, while its season figures stay one card per game per season.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: a game held in two seasons at the same rarity counts once in the legend's overall owned and total figures, and once in each season's column.
