# The Légendaire glitter twinkles in three groups

## Description

A Légendaire's glitter ran one infinite twinkle animation per flake, up to 40 on a large card, above the budget of six infinite animations visible at once on a phone. Each face's flakes now twinkle in three groups drawn from the card's seed, one animation per group, and the glitter looks the same.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: any seed gives exactly three groups, each with its own period and delay, and the same seed always gives the same groups; a still face shows every glint at 0.18 with no flash, as before.
