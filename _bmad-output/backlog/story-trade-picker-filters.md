# Trade picker filters

## Description

The trade board's card picker listed every card in one grid, with no way to narrow it. A title search and rarity chips at the top of the picker now filter the grid, with the wording and look of the collection filters, and a placed card stays placed while filtered out.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: typing part of a title or turning on rarity chips narrows the grid; no match shows a message and a clear action; a placed card filtered out stays in the selection and the count.
