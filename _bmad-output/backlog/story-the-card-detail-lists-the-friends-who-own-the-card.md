# The card detail lists the friends who own the card

## Description

The signed-in card detail lists the viewer's friends who own the card, those holding the viewed edition first, then those holding only another edition with their editions spelled out.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: a friend holding the viewed edition is listed first, one holding only another edition under its own heading, and a friend with a private profile is not listed.
