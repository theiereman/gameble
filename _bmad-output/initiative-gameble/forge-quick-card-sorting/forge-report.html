Quick card sorting from the booster opening

Outcome: hardened as a V2 requirement. Nothing to build before V2.

Locked decisions

  • The problem is leaving the booster screen and searching the collection; the action must happen in place and keep the player active.
  • Desktop: drag into a "to process" zone. Phone: swipe or buttons, with buttons always available.
  • Marking is reversible until a single validation sells or discards everything in the zone.
  • Cards in the zone leave the collection; the only copy of a card needs an extra confirmation.
  • The "to process" screen must be highly legible to review fast.
  • Built with V2: currency, destruction and the market come first.

Rejected

  • Immediate swipe to sell or discard: too dangerous.
  • Default automatic sorting of duplicate commons: some commons are kept for a set or a loved game.

Weak points that survived

  • A per-copy "to process" state must fit the V2 copy model without a heavy migration.
  • The zone can become a chore if it fills up without showing what it yields.
  • Drag and swipe are two gestures to build and keep consistent.
