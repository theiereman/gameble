idea: Drag a card from a booster opening into quick-action zones (discard/destroy, quick sale)
goal: Test whether it holds up
status: complete
updated: 2026-10-07T09:48
---

- (note) Today duplicates just pile up in the collection, nothing is done with them.
- (direction) Action zones sit below the reveal area, to discard or quick-sell without searching the collection card by card.
- (assumption) Most commons will inevitably be discarded by players (unverified).
- (decision) Commons are not all discardable: players keep some to complete a set or because they love the game. The sorting judgment is individual, per card.
- (crack) A pure automatic rule (destroy all commons) would destroy cards players want; the idea needs a per-card choice or a rule with exceptions.
- (lock) The real problem is leaving the booster screen and searching the collection to do a basic action; the player must act in place, per card, and stay active (no automatic default sorting).
- (decision) Author prefers dragging into zones over a default-sort-with-undo, to keep the player active.
- (lock) Desktop: drag a card into action zones (the gesture is part of the fun). Phone: buttons on the card instead of drag. Same actions on both.
- (direction) Phone alternative proposed by author: swipe left = discard, swipe right = quick sale, instead of buttons (not locked; tension with the pile, undo, a keep gesture, accessibility).
- (note) Still open: what the zones do in the MVP given no currency or market (wait for V2, a keep/surplus zone, or discard for nothing).
- (kill) Immediate swipe to discard or sell on phone: too dangerous (no safety net, no keep gesture).
- (lock) Dragging or swiping a card only marks it into a 'to process' zone (possibly its own screen, on desktop too); the player then validates once to sell or discard everything in it. Marking is in place; nothing irreversible happens until validation.
- (lock) Nothing is built before V2: the idea is a V2 requirement (needs currency, destruction and the market).
- (lock) Cards in the 'to process' zone no longer count as part of the collection.
- (lock) Marking the only copy of a card into 'to process' requires an extra confirmation; spare copies need none.
- (lock) A card can be taken back out of 'to process' until validation.
- (decision) The 'to process' screen must be as legible as possible so the player can review every pending card quickly (answer to the chore risk).
- (assumption) A per-copy 'to process' state must fit the V2 copy model without a heavy migration; check in the architecture before V2.
