## Intent

**Problem:** The Archives hold entrusted copies and the settings version now carries an Archive price per rarity, but nothing sells them: there is no withdrawal record, the hourly stock sum ignores purchases, and there is no shop screen or purchase confirmation.

**Approach:** `game_market_archive_withdrawals` (one row per purchase) with `SumArchiveStocks` rewritten to deposits less withdrawals, never writing zero; `Game::Market::BuyFromArchives` under the player's bank lock, idempotent by purchase token; the public Boutique de la Confrérie screen listing priced stock as Archive offer tiles; a reusable purchase confirmation (Modal, bottom sheet on the phone) opened by a tile, posting to the operation and redrawing itself and the tile.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:** writes only through `BuyFromArchives`/`SumArchiveStocks`; GETs never write (the confirmation GET only generates a token in memory); lock order: the player's bank lock, then the player's holding, then the stock row, then (inside the shared buyer step) player state, then `Ledger.post` last; one `ArchivePurchased` (entry 1's keys `player_id, card_season_id, price`) on the player's stream; strong_migrations expand (create table, then each concurrent index in its own migration); `db/schema.rb` regenerated from migrations on PG18; Pundit on every action; rate limited per player; editions named by card slug and season number, never ids (URLs, HTML, DOM ids); every string in `en.yml` and `fr.yml` (French as the Copy deck draws it, English beside it), new `errors.game.*` keys by hand in both; French U+202F before `:` `;` `?` `!` and inside « »; markup server-rendered, script only clones server `<template>`s and toggles; no `style` attributes; no planning ids; files ≤400 lines Ruby/TS, ≤500 CSS; prices read from the settings version, never constants.

**Never:** the ANNONCES shop panel, Archive offers in the ANNONCES grid or in the order form, the market's PASSER UN ORDRE empty search (entry 16); `BuySlot` or slot dialogs (entry 17, which reuses this confirmation); alt signals for Archive purchases (entry 19); export/privacy copy naming purchases (entry 20); a feed handler or a bell line for `ArchivePurchased`; any edition price refresh; event subscriptions (entry 6 already wired `ArchivePurchased` for goals, achievements and the wanted refresh, and `PlayerStream` for the balance); a daily purchase limit; Légendaire special-casing (a null price simply hides a rarity); a conditional-UPDATE decrement (deferred to the author after measurement).

## Design Notes

- A repeated purchase token answers the purchase already made before any other check, under the bank lock, so a retried POST after the last copy was bought reads as bought, not as out of stock; the unique index is the backstop.
- A fresh purchase token per confirmation GET lets a player buy a second copy of the same edition from a new opening; a double activation of one opening buys once.
- Stock-row contention is left as AD-60 has it (locked read, then decrement or delete); measuring it before the shop opens is the author's (the ticket's deferred unknown).
- Suspended market: the shop stays readable with the banner; tiles lead to the card detail as a visitor's, and a confirmation already open is refused with `:market_closed`.
