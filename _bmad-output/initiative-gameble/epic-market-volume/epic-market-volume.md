# Market volume

## Description

The market carries the cards players want instead of the flow of duplicates, and idle PiCoins get somewhere to go. A player states a firm price for a game with a buy order, its money held from placement; a holder of a wanted copy sells it to the best eligible order at once from Mettre en vente, or from a second step of Confier that sets wanted copies apart and applies every choice in one validation. One wanted table, refreshed by every change to goals, holdings and orders, tells sellers how many players want each copy and buyers which cards they want. The Brotherhood's shop sells the Archives' stock at a fixed price, and PiCoins buy more room for goals, custom sets and decks, in context, at the limit. Archive and slot purchases are destroyed like stamps and commissions. Staff see accounts tied to each other, and the order form never shows a tied account's order as the price to beat. The spec, `market-economy.md` and AD-58 to AD-63 hold the rules; this epic builds them.

## Outcome

For the players: a card missing from a goal can be bought at a price they set, a wanted copy sells at once instead of being entrusted by habit, and saved PiCoins buy a card or a slot. For the author: more cards change hands on the market, PiCoins are destroyed by the shop and slots, and the destroyed-to-created indicator rises from the 8% production ran on 2026-11-19 (`market-economy.md`, Indicators).

## Done when

1. A player places a buy order from a card's detail, finds its PiCoins held in MES ORDRES and cancels it for free; an order nobody serves expires after its duration and its PiCoins return.
2. A holder sells a wanted copy to the best eligible order from Mettre en vente's banner, and from the Confier wanted table in one validation beside an auction and an entrustment; a best order changed meanwhile refuses and nothing moves; the sale counts in the edition's average price.
3. The wanted counts every screen shows equal a fresh `RefreshWanted` after every path that changes goals, holdings or orders, and a banned or deleted player never counts.
4. A player buys an Archive copy from the shop, the market grid or the order form, the stock falls, the PiCoins are destroyed and no feed entry is made; a player at a free limit buys the next goal, custom set or deck slot in context and the blocked action follows.
5. The nightly reconciliation balances with orders, direct sales, Archive and slot purchases; the database refuses a second serving of one order; staff see the two new signals.
6. Shipped to production whole in one 1.3.x release, with no feature flag, the slot grant and the wanted rebuild run at its deploy.
