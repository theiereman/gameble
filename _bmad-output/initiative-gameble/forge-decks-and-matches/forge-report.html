Decks and the Top Trump duel

Outcome: hardened. Release 1.4 ships decks, weekly card stats, the asynchronous Top Trump duel and its ranking. Daily Pixel and knowledge questions come later. Forged idea: forge-decks-and-matches.md.

Locked decisions

  • The deck serves the Top Trump duel of #352; 1.4 ships the duel itself, not only decks.
  • Asynchronous only: two halves of 5 calls each, played in one sitting against the opponent's deck; a call is a card from a hand of 3, a stat and a direction.
  • Players alternate calls and the caller picks the direction, so a deck stacked on one stat wins only its own calls.
  • Points, no capture; every stat frozen at the duel's start, so dragging out a losing duel gains nothing.
  • Five stats played both ways: popularity, rating, release date, discovery, circulation. Rarity is out: it is the review count twice.
  • Only living stats were the author's wish; production data (36 players, 12,434 cards) showed them too sparse. Discovery and circulation stay; living-stat density is remeasured each season to bring more in.
  • Stats recomputed every Monday; decks evolve with what the Brotherhood does.
  • Decks of exactly 12 distinct cards, up to 5 decks; a card in any deck blocks one copy.
  • Friend duels and open challenges among players with a defence deck; 48 h, then the missing half plays itself; no win by forfeit.
  • First win of the day gives a duel capsule; nothing beyond it; no stakes.
  • Elo-style ranking on open challenges only, shown after 5 duels, reset each season, laid out like the Flukeometer.

Rejected, and why

  • Capture until a deck is empty: with alternating calls duels never end, worst of all asynchronously.
  • Deck cards only referenced, with a warning: the author wants them blocked.
  • 30-card decks: only 7 cards show per duel, so composition hardly matters.
  • Rarity budgets or quotas: unnecessary once the direction is the caller's choice.
  • Copies owned as a stat, win-count ranking, stakes: each rewards hoarding, volume or collusion.
  • Continuous stat updates, live play, win by forfeit.
  • Market price as a stat: tied to rarity, and within a tier prices sit at the floor or have no sale.
  • Only living stats, for now: 36 players cannot tell 12,434 cards apart; most rounds would tie.

Weak points that survived

  • A carefully built deck can weaken with no action from its owner; the weekly change arrows must make that legible, not felt as theft.
  • Entrusting duplicates to make a card harder to find is accepted as strategy; watch whether it distorts the market.
  • Automatic halves count in the ranking: a player with a defence deck who stops playing will drift down.
  • The phone's tab bar is full; the duel's place in navigation is open for the UX.
  • The spec's Combat non-goal and the "no stats on the card face" rule must be rewritten.
