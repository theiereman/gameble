# Review: structure and prose lenses, UX spine update of 2026-10-31

Scope: only the text changed on 2026-10-31 in `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md` (diff `ux-diff.patch`, 59 lines added, 35 removed). Lenses: `structure`, then `prose` on top of its findings (bmad-review, editorial lenses). Reader: humans. Style guide: Microsoft Writing Style Guide, with the house style of the spines taking precedence (English prose, French UI strings in quotes, "(author, YYYY-MM-DD)" attributions, `{token}` references, U+202F before `: ; ? !` and inside « »). Line numbers are those of the working copy on 2026-10-31.

**Purpose read:** the two spines exist to help the people and agents who build gameble find, screen by screen and component by component, the binding look (`DESIGN.md`) and behavior (`EXPERIENCE.md`) of the game, with each author decision dated.

**Structure model:** Reference/Database. Readers jump to one component, state or table row, so every rule must be stated where a reader lands, consistently, and in the schema the section already uses. Repetition across sections is accepted where it serves random access; findings below flag only repetition that has already drifted or that duplicates within one section.

**Checks asked for, verdicts:**

- One open trade per friend: fully removed from both spines. No "one-at-a-time", "UN ÉCHANGE À LA FOIS", "per pair" (except the supersedes note) or "opens the trade in progress" remains. Wording drift on where trades in progress are reached (rows 12).
- "ÉCHANGE" always opens a new draft: stated on the friend row, friend profile, profile action, friend chooser and state patterns, all consistent.
- Goal bookmarks on every trade board: stated in DESIGN Goal bookmark and Trade board and in EXPERIENCE Goal bookmark; one frame sentence now contradicts it (row 3), one bullet duplicates it (row 2), one size is missing (row 21).
- Back picker large preview: gone from Motion (Idle float, Card back equip), the card sizes table, State Patterns, Flow 3 and the Copy deck. Two leftovers: the Menu theme picker still points to the old grid (row 1), and the card sizes table does not place the new mosaic sizes (row 20).
- 5 suggestions: no leftover; every mention says 3.
- `[ASSUMPTION]` tags: all well formed (`[ASSUMPTION]` or `[ASSUMPTION: reason]`); four are inconsistent in status or wording (rows 9, 10, 15).
- Planning ids: only `spec CAP-11` is new, in the spines' own "spec CAP-n" convention. "Finalize" (a workflow step name) is new and opaque to a reader (row 9). The rewritten IA line keeps `epic-menu-themes` (row 28).
- French typography: all 183 quoted strings in the added lines use U+202F before `: ; ? !` and inside « »; no fix needed.
