# Coherence review: Le Grand Inventaire (2026-11-19)

Scope: the Grand Inventaire, Set de la Confrérie and Classement des pionniers text in EXPERIENCE.md and DESIGN.md, compared with the rest of both files, the last memlog entries and `docs/design-system.md`. Line numbers are as of this review.

## High

- **[high]** The DESIGN hub layout row puts the panel after a weekly mission and lists the mission twice. DESIGN.md:708 (900 to 1179px): "Left 260 to 300px holds menu, weekly mission, the Grand Inventaire panel, weekly mission, daily capsule, the Chattomètre panel, then set progress". EXPERIENCE.md:93: "the left column stacks the menu, the Grand Inventaire panel, the weekly mission, the daily capsule…", and EXPERIENCE.md:373 says "of the left column under the menu from 720px … above the weekly mission panel". *Fix:* make the DESIGN cell read "menu, the Grand Inventaire panel, weekly mission, daily capsule, the Chattomètre panel, then set progress".
- **[high]** The phone hub order in Hub content leaves out the panel. EXPERIENCE.md:93: "on the phone the blocks stack … kicker, showcase, weekly mission, daily capsule, the feed block …". EXPERIENCE.md:870 says "showcase, the Grand Inventaire panel, weekly mission panel …", and EXPERIENCE.md:373 says "after the showcase on the phone, above the weekly mission panel". *Fix:* insert "the Grand Inventaire panel" between "showcase" and "weekly mission" in the line 93 phone list.
- **[high]** The signed-out allow-lists never mention the three new screens, so a builder following them would send visitors to the landing. EXPERIENCE.md:95: "They reach only the landing, Toutes les cartes …, Le Chattomètre, … and Marché …; … every other screen lead[s] a visitor to the landing". EXPERIENCE.md:597: "Only the landing, Toutes les cartes …, Le Chattomètre, Marché and Annonce open; anything else leads to the landing". Against these, EXPERIENCE.md:54 says "open without an account", :367 says Set de la Confrérie is "Open without an account", and :368 says "A visitor has no pinned row". *Fix:* add "Le Grand Inventaire with its Set de la Confrérie and Classement des pionniers, linked from the landing (author, 2026-11-19)" to both lists.
- **[high]** "Found" does not say which edition's pioneer counts, but pioneers are per edition and the screen covers one season. EXPERIENCE.md:358: "a card is **found** once it has a pioneer, for good". EXPERIENCE.md:321: "each edition has its own pioneer, and the card detail shows the one line for the edition of the published season". A card pioneered in an earlier season's edition but not in the published one could be read as found. "For good" also leaves open whether the count starts again at 0 when a new season is published. *Fix:* "a card is found once its edition of the published season has a pioneer; it stays found for that season even when …; a new published season starts its own Grand Inventaire from 0". Apply the same rule to MES DÉCOUVERTES ("the cards whose published-season edition the viewer pioneered").
- **[high]** On the viewer's own found cards, two badges claim the top-right corner of the face. DESIGN.md:1278: "the 'VOUS' badge, top right of the face … The Brotherhood's copy badge is the collection's copy badge, shown from x0". DESIGN.md:1083: "Copy badge: 'x3' in a small accent box at a card's top-right". EXPERIENCE.md:370 shows the copy badge "on every found card", so every card the viewer pioneered carries both. *Fix:* give VOUS its own place (top-left, where no NOUVEAU tag shows on this grid, or below the copy badge in the same stack, like "3 éd."), and state it in DESIGN.

## Medium

- **[medium]** The copy badge and "found" use different scopes. EXPERIENCE.md:370 counts the badge "across editions", while found and "à découvrir" count the published season only (EXPERIENCE.md:358, :369). A card nobody pioneered this season can still have Brotherhood copies of older editions, yet EXPERIENCE.md:371 draws it as Card not owned "whoever views it". The lock face is defined at :334 as "a game they hold in no edition", which then contradicts both the viewer's own holdings and the Brotherhood's. *Fix:* say outright that "à découvrir" means "no pioneer for the published season's edition, whatever older copies exist". Or count the badge for the published season's edition only, so the badge matches the found count. Then remove the assumption tag.
- **[medium]** The difference from the Chattomètre's privacy rule is stated once, but none of the profile-visibility text carries the exception. EXPERIENCE.md:366: "Unlike Le Chattomètre, a private or friends-only player still appears in the boards and under their cards, by name without a link". EXPERIENCE.md:354: "A private profile has no row, is not counted, and has no rank". The visibility rules do not mention it. EXPERIENCE.md:652 says "PRIVÉ: nobody else, friends included", and the settings copy at EXPERIENCE.md:168 says "Profil privé : vous seul le voyez". Boards with a rank and a count, shown to visitors, expose more than the existing pioneer-row name. A builder who reads the visibility rules may "fix" the boards; a player who reads the setting is promised more privacy than they get. The Chattomètre's weekly entry avoids naming a friends-only winner to everyone (:352), while the boards name private players to visitors, and the text gives no reason for the difference. *Fix:* add to the Profile visibility row and the Profil et paramètres summary that "your name stays as pioneer, on the cards you found first and in the pioneer boards, without a link". Add the reason to :366 (pioneers are a shared record, as the delete dialog keeps first discoveries). Also state that private players hold ranks and are counted.
- **[medium]** The Names wording contradicts its own first clause. EXPERIENCE.md:366 first says "a link to the profile for a viewer allowed to see it", then "a private or friends-only player still appears … by name without a link". A friend viewing a friends-only player is allowed to see the profile and should get a link (Pioneer row :321; Chattomètre :354, "a friends-only player seen by a friend … opens their player card"). *Fix:* "a private player, or a friends-only player seen by anyone but a friend, still appears … by name without a link". Also add "the pioneer boards and Le Grand Inventaire" to the shared Names row (EXPERIENCE.md:395).
- **[medium]** The tab bar does not say which tab owns the new screens. EXPERIENCE.md:283 says "a tab carries `aria-current="true"` on its sub-screens: PONT over Fil d'activité and Le Chattomètre …", and :890 says "Each tab keeps its own back". The Grand Inventaire is reached from the hub (PONT) and from the collection (SETS), and a visitor's tab bar has only ACCUEIL and TOUTES LES CARTES. *Fix:* name the owning tab, for example "SETS over Le Grand Inventaire, Set de la Confrérie and Classement des pionniers", plus the visitor's case, and add it to the Tab bar row.
- **[medium]** The grid borrows the "Filter zone of Toutes les cartes" without deciding which of its groups apply. EXPERIENCE.md:369: "with the search and the Filter zone of Toutes les cartes". EXPERIENCE.md:332: "Groups: state (absent for a signed-out visitor), OBJECTIFS …, rarity, set …, genre". EXPERIENCE.md:335 adds the PRIX group and the price sort. EXPERIENCE.md:372 implies the visitor keeps the state group (only MES DÉCOUVERTES is dropped), which contradicts "state (absent for a signed-out visitor)". The text is silent on OBJECTIFS, PRIX, the price sort and the SortComponent, and on whether the set group lists custom sets. *Fix:* list the groups as the Set album row does. For example: "state (DÉCOUVERTES, TOUT, À DÉCOUVRIR, shown to visitors too since it reads the Brotherhood, plus MES DÉCOUVERTES signed in), rarity, set (game sets of the season only), genre; no OBJECTIFS, no PRIX, no sort control: the order is fixed". Add a line about the Grand Inventaire to the Filter zone row.
- **[medium]** The card detail opened from the grid does not match the grid. EXPERIENCE.md:369: "A card opens its card detail, whose back reads '◀ GRAND INVENTAIRE'". EXPERIENCE.md:319 says ◀ ▶ follow "the collection grid as shown, with its filters … anywhere else …, the card's tome", and it greys a card by the viewer's own holdings while a visitor sees it plain. So a card shown "à découvrir" and locked for everyone opens plain, or owned, in the detail, and ◀ ▶ leave the Grand Inventaire's order. No back label is given for a card opened from Set de la Confrérie, and none for Classement des pionniers itself. *Fix:* add a Grand Inventaire case to the card detail's ◀ ▶ list ("the Grand Inventaire's grid as shown, back ◀ GRAND INVENTAIRE"; from Set de la Confrérie, its order, back with the set's name). State what the pioneer row shows for a card with no pioneer this season. Give Classement des pionniers its back label ("◀ GRAND INVENTAIRE").
- **[medium]** The landing 04 module rows were not updated for the second See-more link. EXPERIENCE.md:231 (copy) and :54 (IA) add "Voir le Grand Inventaire". The behavior row EXPERIENCE.md:545 still ends "Under the list, after 'Ouvrir le fil d'activité', the See-more link 'Voir le Chattomètre', a visitor's way to the leaderboard". DESIGN.md:1186 (04 · En direct) mentions neither See-more link: "the link to the feed at the foot", and the two columns are "of matching height". *Fix:* in :545 and DESIGN 04, state that both See-more links stack under "Ouvrir le fil d'activité", in that order, as link-variant buttons, and say how the left column keeps its matching height (or that the season panel stretches).
- **[medium]** The gold rule does not cover the new gold uses, and the Panels line says it never happens. DESIGN.md:618: "Gold belongs to the Légendaire tier, the 'PREMIÈRE DÉCOUVERTE' chip, the quantity badge and the 'DERNIÈRE OBTENTION' kicker. It is never a general accent; the one exception is Le Chattomètre". DESIGN.md:1274: "never gold (gold stays the Chattomètre's exception)". DESIGN.md:1278 then colours "The viewer's own pioneer name and the 'VOUS' badge … in `{colors.gold}`". *Fix:* add "the viewer's own pioneer name and VOUS badge in Le Grand Inventaire, the PREMIÈRE DÉCOUVERTE chip's kin" to the Colors gold list. Reword :1274 to "panel titles never gold".
- **[medium]** The screen shows a visible h1, which the house design system forbids. DESIGN.md:1273: "Head: the kicker …; the h1; the count …", and EXPERIENCE.md:362 says "The h1 is 'Le Grand Inventaire'". `docs/design-system.md` (Screen name and top bar) says: "A screen shows no title at the top of its content. Its name is in the top bar … Keep a visually hidden `h1`". *Fix:* make the h1 visually hidden and put the name in `screen_title`, with the season kicker heading the head; or record the exception explicitly.
- **[medium]** The phone tabs name a pattern that conflicts with the house tabs component. EXPERIENCE.md:362: "the tabs follow Tabs and filters", and EXPERIENCE.md:254 says "aria-pressed, in a role="group" …; never role="tab" or a <nav>". `docs/design-system.md`: "`.tabs` … A `nav.tabs` of `.tabs__tab` (current: … `[aria-selected=true]`)". CARTES / PIONNIERS / SETS switch whole views, which is the `.tabs` job. *Fix:* choose one: use `.tabs` (and reconcile its nav markup with the EXPERIENCE rule), or keep pressed buttons and say why. Also state whether the chosen tab lives in the URL, so Back restores it.
- **[medium]** The visitor's sign-in line is placed two different ways. EXPERIENCE.md:372: "in place of each board's 'Vous' row the line … once per screen, under the boards". "In place of each board's row" means two lines; "once per screen" means one. The memlog says "once under the boards". The text is also silent on whether Classement des pionniers shows the line to a visitor. *Fix:* "no 'Vous' row on either board; once per screen, under the two boards (in the PIONNIERS tab on the phone), the line … ; Classement des pionniers shows it once above the rows".
- **[medium]** No line covers an empty board when the season is not empty. EXPERIENCE.md:645 gives the empty-season line only when "nothing found yet", and :647 covers only the viewer's own no-discovery row. Every Monday from 00:00 UTC, Pionniers · Semaine has no top 3 while the season has discoveries. *Fix:* add a state row "Pioneer board, nobody this period", for example "Aucune découverte cette semaine pour l'instant.", in place of the top 3.
- **[medium]** "Semaine" means two different weeks on the same hub from Monday to Wednesday. EXPERIENCE.md:351: "From Monday 00:00 UTC to the end of Wednesday, the Semaine chip reads last week's result". EXPERIENCE.md:365: "Semaine (from Monday 00:00 UTC)", which is the current week. The two panels sit stacked in the same column, so for three days each week the same label shows two different periods. *Fix:* accept it and record that it is deliberate, or label the Chattomètre's verdict state "Semaine passée" on its chip.

## Low

A reader can take "low" to mean low in the column, against EXPERIENCE.md:93, "at the top". *Fix:* "the Grand Inventaire panel (a short panel) at the top, then …".
- **[low]** The rule exception is stated only in the new sections. EXPERIENCE.md:336 says "'x3' shows copies owned, from x2 (a single copy shows no badge)", and DESIGN.md:1083 says "from x2". The Grand Inventaire shows the badge "from x0" (DESIGN.md:1278, EXPERIENCE.md:370). *Fix:* add to both Copy badge rows: "Le Grand Inventaire shows the Brotherhood's count instead, from x0, with no edition count".
- **[low]** "COMPLET" in the Sets panel collides with the player's completion word. EXPERIENCE.md:182 lists "COMPLET" among the panel strings, while the set view says "ENTIÈREMENT DÉCOUVERT" and COMPLET is a player's own set stamp (:330). *Fix:* use "ENTIÈREMENT DÉCOUVERT" (or no tag) on fully found rows, or say where COMPLET shows and why.
- **[low]** A sentence is missing its full stop. EXPERIENCE.md:364: "fully found ones first, then by cards missing Each row opens that Set de la Confrérie". *Fix:* "…then by cards missing. Each row opens…".
- **[low]** "Tout voir" in the Sets panel unfolds in place but is not specified as a disclosure. EXPERIENCE.md:364 says "unfolds the full list in place". Under `docs/design-system.md` it is a `ButtonComponent variant: :link`. *Fix:* state `aria-expanded`, its folded label, and that it is the link variant.
- **[low]** One French link has two English versions. EXPERIENCE.md:181 gives "Voir tout le classement" as "See the full leaderboard" (Chattomètre); EXPERIENCE.md:182 gives the same French as "See the full ranking". *Fix:* use one English string, or note that the two are distinct keys.
- **[low]** The viewer's own card reads "Vous" in the grid, but the card detail's pioneer row shows the player's own name, and the accessible name pattern "<game>, <rarity>, découverte par <player>" says nothing for the viewer's case. *Fix:* give the accessible name for it ("découverte par vous") and decide whether the detail also says "Vous".
- **[low]** Banned or deleted players' places on the boards are undefined. EXPERIENCE.md:366 makes them "anonyme", and the delete dialog (EXPERIENCE.md:525) keeps "first discoveries … without the Steam id or the name". Nothing says whether anonymous players keep a ranked row and count, so several "anonyme" rows could appear, one possibly at rank 1. *Fix:* state it: they keep their row as "anonyme", or they leave the boards while their cards stay found.
- **[low]** DESIGN gives no page width for the screens. DESIGN.md's per-screen table has no row for Le Grand Inventaire, Set de la Confrérie or Classement des pionniers. `docs/design-system.md` puts grids and dashboards on `page--wide` (1280px) and leaves a ranking list unassigned. *Fix:* add a row: Grand Inventaire wide, Set de la Confrérie as the Set album, Classement des pionniers regular.
- **[low]** The Classement des pionniers period chips are less specified than the Chattomètre's. EXPERIENCE.md:368 says only "period chips Semaine and Saison". The Chattomètre's (:349) carry a named `role="group"`, a polite status line and focus kept on the chip, and `docs/design-system.md` assigns "This week / All time" choices to `SegmentedComponent`. *Fix:* reuse the Chattomètre's chip rules by reference, with a group name such as "Période du classement".
- **[low]** The new lime use is not in the lime list. DESIGN.md:1273 says "the pace line's counts in `{colors.lime}`". DESIGN.md:616 limits lime to the primary fill, count badges, the online dot, pips, PRÊTE, NOUVEAU, the active back and a won round. *Fix:* add "the Grand Inventaire's pace counts" to the list, or use `{colors.text-bright}`.
- **[low]** The season name example sits close to the screen name. EXPERIENCE.md:231 gives the season example "Saison 1 · Grand Catalogue", and the Grand Inventaire head uses the season name as its kicker (:363), so it reads "Saison 1 · Grand Catalogue" above "Le Grand Inventaire". *Fix:* none needed if the season name is placeholder data; otherwise avoid "Grand …" season names.
- **[low]** The Card not owned row does not list the new screens. EXPERIENCE.md:334 lists every place the face is used and says "A visitor's Toutes les cartes sets nothing apart". The Grand Inventaire uses it for everyone, visitors included. *Fix:* add "in Le Grand Inventaire and Set de la Confrérie, for every viewer, for a card with no pioneer (see Le Grand Inventaire)".
