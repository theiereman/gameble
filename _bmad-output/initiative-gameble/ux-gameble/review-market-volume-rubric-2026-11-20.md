Scope: the market-volume update of 2026-11-20 only (wanted cards, the Confier wanted table, buy orders, selling to an order, the Archives shop, PiCoin capacity slots). ## Overall verdict

The update mostly works as a contract. Every `{…}` reference in DESIGN.md resolves. I recomputed every stated contrast and ΔE for goal-6 to goal-10 and the archives tokens, and all of them match. Flow 13 is complete and its arithmetic is right. No buy-now or old-limit text survives in either spine. The main gap is in the definitions. Two accepted decisions (memlog 939 narrowing "wanted" to goals, and EXPERIENCE 334 allowing an order on any card not owned) combine so that a buy order on a card nobody has as a goal never reaches a seller in Confier, Vos cartes voulues or the card detail. Beyond that, the new purchase surfaces miss three races a builder will hit (a settings change, a removed participant, self-dealing). Two state rows contradict each other on the suspended market. Ten goals break the bookmark row on small cards. Several new DESIGN values break `docs/design-system.md`. The 76 new `[ASSUMPTION]` tags were accepted as written (memlog 959) but are still in the text, so ticket writers cannot tell what is still open.

Category verdicts: Flow coverage **adequate** · Token completeness **adequate** · Component coverage **adequate** · State coverage **thin** · Visual reference coverage **strong** · Bloat **thin** · Inheritance discipline **adequate** · Shape fit **adequate**.

- **[high]** A buy order on a card nobody has as a goal never reaches a seller anywhere except inside Mettre en vente. Since memlog 939 (author), a copy is "wanted" only when its game is missing from another player's goals. Since EXPERIENCE 334 (assumption accepted by memlog 959), "PASSER UN ORDRE" is on "every card not owned, not only a goal's". The surfaces that put orders in front of holders are all gated on "wanted": the Confier recap only sets wanted copies apart (422), Vos cartes voulues lists wanted copies only (409), and the card detail shows its line "when other players want a card" (334). So Inès can hold 100 PiCoins on a game outside her goals (or keep her order after removing the goal), and Camille's Confier entrusts that game's copy at 50 with no step 2. The order shows only if Camille happens to open Mettre en vente on that exact copy (427). This defeats the intent of CAP-45 ("sells the wanted ones in the same move instead of entrusting them by habit") and CAP-47 ("A holder of a wanted card sells it to an order"). The goal-only rule itself stands as the author's; the problem is the combination. *Fix:* on the seller side, treat a copy as wanted when its game is missing from another player's goals **or** an open order it can serve exists, and count the seekers as before. Alternatively, offer PASSER UN ORDRE only on cards missing from the player's goals. The author decides which.
- **[medium]** A settings change while a purchase is open is handled only for Confier and Mettre en vente (Settings behind an open form, 428). The Archive price and the slot price are read from the settings (341, 434). The order form's rarity minimums decide which rarities a price reaches (432). An administrator can change any of these while a dialog or the form is open, and the player would then pay something other than what they read, which 428 forbids. *Fix:* extend 428 to the Archive purchase confirmation, the Slot purchase and the order form: refuse, redraw the new amount or rarities, keep what was typed, charge nothing. Reuse the existing "Les montants de la Confrérie viennent de changer…" refusal.
- **[medium]** Removed participants and self-dealing are not covered for orders (Participant removed 712 covers Annonce, MES ENCHÈRES and MES VENTES only; banner 427). Three cases are open. A banned or deleted player's open orders: are they released, and can a seller still sell to one and send a card to a banned account? A seller who holds a running order of their own on the same game: does "the best eligible order" exclude it? A buyer who pulls the card from a capsule while their order runs: does the order keep running? *Fix:* add "Order owner removed" to State Patterns (orders cancelled, money released or destroyed with the balance, no bell line), state that every best-order read excludes the viewer's own orders, and say that an order outlives the buyer obtaining the card (or is cancelled, with a bell line).
- **[medium]** Two rows contradict each other on the suspended market. Goal suggestion says neither the line nor "PASSER DES ORDRES" "shows … while the market is suspended" (339). Market suspended lists "PASSER UN ORDRE and PASSER DES ORDRES disabled" (708). In the card detail, "PASSER UN ORDRE" is "disabled while it is suspended" (334), while its sibling actions CONFIER and METTRE EN VENTE are "Absent while the market is suspended" in the same row. *Fix:* pick one rule. Disabled with the banner as reason matches 708 and the market's own screens, and then the card detail's CONFIER and METTRE EN VENTE follow it too.
- **[medium]** Ten goals overflow the bookmark row on small cards (DESIGN 969, sizes in `goal-bookmark`; EXPERIENCE 340). A card in several goals shows its bookmarks side by side. Five 18px bookmarks with 3px gaps already need 102px, against card-s art 100px wide minus the 4px inset. Up to ten goals make six, seven or more plausible (a Supergiant game in a studio goal, a theme goal and a custom set). No rule says what happens: wrap, truncate, or "+n". *Fix:* cap the visible bookmarks per card size (for example three on card-xs and card-s, five from card-m), then a "+n" chip in `{colors.on-goal}` on `{colors.line}`, with the full list kept in the accessible name. Also confirm the two digits of "10" at the 14px small size (967, already flagged).
- **[medium]** The wanted table has no rule for a long batch (EXPERIENCE 424; DESIGN 1375). Goals go up to ten per player and count every player, so in a typical clear-out of duplicates (Flow 8's case) many copies will be wanted. The table can then hold dozens of lines, each of which can unfold the auction fields, and on the phone each line is a stacked panel. Nothing fixes the line order, paging, or how the dock stays usable. *Fix:* order lines by best order descending, then seekers, then value. Say whether the table pages or shows everything (show everything up to N, then "Charger plus"). Keep "TOUT CONFIER QUAND MÊME" reachable in the phone dock when there are more than about five lines.
- **[low]** Small races and visitor gaps. MES ORDRES has no refusal for "ANNULER" pressed on an order served a moment earlier (431); Busy market (710) is not extended to the order form, the shop and the wanted table. The Ordre d'achat IA row (60) does not say whether a visitor can open it. A visitor's Archive offer tile and shop tile are "greyed", yet the whole tile is the one link to the purchase (407, 434). *Fix:* add a "Cet ordre vient d'être servi." refusal to the cancel dialog; add the three screens to 710; say that a visitor reads Ordre d'achat's DISPONIBLE MAINTENANT with the sign-in line in place of VOTRE ORDRE; say that a visitor's tile stays a link to the card detail, not to the confirmation.
- **[low]** "One validation … or nothing" (424) reads as absolute, but the result row (425) has the cap skip copies while the sales and auctions still apply. *Fix:* in 424 write "all or nothing for orders and settings; the daily cap skips copy by copy, as in Confier".

### 7. Inheritance discipline: adequate

I searched both spines: no buy-now remains except the two historical notes (195, 432), and the bell's "bought over a bid" line is gone. No old limit remains: "10 sets au plus", "five goals" and "MES DECKS · n / 5" all read as ceilings now, with free counts 5 / 5 / 3. "offre d'achat" does not appear (memlog 957). Both memlog overrides are carried and named inline (408 "overriding spec CAP-44's near-complete sets", 432 "the form offers no buy-now"). The spec's nouns map one to one: Brotherhood's shop → Boutique de la Confrérie, your wanted cards → Vos cartes voulues, order → ordre d'achat, Archive offer → Archive offer tile.

- **[medium]** Spec drift is not handed off. The spec still says a card is wanted when "from a set that player lacks only 1 or 2 cards of" (CAP-44, spec 354), still has the buy-now clause (CAP-46, 371) and "slots may also be listed in the shop" (CAP-49, 389), and has no ceiling for goals, custom sets or decks (CAP-28 238, CAP-27 230, CAP-40 325). The UX now fixes 10 / 10 / 5 and "never in the shop". `ux-gameble.md`, where "Changes this run asks of the spec" and "For the architecture" live (16, 60), has no market-volume entry. The architecture-level points also have no home: the table validated all at once over other players' escrow, Archive offers paginated together with listings under "se termine bientôt", best-order reads that exclude the viewer, and the 7-day expiry. *Fix:* add a market-volume block to `ux-gameble.md`: the spec changes (CAP-44 narrowing, the CAP-46 buy-now clause dropped, CAP-49 shop clause dropped, the three ceilings) and the architecture items listed here.

### 8. Shape fit: adequate

The screens fit the market's existing family: the order form at regular width as Mettre en vente, the shop on the market grid, MES ORDRES as MES ENCHÈRES, results as Bid result panels, the dock reused. Some new DESIGN values break `docs/design-system.md`, which wins.

- **[medium]** Spacing off the scale, and loud flavour text. design-system.md allows only 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 and 32px, and a compact panel pads 12px 16px. The new bullets use 6px (tags hang "6px outside", the badge "6px in", 1369), 26px (shop tiles "26px apart", 1371), 12px 14px (order banner, 1377) and 14px 16px (Order form result, 1379). The Shop screen's Brotherhood line is italic `{colors.text-bright}` (1372) and the Shop panel's is `{colors.text}` (1371), although "Lore and flavour text is rare, short, small and muted". *Fix:* 8px for the tags and badge, 24px between shop tiles, `padding: :compact` (12px 16px) for the banner and the result panel, and the Brotherhood lines in `{typography.body-small}` `{colors.muted}`.
- **[low]** "passer un ordre" at the end of the Goal suggestion line is plain text that reads like an action (339, Copy deck 214), while the card names are the links. *Fix:* drop the words, or make the line read "À commander : Pyre · Transistor", with the names as the links.
