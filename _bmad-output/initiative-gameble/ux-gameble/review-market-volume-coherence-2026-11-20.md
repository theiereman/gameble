# Coherence review: market volume

Reviewed 2026-11-20 against the uncommitted diff of `EXPERIENCE.md` and `DESIGN.md` (market-volume update), `.memlog.md` 920 to 961, the three `mockups/market-volume-*.html`, `spec-gameble/spec-gameble.md` (CAP-27 to CAP-31, CAP-40, CAP-44 to CAP-49, Constraints, Assumptions), `spec-gameble/market-economy.md`, `forge-market-volume/forge-market-volume.md` and `docs/design-system.md`. Line numbers are those of the files as read on 2026-11-20. Abbreviations: EXP = `EXPERIENCE.md`, DES = `DESIGN.md`, ECO = `market-economy.md`, SPEC = `spec-gameble.md`.

## Verdict

The update holds together on the happy path. Flow 13's numbers are right, every new token resolves, the goal colours match their table, the word "ordre d'achat" is used throughout, and no buy-now remains. Where it fails is at the joins with the rules that were already there. Narrowing "wanted" to goals while letting anyone place an order on any card hides most orders from the Confier flow that CAP-45 built to catch them. Shop spending is missing from the "destroyed" side of the Bourse and its alert indicator. The money forms do not say what happens when the best order or a price changes between drawing and pressing. The daily cap, the stamp and the all-or-nothing table are specified in three rows that do not agree. Navigation, visitor access, the suspended state and the plural forms were not carried to the new screens.

## Findings

- **[high]** An order on a card nobody has as a goal never reaches the seller's set-apart flow (EXP 334 "On every card not owned, not only a goal's", 408, 409, 422; Copy deck 205; memlog 939, 959). Under the author's narrowing (memlog 939), a card is wanted only when it is missing from another player's goals. But "PASSER UN ORDRE" sits on every card the player does not own, and the market's empty search places an order on any game. A copy with an eligible order and no goal seeker gets no VOULUE badge, never shows in Vos cartes voulues, and is not set apart in the Confier recap, so Confier entrusts it at its entrustment value even when an order would pay more. This is the "entrust by habit" loss CAP-45 exists to stop. Only the Mettre en vente banner catches it. The narrowing is the author's and stands; what breaks is that the seller's set-apart and count read goals only, while orders do not depend on goals. *Fix:* set a copy apart in the Confier recap, and show it in Vos cartes voulues, when it has seekers **or** an eligible order. "JOUEURS" reads 0 and "MEILLEUR ORDRE" carries the line. Say this in the Confier, Vos cartes voulues and Card detail rows. The alternative is to offer "PASSER UN ORDRE" only on cards missing from the player's own goals; if so, change EXP 334's `[ASSUMPTION]`.

- **[high]** PiCoins spent in the shop are left out of "destroyed" on the Bourse and in its indicator (ECO Charts line 99 "Destroyed: Stamps, commissions and a deleted account's balance"; EXP Copy deck 216 legend "Détruits (timbres et efforts)", the Reactor line "réduit en cendres chaque timbre et chaque effort"; against ECO Money line 21 and SPEC 398, which destroy Archive card and slot spending). Once the shop opens, the public Reactor, the "DÉTRUITS / FRAPPÉS" figure and the back-office alert ("Below 40%") under-count what is destroyed, so the indicator meant to show whether money piles up reads too low. The Archive purchase row (EXP 435) says the PiCoins are destroyed. The Slot purchase row (EXP 341) says nothing. SPEC 398 and ECO 21 tie slot destruction to "PiCoins spent in the shop", while the author keeps slots out of the shop (memlog 924). *Fix:* in ECO Charts, define Destroyed as stamps, commissions, Archive purchases, slot purchases and deleted balances. Rewrite the legend and the Reactor line to cover purchases ("Détruits (timbres, efforts et achats)"). In the Slot purchase row, say the price is destroyed by the 100-6-Tron. Reword SPEC 398 and ECO 21 to "spent on Archive cards and slots".

- **[high]** No rule says which order a direct sale serves when a better one arrives after the screen was drawn (EXP 427 banner, 424 table "À L'ORDRE · <amount>", 717 Order already served; SPEC CAP-46 "Best price, then oldest, is served first"; EXP 428 "a player never pays or receives other than what they read"). The UX refuses only when the shown order was served or withdrawn. If a higher order arrives meanwhile, serving the shown one breaks the spec's priority, and serving the new one pays an amount the seller never read. Either way one rule is broken, and the build will pick silently. *Fix:* treat "the best eligible order changed" (served, withdrawn **or** outbid) as a refusal, the same as an order served meanwhile. Redraw with the new amount and nothing moves. Add it to the Order already served state, the banner row, the table's One validation paragraph and the Copy deck's "Cet ordre d'achat vient d'être servi" family ("Un meilleur ordre vient d'arriver…").

- **[medium]** Prices read from the settings can change behind the new dialogs and forms, and no rule covers it (EXP 428 Settings behind an open form covers Confier and Mettre en vente only; 341 slot price from the settings, 434 and 435 Archive prices from the settings, 432 rarity minimums behind "RARETÉS ATTEINTES"). An Archive or slot price edited while the confirmation is open, or a rarity minimum changed while the order form shows "Votre prix atteint les … rares", would charge or hold against something the player did not read. MES ORDRES also shows pills of rarities reached without saying whether they are fixed at placement or follow the minimums later; SPEC CAP-30 fixes a setting change to new listings only. *Fix:* extend Settings behind an open form to the order form, the Archive purchase confirmation and the Slot purchase dialog, using the existing refusal copy. State that an order's reachable rarities are fixed at placement, like a listing's minimum.

- **[medium]** The daily cap, all-or-nothing and the totals are stated differently in four places (EXP 424 "applies the whole table at once, or nothing", 425 Over the cap "the sales and auctions still apply", 718, 423; Copy deck 206; SPEC CAP-29, CAP-45). The table row promises all-or-nothing and then mentions the cap in a single clause; the partial result lives in two other rows, both `[ASSUMPTION]`. The Prefill rule chooses CONFIER whenever there is no order, even when the cap left by step 1 is already used. "TOUT CONFIER QUAND MÊME · <amount>" and "VOUS RECEVEZ MAINTENANT" show the full entrustment value, while Confier's rule is that "VOUS RECEVEZ" is "what will really be received". Step 1's outcome is one line at the head of the table (EXP 423), so copies that step 1 skipped over the cap are never named, against CAP-29 ("the screen says so") and Confier result. *Fix:* in the table row, state the cap exception ("all or nothing, except copies entrusted past the cap, which are skipped as in Confier"). Have the Prefill rule skip CONFIER for a line that will not fit, and fall back to ENCHÈRES at the average price. Count only what fits in the dock's totals and in the TOUT CONFIER amount. Name step 1's skipped copies in the head line, with Confier's skipped lines.

- **[medium]** The rows do not say whether the table's sales can pay its own stamps (EXP 424 "the stamp, with the same rules and refusals"; Copy deck 206 "TIMBRE À PAYER"; Market refusals' "Il vous manque <amount> PiCoins pour le timbre."). One validation both receives PiCoins (sales to orders, copies entrusted) and pays stamps (each ENCHÈRES line). With all-or-nothing, a player whose stamps exceed their available balance either has the whole table refused or is funded by the same validation's proceeds, and neither the rows nor the copy say which. No state row or dock line covers a stamp shortfall. *Fix:* decide the order: proceeds first, then stamps, which follows from the single ledger transaction. Then either say that stamps never block when proceeds cover them, or add a dock line and a refusal that point to the ENCHÈRES lines.

- **[medium]** "Offre" still names two things besides a bid (EXP 405 and Copy deck 213 "3 annonces · 3 offres des Archives"; component names "Archive offer tile"; against memlog 957: "'offre' already means a bid"). In the same ANNONCES grid, a listing tile reads "n offres" (bids) or "Aucune offre", and the search count calls the Archive tiles "offres des Archives". The English component name is harmless, but the French count is exactly the collision the author ruled out for buy orders. *Fix:* "3 annonces · 3 cartes des Archives" (or "· 3 en boutique"); keep "offre" for bids only.

- **[medium]** One word, "VOULUE", carries two opposite meanings in the same accent colour (Copy deck 205: buyer's tag "VOULUE" = missing from **my** goals; seller's badge "VOULUE · <n>" and the card detail's "VOULUE" = **others** want my copy; DES 1369 draws both in accent). A player who uses both sides of the market learns that a card marked VOULUE is one they want, then sees the same word on their own copies in Confier. It is the same problem memlog 957 fixed for "offre". *Fix:* keep "VOULUE" for the buyer (the spec's word, CAP-44) and give the seller's badge its count as the label: "<n> CHERCHEURS", or "DEMANDÉE · <n>". Update DES Tags and Wanted count badge, Copy deck 205 and EXP 334, 422, 426.

- **[medium]** The new screens are missing from the navigation and visitor rules (EXP 94 Phone navigation: "Annonce, Confier and Mettre en vente name their way back: '◀ MARCHÉ'…"; 297 Tab bar: "ÉCHANGES over the trade board and the Annonce"; 98 Signed-out visitors; 60, 59, 432 "Back: '◀' naming the screen it came from"). No rule says which tab is current on Ordre d'achat when it is opened from the SETS tab (goal suggestion, card detail, album) or from the market. Boutique has no back rule. The Confier wanted table has its own address (424), yet Back and reload are unspecified: Back lands on a Confier grid whose batch is already entrusted, and returning to the step's address after "leaving without validating" has no defined state. The visitor list (98) sends "every other screen" to the landing, while the Boutique IA row says a visitor reads it `[ASSUMPTION]` and the card detail shows a visitor a greyed "PASSER UN ORDRE" with no screen behind it. *Fix:* add Boutique and Ordre d'achat to the Phone navigation's way-back sentence ("◀ MARCHÉ" from the market, otherwise the screen they came from). Make ÉCHANGES current on Boutique, Confier's step 2 and Ordre d'achat opened from the market, and SETS on Ordre d'achat opened from a goal or card. Say that step 2's address, opened again or reached by Back, shows the table of the copies still set apart, or Vos cartes voulues when none is left. Add the Boutique to line 98's visitor list, and say Ordre d'achat leads a visitor to the landing.

- **[medium]** The goal suggestion's "one or two cards" threshold brings back the near-complete rule the author dropped, and its order chain can stall (EXP 339 `[ASSUMPTION: the threshold as drawn; beyond two the line no longer fits]`, 346, DES 1129, EXP 433, 888; SPEC CAP-46 "a suggestion on the goal itself", no threshold). The line's limit is a layout reason, yet it also gates the album's "PASSER DES ORDRES · n", which has room for any n, while DES 1129 and EXP 346 draw that button on every goal's album. The chain opens on "the first missing card in set order", but advances to "the next missing card the player has no order on". When the first card already has an order, the form shows that order in place of "PASSER L'ORDRE" (EXP 719), so the chain cannot advance. The chain also skips the Order form result, so money is held without the confirmation every other order gets. *Fix:* show the album button on every goal with missing cards; n counts the missing cards without an order. Start the chain on the first card without an order. Show the Order form result after the chain's last order, listing the orders placed. If the suggestion line keeps a limit, call it a display limit ("at most two names, then '+ n'") rather than a condition of being wanted.

- **[medium]** The market-suspended rules contradict each other (EXP 339 "Neither shows … while the market is suspended" vs 708 "PASSER DES ORDRES disabled"; 334 "PASSER UN ORDRE … disabled while it is suspended" beside the same row's CONFIER and METTRE EN VENTE "Absent while the market is suspended"; Copy deck 219 banner "Il n'est plus possible de confier, de vendre ou d'enchérir", which omits ordering and buying from the Archives; 341 and 715, whose short-of-PiCoins slot dialog offers "CONFIER DES CARTES" while Confier is disabled). *Fix:* use one treatment, disabled with the banner as the reason as state 708 says, and apply it in 334 and 339. Extend the banner sentence to "…de confier, de vendre, d'enchérir, de passer un ordre ou d'acheter aux Archives…" and add that running orders stay held and expire normally. While the market is suspended, the slot dialog drops "CONFIER DES CARTES" and keeps only "ANNULER".

- **[medium]** Many new counts lack their one and zero forms (Copy deck 205, 206, 211, 213; AGENTS: "French plural keys need a `zero` form"). "VALIDER LES <n> CHOIX" and "MISES DE CÔTÉ · <n> CARTES VOULUES" both reach n = 1 in Flow 13 (steps 3 and 4), and the flow flags the first as not drawn. The others: "Les <n> autres exemplaires sont déjà confiés", "<n> cartes que d'autres joueurs veulent.", "<n> ventes aux ordres <amount> · <n> carte confiée <amount>", "<n> cartes aux Archives", "<n> en stock", "<n> annonces · <n> offres des Archives", "<n> cartes · <n> éditions". State 714 shows "VOULUES PAR D'AUTRES" reading 0, and "<n> cartes voulues par d'autres joueurs" has no zero form. *Fix:* add the one and zero forms beside each, as earlier rows do ("n sets affichés." (zero, one and other forms)); for one choice, "VALIDER MON CHOIX".

- **[medium]** The spec and the forge still state what the author overrode, and no hand-off records the overrides (SPEC 354 CAP-44 "or from a set that player lacks only 1 or 2 cards of"; SPEC 371 CAP-46 buy-now clause; SPEC 389 CAP-49 "slots may also be listed in the shop" against memlog 924 "the shop sells Archive cards only"; SPEC 230, 238, 325 give no ceiling against memlog 944 and 954 (10 goals, 10 custom sets, 5 decks); ECO Known limits 132 "A near-complete set missing a Légendaire flags it as wanted"; memlog 939 and 940 say "to be carried into the spec"). An architect or builder who reads the spec will build the near-complete rule, the buy-now line and shop slots. *Fix:* carry the five changes into SPEC and ECO through `bmad-spec`: the wanted definition, no buy-now in the order form, slots in context only, the three ceilings, and the stale Known limit. Until then, list them in `ux-gameble.md`'s decisions for the spec.

- **[medium]** Panels are left out when empty, against the design system's "one shape per screen" (EXP 409 "absent when the player has no wanted copy", 410 "With an empty Archives the panel is left out", 714; `docs/design-system.md` Principles: "A new player and a regular see the same structure; only the values change", and `EmptyStateComponent`). A player with no wanted copy sees the chips at the top of the market, while another sees a panel there, so the market changes shape. AGENTS: "where a screen disagrees with it, change the screen, not the rule". *Fix:* keep both panels, each with its count at 0 and one EmptyStateComponent line ("Aucune de vos cartes n'est voulue pour le moment.", "Aucune carte aux Archives pour le moment."). For a visitor, Vos cartes voulues may stay absent, since it is per-player.

- **[low]** Limits outside the new rows still show the old values (EXP 652 No goal "OBJECTIFS shows '0 / 5'", which is "0 / <slots>" for a player with bought slots; EXP 831 "MES DECKS · 3 / 5" read as "3 sur 5", which should be "3 / 3" with 3 free; DES 1121 "so five read at a glance", with ten goals plus their suggestion lines; Copy deck 215 "Les 5 premiers sets perso sont gratuits", which is wrong for the player who keeps a sixth free (EXP 341, 342)). *Fix:* write "0 / <slots>" and "MES DECKS · 3 / 3"; change "five" to "ten" or drop the number; for the six-set player, write "Vos 6 premiers sets perso sont gratuits" (the free count from the player's own free slots).

- **[low]** The balance chip row does not say that held orders count as committed (EXP 404 was edited for new screens only; the rule sits as an `[ASSUMPTION]` in EXP 433, the Order form result). "Vous pouvez engager au plus <amount>" in the order form depends on it. *Fix:* in EXP 404, add "Committed: bids' maximums and buy orders' held prices", and drop the assumption from 433.

- **[low]** English twins are given for two new Copy deck rows (210, 211) and missing from the rest (205 to 209, 212 to 215). - **[low]** "Étape 2 sur 2" has no step 1, and it reuses the phone Mettre en vente's step words (EXP 424, 884; Copy deck 201 "Étape 1 sur 2" for Mettre en vente on the phone).
