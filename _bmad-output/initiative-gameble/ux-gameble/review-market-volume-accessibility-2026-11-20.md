# Accessibility review: market volume

Reviewed 2026-11-20 against the working copy of `EXPERIENCE.md` (Copy deck 205 to 215, Card detail and the market 334, Collection 337 to 342, Bell 292, Marché 400 to 435, Duels 444 and 447, State Patterns 653 to 658 and 708 to 721, Interaction Primitives 751 to 752, Accessibility Floor 823 to 849, Focus after each step 878 to 895) and `DESIGN.md` (Colors 665 to 667, Goal colours 698 to 717, goal-bookmark tokens 586 to 597 and Shape and Sizes 967 to 968, Marché 1358 to 1382), `.memlog.md` 920 to 961, the three `market-volume-*` mocks, `review-market-accessibility-2026-11-09.md` and `docs/design-system.md`. Target: WCAG 2.2 AA. The author has accepted the agent's market-volume assumptions as written (memlog 958), so they stand unless they break a rule elsewhere; no finding below reverses an author choice. Where a mock and a spine differ, the spine wins.

## Verdict

The update carries over most of the market floor from 2026-11-09: statuses in words, the π mark hidden, polite statuses after 500 ms, focus rows for every new result and dialog, 44px phone rows, and new colours that clear their ratios (all recomputed below). It is not buildable yet for a keyboard or screen-reader user on its two hardest screens. The Confier wanted table has no rule for a validation refused on its own fields, and its refused redraw changes choices silently. The order form hides each rarity's minimum in chips that are removed from assistive technology. Several new flows also remove the focused element (the last Archive copy, the sale-to-an-order refusal) or skip their only confirmation (the PASSER DES ORDRES chain). Each fix is a sentence or a row in the spines.

## Findings

- **[high]** The wanted table has no rule for a validation refused on its own fields (`EXPERIENCE.md` 424, 425, 206; Focus 885, 886, 890; 3.3.1, 3.3.3, 2.4.3). "ENCHÈRES" brings "the same rules and refusals" as Mettre en vente, so a table can hold several starting prices, each under its minimum, along with stamps the balance cannot pay ("Il vous manque <amount> PiCoins pour le timbre", Copy deck 198). Rows 424 and 425 and the Focus table cover only the server-side refusal (an order served meanwhile). Nothing says what "VALIDER LES <n> CHOIX" does when line 4's price is too low: whether focus moves, where, whether the other lines are applied, or how a screen-reader user learns that two more lines are wrong further down. Because the validation is all or nothing and sells at once, this is the screen's main failure path. *Fix:* add to row 424: "Fields are checked on submit. With any invalid field nothing is applied: each invalid field shows its refusal under it, with `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`; the dock's line says how many need fixing ('2 prix à corriger', and the stamp line when the balance is short); focus goes to the first invalid field." Add the matching Focus row, and the Copy deck strings in both languages.

- **[high]** On the order form, each rarity's minimum exists only in chips that are hidden from assistive technology (`EXPERIENCE.md` 432, "The rarity chips are text, not controls, hidden from assistive technology behind the sentence, which carries them"; Copy deck 208; `DESIGN.md` 1378; 1.3.1). The chips show every minimum ("COMMUNE <amount>" reached, "ÉPIQUE dès <amount>" not). The sentence that replaces them, "Votre prix atteint les communes, peu communes et rares.", names only the rarities already reached. A screen-reader user never learns what price reaches the next rarity, which is the one figure that guides the price, and has to find it by trial and error. *Fix:* the sentence names the next threshold: "Votre prix atteint les communes, peu communes et rares. Les épiques à partir de <amount>." (with a form for when every rarity is reached). Or keep the chips exposed as a list, each read "Épique, à partir de <amount>, non atteinte", and post only the sentence to the status.

- **[medium]** After a refused validation, the wanted table changes choices without saying so, and the summary of the refusal is never read (`EXPERIENCE.md` 425, Focus 890, Copy deck 211; 3.3.1, 4.1.3). Each changed line's choice is "prefilled anew", so a line the player set to À L'ORDRE can come back set to ENCHÈRES or CONFIER. Focus lands on the first changed group, whose description is that line's sentence. The dock's line ("1 ligne a changé. Rien n'a été vendu, mis en vente ni confié.") is visible only, so a screen-reader user does not hear that nothing moved, how many lines changed, or that a different choice is now selected. *Fix:* the refused sentence of a changed line names the new choice ("…aucun autre n'atteint cette carte. Votre choix passe à CONFIER."); the focused group's description is the dock's line followed by that sentence; the dock's line names the changed lines ("2 lignes ont changé : Pyre, Hades.").

- **[medium]** The labels inside the wanted table are ambiguous once they leave its grid (`EXPERIENCE.md` 424, Copy deck 206, `DESIGN.md` 1375; 1.3.1, 2.4.6). (a) A line is one card *and edition*, but the group is named "<card>, votre choix", so two editions of one game produce two identical group names. (b) Each line set to ENCHÈRES unfolds its own "PRIX DE DÉPART" and "DURÉE", so a form-field list reads "Prix de départ" three times. (c) On the phone the table becomes stacked panels and the column heads that name the figures disappear. (d) Choosing ENCHÈRES adds fields after the group without telling the user (focus stays, rightly), and a radio cannot carry `aria-expanded`. *Fix:* name the group "<card>, <rarity>, saison <n> : votre choix"; name the fields "Prix de départ, <card> <rarity>" and the duration group "Durée, <card> <rarity>"; on the phone each figure carries its column head as a visible label; give the ENCHÈRES radio a description, "ouvre le prix de départ et la durée sous cette ligne".

- **[medium]** The unavailable "À L'ORDRE" is an `aria-disabled` radio inside an arrow-key radio group, and the spec does not say how the keys treat it (`EXPERIENCE.md` 424, 751; 2.1.1, 4.1.2). Native radios select on arrow, so moving through the group changes the choice at each step; a native `disabled` radio is skipped, but an `aria-disabled` one is focusable and, on a native input, still selectable. A builder must pick one of these, and either choice changes what the player hears. *Fix:* state it: "The unavailable segment is a native `disabled` radio, skipped by the arrow keys; its reason, 'Aucun ordre', is the group's description. Moving with the arrows selects, as in any radio group, and the line's 'VOUS RECEVEZ' and the dock's status follow after 500 ms."

- **[medium]** The phone's segment labels cannot fit on one line (`DESIGN.md` 1375, choice-height-phone; `market-volume-seller-options.html` `.sg`: 10px bold, `white-space:nowrap`; 1.4.10, 1.4.12). At 390 CSS px a stacked line has about 330px for three segments, 110px each. "À L'ORDRE · 260" plus the coin mark and the padding needs about 120px; "À L'ORDRE · 70 000" needs about 137px. At 320 px a segment has about 88px. Under the text-spacing override, every label overflows. *Fix:* on the phone, the three choices become a vertical radio list of three 44px rows, the amount at the right of each; or each segment wraps its amount under its word, with no `nowrap` and a minimum height rather than a fixed one.

- **[medium]** The best-order banner appears in silence, and its refusal can drop focus (`EXPERIENCE.md` 426, 427, 719; Focus 881, 895; 4.1.3, 2.4.3). On desktop the banner appears above the form when Space or Enter chooses a cell in the grid on the left, far from the focus, and the 2026-11-09 rule only posts "the chosen card and its minimum". If the order was served meanwhile, "VENDRE MAINTENANT" is pressed, the banner redraws to the next order or to the no-order line, and the button that had focus may no longer exist. The only Focus row that applies, "Refused inline: the field", has no field here. *Fix:* the choice's status adds "Un ordre d'achat vous attend : <amount>" when the banner shows. A refused sale moves focus to the banner's refusal sentence (`tabindex=-1`), which names the next best order or says there is none. Add both as Focus rows.

- **[medium]** Closing the Archive confirmation after the last copy is gone sends focus nowhere (`EXPERIENCE.md` 435, 716; Focus 878; 2.4.3). "Esc closes it, focus returning to the tile", but the tile "leaves at zero", both after the player's own purchase of the last copy and after "Cette carte n'est plus disponible.". The opener no longer exists, and floor 827 forbids leaving focus on `body` without naming a destination. *Fix:* add a Focus row: "Archive confirmation closed after its tile left: the next tile in the grid or strip, else the previous one, else the section's heading."

- **[medium]** The PASSER DES ORDRES chain skips the only confirmation of each order (`EXPERIENCE.md` 339, 433, Focus 888; 4.1.3, 3.3.4). After "PASSER L'ORDRE", the next missing card's form opens with focus on its h1, without the result panel. A screen-reader user hears another game's name and is never told that the previous order was placed, how much was held, or how many cards remain. *Fix:* the next form opens with a one-line result above its h1, which is the h1's description: "Ordre passé sur Transistor : 12 000 PiCoins bloqués. Carte 2 sur 3." After the last card, the album opens with the same line, announced once.

- **[medium]** "10" may not fit on the small bookmark under text spacing or text resize (`DESIGN.md` 967 `[ASSUMPTION: the two digits of 10 fit the small size]`, 592 to 597; floor 845; 1.4.12, 1.4.4, 1.4.1). The number is the only thing that tells a goal apart where colours merge (see the table above: 2–10 and 3–10 merge for the most common colour-vision deficiencies). JetBrains Mono advances 0.6 em, so at 10px "10" is 12px wide in a 14px bookmark. With the 0.12 em letter-spacing override it is 14.4px, and clipped. With text in rem and the bookmark in px, 200 % text overflows it. The small size is used in chips, the goal button "OBJECTIF 10 · RETIRER", the card detail's OBJECTIFS row and the order form's head. *Fix:* size the small bookmark in em, or widen it to 16px when it holds two digits, and set its number with `letter-spacing: 0 !important`, since a pixel mark is not text spacing users need to change. Check it at the build with the text-spacing bookmarklet and at 200 % text.

- **[medium]** Visitors meet greyed buy actions with no reason (`EXPERIENCE.md` 334 "greyed for a visitor", 410 "with nothing to buy", 434 "ACHETER greyed"; `DESIGN.md` "A disabled button never carries information on its own"; 3.3.2, 1.3.1). The 2026-11-09 review fixed this for the Annonce with "Connectez-vous pour enchérir". The new shop tiles are links that "open the Archive purchase confirmation", and nothing says what a visitor's link opens. *Fix:* a visitor's PASSER UN ORDRE is replaced by "Connectez-vous pour passer un ordre" with the sign-in link. A visitor's shop and Archive tiles open the card's public page, with "ACHETER ▸" replaced on the tile by "prix fixe" alone, and the shop screen carries one line, "Connectez-vous pour acheter aux Archives".

- **[medium]** On the phone, the fourth shop tile is clipped, not removed (`DESIGN.md` 1371, "three on the phone, the row clipped, not scrolled"; 2.4.11, 2.4.7, 1.3.2). A tile cut off by `overflow:hidden` stays in the tab order and in the accessibility tree, so a keyboard user can focus a tile they cannot see, and a screen reader lists four offers while three show. *Fix:* "On the phone the panel renders three tiles; the fourth is not drawn (removed from the DOM or `display:none`), never clipped."

- **[medium]** The market screen gains two panels above its grid and none of them has a heading level (`EXPERIENCE.md` 405, 409, 410; `DESIGN.md` 1371, 1373; 2.4.1, 1.3.1, 2.4.6). From the top there are now up to four wanted tiles and "Tout voir", the chips and actions, then four shop tiles and their link, before "TOUTES LES ANNONCES". The Bourse got a heading rule (row 416); these did not. *Fix:* the market's h1, then h2 "VOS CARTES VOULUES", "BOUTIQUE DE LA CONFRÉRIE" and "TOUTES LES ANNONCES"; each strip of tiles is a list. Give the same rule to the order form's two kickers, "DISPONIBLE MAINTENANT" and "VOTRE ORDRE", so a user can skip a long list of listings and land on "MON PRIX".

- **[medium]** Repeated controls in the new lists have no distinct names (`EXPERIENCE.md` 431, 432; Copy deck 199, 208, 209; 2.4.6, 4.1.2). The 2026-11-09 copy names "Annuler la vente, <card>". MES ORDRES has one "ANNULER" per row with no name given. The order form has one "VOIR L'ANNONCE ▸" per listing and one "ACHETER · <amount>" per Archive edition (two editions may share a price). The MES ORDRES under-row link is also "VOIR L'ANNONCE ▸". *Fix:* add to Copy deck, Market accessible text: "Annuler l'ordre, <card>"; "Voir l'annonce, <rarity> saison <n>, vendue par <name>, <amount> PiCoins"; "Acheter aux Archives, <card> <rarity> saison <n>, <amount> PiCoins"; each name starting with its visible words (2.5.3).

- **[low]** The goal-colour note names the wrong pairs for colour-vision deficiency, and leaves out two colour families (`DESIGN.md` 698, 715). It says "4 and 9, 8 and 10, 1 and 7 sit closer still". Those pairs are close in normal vision. The pairs that merge for protan and deutan vision, about 8 % of men, are 2–10, 3–10 and 5–6, and 4–9 is a perfect greyscale match. The table measures distance from every reserved colour but leaves out the Légendaire's iridescence (`{colors.iridescence-1}` to `-7`, DESIGN 921): goal-7 is ΔE 2.4 from iridescence-4, goal-8 3.2 from iridescence-1, goal-10 5.0 from iridescence-2, goal-6 6.1 from iridescence-6. Goal-9 sits ΔE 13.6 from the alert orange, so a goal-9 bookmark beside an orange badge looks like a second alert. None of this fails 1.4.1, because the number tells the goal, but the note is what testers and builders will check against. *Fix:* rewrite the sentence with the simulated pairs above; add the iridescence to the reserved list, or state that the bookmark's 2px `{colors.outline}` keeps it apart from the art; make the check of "10" above a ship condition, since the number does all the telling.

- **[low]** Some new strings have no spoken form (`EXPERIENCE.md` Copy deck 205, 206, 208, 214, 215; floor 831; 1.3.1). "UN 6E OBJECTIF ?" and "6E OBJECTIF ACHETÉ" (the dialog's title is read first) come out as "six E"; "—" for "Solde après" and "Meilleur ordre en cours" is read as nothing or as "tiret"; "PASSER DES ORDRES · 2", "VOULUES · 3", "VOULUES PAR D'AUTRES · 4", "MISES DE CÔTÉ · 2 CARTES VOULUES" and the radios "À L'ORDRE · <amount>" and "CONFIER · <amount>" leave the dot and the number without context. The duels already solved this with visually hidden text (floor 831). *Fix:* the same treatment: "Un sixième objectif ?", "aucun" for "—", "Passer des ordres, 2 cartes manquantes", "Voulues, 3 cartes", "À l'ordre, <amount> PiCoins".

- **[low]** Three outcomes change state with nothing announced (`EXPERIENCE.md` 431, 720, Focus 883, 891; 4.1.3). A cancelled order moves focus to the next row's title, but "Vos <amount> PiCoins … vous sont rendus" is never read. After a slot is bought, focus lands on the goal button and "<n>E OBJECTIF ACHETÉ" has no stated channel. *Fix:* both post one polite status (the cancel dialog's sentence in the past tense; the slot's result line).

- **[low]** After a slot purchase, focus lands on a button that removes the goal just bought (`EXPERIENCE.md` 340, Focus 883). "OBJECTIF n · RETIRER" acts at once, without confirmation. A key repeat or a double press on "ACHETER" lands on it and removes the new goal. The slot is kept, so the harm is small but confusing. *Fix:* the goal button ignores activation for 400 ms after the dialog closes (the reveal's guard), and "a double activation submits once" is stated for "ACHETER", "VENDRE MAINTENANT" and "VALIDER LES <n> CHOIX".

- **[low]** Buttons at a free limit do not say they open a paid step (`EXPERIENCE.md` 340, 341, 653, 657, 734; 3.2.2 spirit, 3.3.2). "MARQUER COMME OBJECTIF", the new set tile and "+ NOUVEAU DECK" look and read the same at the limit, and the press opens a PiCoin dialog. The dialog's title takes focus, so nothing is bought by surprise, but the player cannot tell beforehand. *Fix:* at a free limit each gets a description, visible on desktop beside the count, "Emplacements gratuits pris : le suivant coûte <price>."

- **[low]** Finality on the wanted table is not tied to its buttons (`EXPERIENCE.md` 424, Copy deck 206; floor 840; 3.3.4). "Une vente à un ordre est immédiate et ne peut pas être annulée." sits under the totals. "TOUT CONFIER QUAND MÊME" entrusts every set-apart copy, Légendaires included, in one press, and Confier's "Les cartes confiées ne vous sont pas rendues." is not repeated here. The 2026-11-09 fix tied finality to Confier's button. *Fix:* "VALIDER" is described by the finality sentence; "TOUT CONFIER QUAND MÊME" by "Les cartes confiées ne vous sont pas rendues."

- **[low]** The Confier recap's status and button ignore the set-apart copies (`EXPERIENCE.md` 422; floor 840; 4.1.3). Ticking a wanted cell sets it apart, so the status "<n> exemplaires, <amount> PiCoins" does not change, and the player hears nothing. With only wanted copies ticked, the button still reads like an entrustment (its label is not drawn). *Fix:* the status adds ", <n> mises de côté"; the button with nothing to entrust reads "CHOISIR POUR LES <n> CARTES VOULUES ▸", without the finality description.

- **[low]** The wanted table step does not state that it inherits the Esc rule and the 560px dock rule (`EXPERIENCE.md` 424, 752, floor 840; 2.1.1, 2.4.11). It is a separate address, while the Esc primitive names "Annonce, Confier and Mettre en vente". With choices and prices filled in, a stray Esc would return to the hub. The dock holds more than Confier's: labelled totals, a refusal line and two buttons. *Fix:* "On the wanted table, choices and typed prices count as a pending edit; its dock pins only from 560 CSS px of viewport height, like Confier's."

- **[low]** The phone's scrolling chip row (`EXPERIENCE.md` 405, `DESIGN.md` 1360 `[ASSUMPTION]`; 2.4.11, 2.1.1). The fade on the right edge can cover a focused chip and its ring. "The chips' group keeping its arrow keys" contradicts the Tabs and filters primitive (268), whose buttons are reached by Tab, and differs from the landing's rule for sideways rows (751). *Fix:* the focused chip scrolls fully into view, clear of the fade; state whether the chips take Tab (as primitive 268 says) or arrow keys, and drop "its arrow keys" if Tab.

- **[low]** The phone result shows the rarities as letters alone (`DESIGN.md` 1379; Copy deck 209; 3.1.4 is AAA, cognitive accessibility). "C PC R É L" is read in full by a screen reader, but a sighted player sees only the letters, and "PC" reads as the platform in a game about Steam. *Fix:* keep the words on the phone under the pills, as on desktop, or use the rarity icons, which already carry shape.

- **[low]** The badge for order notices is undefined (`EXPERIENCE.md` 292, 294, Copy deck 191; 4.1.2). The bell line count is named ("Nouveautés, 3 non vues"), but nothing says whether a served or expired order counts in the ÉCHANGES and MARCHÉ badges, whose names list only trades and auctions. *Fix:* state that order lines count in the bell alone, or add "ordres" to the badge names.

- **[low]** Mock departures a builder may copy (`market-volume-seller-options.html`, `market-volume-results.html`). The segments are `<span>`s in a `role="radiogroup"` without `role="radio"`, at 10px with `nowrap`. Tile tags are drawn as plain spans. *Fix:* add to the mocks' header note that controls and their roles follow the spines, not the markup.

## Checked, no finding

- **Status in words:** the new chips (SERVI, EXPIRÉ, ANNULÉ, CONFIÉE), the VOULUE and ARCHIVES tags, the wanted badge, the reached and not-reached rarity chips (fill plus ✓ against a frame plus "dès") and the order pills (letter on fill) never rely on colour alone. The tags are hidden behind the tile's accessible name, without being read twice.
- **Contrast:** every new text and boundary pair passes (table above); the claims in `DESIGN.md` 667, 709 to 713, 1369 and 1378 are accurate to 0.1.
- **Dialogs:** the Archive and Slot confirmations take focus on their title so the price is read first, close on Esc or ✕, offer the way out when PiCoins are short, and become bottom sheets on the phone; the order cancel dialog reuses the market's.
- **Results:** the order result, the wanted table result (with its partial "n CHOIX SUR n APPLIQUÉS" heading over the daily cap) and the Archive purchase each take focus on their heading, with no second announcement.
- **Order form field:** it reuses My maximum's input rules (text with `inputmode`, empty on arrival, Enter moves to the button, a double activation submits once), the status after 500 ms, and refusals under the field with `aria-invalid`. Each refusal at the press has a focus target.
- **Rows and tiles:** the MES ORDRES title is a stretched link with ANNULER as a sibling; Archive, wanted and shop tiles are single links (the design system's TileComponent); the goal suggestion is a sibling of the goal row's link, never inside it; the See-more links follow the shared rule, with names.
- **Touch:** wanted panel rows, goal suggestion links, segments and "VENDRE MAINTENANT" reach 44px on the phone; the order pills and chips are text, not targets.
- **Live updates:** Vos cartes voulues and the shop are read on load, not live; the bell's new lines follow the market notices; totals post one throttled status.
- **Motion:** nothing new animates. The live-change and reduced-motion rule of floor 840 covers the redraws.
- **Time limits:** an order's 7 days and an auction's duration are chosen, not imposed on a task in progress; leaving the wanted table keeps the cards.
