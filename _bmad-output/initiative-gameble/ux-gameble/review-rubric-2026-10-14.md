# Spine Pair Review — gameble (landing update, 2026-10-14)

Scope: the 2026-10-14 update that adds the visitor landing to `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md`, and how it fits the rest of the pair. Earlier issues that have nothing to do with the landing are not reported again. Reference render: `mockups/landing.html`. Decision log: `.memlog.md`, 2026-10-14 entries.

## Overall verdict

The landing is well integrated in structure. It has a full DESIGN.md Landing section, a matching EXPERIENCE.md Landing component table, a Copy deck block, Motion and reduced-motion rows, Responsive bullets, two new flows, and updated IA rows for sign-out and account deletion. Every `{token}` reference resolves, and the five new tokens are well formed. A consumer still cannot build it cleanly, for three reasons. First, the source spec has no capability for the landing or the roadmap, and CAP-1 still contradicts the new sign-out destination. Second, the live modules have no empty, cold-load or failure states, and the root route for a signed-in player is not committed. Third, the DESIGN Landing section gives about fifteen type sizes as raw px, several of which duplicate existing tokens or contradict them.

## 1. Flow coverage — adequate

Checked the IA's new and changed entries (Landing, the hub's signed-out reach, Public card page links, Roadmap page, Code repository) against Key Flows; Flows 5 and 6 for a named protagonist, numbered steps, a climax and a failure path; and Flow 1's edits.

### Findings
- **medium** No flow walks the landing's main entry. The IA makes the landing the root at /fr and /en, but Flows 5 and 6 both arrive through a shared card and "Découvrir". So no flow covers a visitor who types the address or follows a plain link, or the hero's « Embarquer sur le pont » → signed-out hub → sign-in panel branch (EXPERIENCE.md, Key Flows, Flows 5–6; IA, Landing row). *Fix:* add a short variant to Flow 5, or a Flow 7, for a direct visitor at /fr, including the detour through the signed-out hub and its sign-in panel.
- **medium** Flow 5's climax depends on a component that does not exist: "toasts and her first achievements come quickly" is tagged `[GAP: no achievement-earned toast is specified yet]`, and Open questions confirms it. A consumer cannot build the journey's payoff (EXPERIENCE.md, Flow 5 step 7; Open questions, Achievement toast). *Fix:* specify the achievement-earned toast (Component Patterns row, Copy deck key, DESIGN toast variant), or restate the climax with the components that already exist (set-tier toasts, the Succès counts).
- **low** The middle beats of Flows 5 and 6 rest on four `[ASSUMPTION]`s: who shared the card, which items Camille plays with, which Steam entry she uses, and whether Yanis uses the gauge. The steps read as illustrative rather than committed (Flow 5 steps 1, 4, 5; Flow 6 step 2). *Fix:* confirm them, or rephrase as "for example" so no one reads them as required behavior.

## 2. Token completeness — thin

Parsed the DESIGN.md frontmatter and resolved every `{path.to.token}` in both spines: **all resolve**, including the new `spacing.button-height-lg`, `landing-content-max`, `landing-module-gap(-phone)`, `breakpoint-landing-gauge` and `components.lamp.*`. Then checked the landing prose for raw values and for contrast targets on new combinations.

### Findings
- **high** The landing's type is given as raw sizes, not tokens: VT323 at 26, 22, 24, 36, 28 and 32px, and JetBrains Mono bold at 13, 16, 14, 11 and 10px, across 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09 and the side gauge. Most match existing tokens: `screen-title` 26, `card-title-xl` 22, `screen-title-phone` 24, `banner-small` 36, `showcase-name-phone` 28, `showcase-name` 32, `button` 13/700, `card-title-l` 14/700, `kicker`/`label` 11/700, `kicker-phone` 10/700. A builder will either hard-code them or guess which token was meant (DESIGN.md, Components › Screens › Landing). *Fix:* name the token for each size, or add a small set of landing typography tokens (for example `landing-module-title`, `landing-panel-title`, `landing-figure`) and reference them.
- **medium** The title sizes contradict their tokens. The module title is "44px on a 48px line, the `{typography.hero}` size", but `hero` is 44px on a 44px line, and the 32px phone size has no token. The hero hook is "at the `{typography.public-title}` size (`public-title-phone` on the phone)", while the Layout per-screen table commits "title 42px" at 900–1179px. The same bullet also notes that the mock sets 42px and 38px, so which value wins is unclear (DESIGN.md, Landing › Module header and Hero; Layout & Spacing, per-screen table, Landing row). *Fix:* commit one value per width as a token (for example `public-title-medium: 42px`, a module-title token with its own line height and phone size), and drop the mock commentary once settled.
- **medium** There is no contrast target for text over the lamp field on the phone. The hero text sits on top of the field, the lamp opens the veil (`{components.lamp.veil}`) behind it, and only a "soft `{colors.ground}` text shadow" with no value protects it. The global "measured with every overlay applied" rule cannot be checked while the lamp moves (DESIGN.md, Landing › Lamp field; Layout & Spacing › Landing on the phone). *Fix:* state that the hero text keeps 4.5:1 with the lamp fully open behind it, give the text shadow's value, or keep the lamp's opening off the text block.
- **low** Several values have no token: "faint accent glow", "hard dark drop shadow 18px below", the soft text shadow, the lamp field's ~500px height, 224/184px illustration heights, 46px step squares, 24px roadmap nodes, 216px roadmap columns and the 176px example minimum height. The hero's "capped at 1120px" also restates `{spacing.landing-content-max}` as a number (DESIGN.md, Landing; Layout & Spacing › Landing on the phone). *Fix:* move the reused ones into `components.lamp` or new `landing-*` spacing tokens, and refer to `landing-content-max` by name.

## 3. Component coverage — adequate

Paired DESIGN.md Landing parts with EXPERIENCE.md Landing rows: Lamp field, Hero / Hero action, Moving cards, Module header / Module, Rarity cards (01), Reveal demo (03), Live feed list (04), Goal panels (02, 04), Roadmap (06), Lore panel (02), Open-source module (07), Support panel (08), Final call (09), Side gauge and Landing footer. All have both a visual and a behavioral spec. The verb tiles (02), the 05 tiles and the 07 role panels are static and need no behavior row.

### Findings
- **medium** Nothing says where the sample cards come from. The lamp field cards, the moving cards, the 01 rarity cards and the reveal demo's "next sample" are all shown, and the 01 cards' accessible name must give "title, studio, year, genre, rarity and number". The memlog says "chosen sample cards", but neither spine says who chooses them, from which pool, whether they are real catalog cards of the current season, or how they follow a season change (EXPERIENCE.md, Landing; memlog "Landing content"). *Fix:* add a "Landing sample cards" row: a curated list of real catalog cards, chosen by whom, refreshed when, and what happens if one leaves the catalog.
- **low** The same parts have different names across the two files: DESIGN "Module header" / EXPERIENCE "Module"; DESIGN "Hero" / EXPERIENCE "Hero action"; "Lamp field" / "Lamp field (the lamp hero)"; DESIGN headings "06 · Feuille de route (the roadmap)" and "09 · Embarquement (the final call)" / EXPERIENCE "Roadmap (06)" and "Final call (09)". *Fix:* use one name per part in both files.
- **low** The 01 rarity row and the scrolling roadmap row are each "one tab stop", but no rule says how a keyboard user moves between the cards or milestones inside them. Interaction Primitives › Keyboard lists only the gauge markers for the landing (EXPERIENCE.md, Landing › Rarity cards, Roadmap; Interaction Primitives). *Fix:* add the in-row keys (arrow keys, or "the container scrolls; items are not focusable") to the Keyboard primitive.

## 4. State coverage — thin

Walked the landing as an IA surface: cold load, empty and low data, data failure, signed-in arrival, reduced motion, touch, focus destinations, the lore text missing, and arrival after sign-out or deletion. Reduced motion is thorough, and the lore gate and the sign-out and deletion landings are covered.

### Findings
- **high** The live modules have no empty, low-activity, cold-load or failure states. 04's feed list ("five latest entries") has no rule for zero to four entries. The season facts can be zero (« Premières découvertes cette semaine ») or have no active season. The goal panels in 02 and 04 have no state for a missing goal, and the roadmap has none for missing data. This is parked under Open questions, but the landing is the root for every signed-out visitor and goes live when the community is smallest (EXPERIENCE.md, State Patterns; Open questions › Landing live module). *Fix:* add State Patterns rows for the landing: feed with 0–4 entries, season or goal unavailable, zero counts (French `zero` plural forms), roadmap data missing, cold load (does the generic "—" rule apply?).
- **high** The root route for a signed-in player is not committed. The hub's IA row no longer says it is the root after sign-in. The landing row covers only signed-out visitors, and "/fr and /en open the hub for them" is an `[ASSUMPTION]` in Open questions. Routing is load-bearing for the first build ticket (EXPERIENCE.md, IA, Landing and hub rows; Open questions › Signed-in players and the landing). *Fix:* commit the rule in the IA and in State Patterns (for example "Signed in: /fr and /en open the hub; the landing is not reachable" or "reachable through …").
- **medium** Focus after each step has no landing rows: a side-gauge marker jump (focus to the module's h2?), « Participer à l'écriture » scrolling to 07, the reveal demo after « Révéler » / « Recommencer », and the account-deleted toast arriving on the landing (EXPERIENCE.md, Accessibility Floor › Focus after each step). *Fix:* add these rows.
- **medium** Touch at desktop widths is not covered. Moving cards appear "from 720px" and move by "a pointer drag", but a touch tablet at or above 720px gets no rule: does a touch drag move the card or scroll the page, and can a touch user move a card that covers text? The lamp does handle touch ("Phone and touch: always sweeps") (EXPERIENCE.md, Landing › Moving cards; Responsive › Landing on desktop). *Fix:* turn moving cards off for coarse pointers, or state the touch-drag rule.
- **low** No way to switch language on the landing. A signed-out visitor has no settings, and the landing is both /fr and /en; nothing says how a visitor who arrived on the wrong locale changes it (EXPERIENCE.md, Landing; Accessibility Floor › Language). *Fix:* state whether the landing (or its footer) carries a FR/EN switch, or that the URL segment is the only way.

## 6. Bloat & overspecification — adequate

It quotes about thirty French strings (« MISSION EN COURS », « JEUX RETROUVÉS », « ARCHIVE 001 », « NOUVEAU MEMBRE », « Prochain booster dans », « SEMAINE 41 », « LÉA DONNE », « POSTE OUVERT », « SOUTENIR GAMEBLE »…), sometimes in a different form from the Copy deck: « TROUVÉ » vs "Trouvé", « Réserve 10 / 10 » vs "Réserve : 10 / 10". - **low** The Do's and Don'ts row on money, game figures and technical details repeats EXPERIENCE › Voice and Tone almost word for word. It is a copy rule, not a visual one (DESIGN.md, Do's and Don'ts). *Fix:* keep it in Voice and Tone and point to it.

That is ticket material, not behavior. *Fix:* one line in the Roadmap row: "reads app roadmap data; its sync is a prerequisite ticket".
- **low** The phone landing is spread over three places: the per-screen Layout table, the "Landing on the phone" bullet (the only place with the 216px roadmap column) and the Landing section bullets (DESIGN.md). *Fix:* keep phone variants in the Landing section and point to them from Layout.
