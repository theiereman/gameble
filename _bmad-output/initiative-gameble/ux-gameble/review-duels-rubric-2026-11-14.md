# Spine Pair Review — gameble 1.4 duels

Scope: the release 1.4 additions only (commits `1c796569`, spec, and `3f6e8b09`, UX) and what they touched. Spec: CAP-39 to CAP-43 (spec-gameble.md:310-344), the new constraint, non-goal and open questions (spec-gameble.md:432-434), and `duel-rules.md`. DESIGN.md: `colors.capsule-duel`, the capsule origins paragraph (622), Tab bar (phone) (953), Special capsules (993-1001) and the new "### Duels" (1248-1257). EXPERIENCE.md: the IA rows (44, 50, 59-64), Desktop and Phone navigation (85, 87), the Bell, Count badge and Tab bar rows (269-274), the Copy deck rows "Duels" and "Decks and duel stats" (178-179), "### Duels" (375-391), the Reserved tag (411), Player menu (475), the eight new State Patterns rows (638-645), the Accessibility Floor "Duels" bullet (732), Flow 11 (969-979) and the Duels open question (1017). The navigation mockup ("Navigation des Duels", boards A1 and A2) is an external artifact and was not opened.

## Overall verdict

The additions are lean, their tokens all resolve, and Flow 11 tells a believable first duel, so the pair is a sound sketch of the Duels screen and the friend duel. It is not yet buildable. No source says which deck the challenged friend plays. The spec's "one sitting" and the UX's "resume within 48 hours" disagree. Whether a deck slot is a card, a card-season or an edition is undecided. The release-day state, when decks open but no stats exist, is missing. Several screens (the duel half, the result, the merged Échanges sub-tabs) have rules on one side of the pair only.

## 1. Flow coverage — thin

It covers CAP-40 (deck, defence deck, blocked copy), CAP-41 (a friend duel, automatic half) and CAP-42 (the capsule). CAP-39 appears only as a sort in step 1, the open challenge is absent, and CAP-43 has no beat at all.

## 7. Inheritance discipline — thin

Most player-facing names follow the spec ("capsule de duel", "défi ouvert", "deck de défense", "atout"; the five stats and their directions match `duel-rules.md`). The breaks are in rules, not words, and three of them change what gets built.

### Findings
- **[high]** "One sitting" versus resume. CAP-41 and `duel-rules.md:29` say a player makes their 5 calls "in one sitting", and the challenger "plays their half at once". EXPERIENCE's Duel row says "Leaving keeps the half where it stands; coming back within the 48 hours resumes at the same round" (386). Neither source says what happens to a challenger who leaves mid-half (they have no 48-hour clock), or when the opponent's 48 hours start. *Fix:* the author picks one rule (resume allowed, with a clock for the challenger too, or an abandoned half finishes by automatic play), and both the spec and `duel-rules.md` state it.
- **[high]** What a deck slot is. CAP-40 says "12 distinct cards", `duel-rules.md:23` says "each card-season at most once", stats are ranked "over the season's catalog", and copies carry a minted rarity and a season. The Reserved tag blocks "one copy" without saying which edition (411). Neither the Deck editor nor the Duel profile says which season's stats a game owned in two seasons shows. *Fix:* define the slot unit in `duel-rules.md` (card-season), say which copy it blocks (for example the lowest minted rarity of that season), and make the editor list one entry per card-season and the card detail's DUEL block show the stats of the season being viewed.
- **[medium]** The round shows raw values ("1998 contre 2014"; DESIGN Round outcome, 1257), but a round is decided on percentiles: within the season's catalog, and for Circulation within the rarity tier (`duel-rules.md`, Stats). A Commune held 500 times can beat a Légendaire held 3 times on "LA PLUS INTROUVABLE", so the raw values would show a result that looks wrong. *Fix:* show each card's percentile in words ("plus introuvable que 92 % des Communes"), with the raw value secondary, in the round, the result list and the status line.
- **[medium]** Classement des duels inherits "Le Chattomètre's … rules (… 'Membre discret' …)" (390), and `duel-rules.md:49` says a friends-only player is "Membre discret" to non-friends. The Chattomètre's actual Rows rule says such a player shows "name, avatar and figures with no link" (345), and its Copy deck's "Membre discret" ("an anonymous row") has no rule that uses it. The duel ranking therefore inherits a rule the Chattomètre does not have. *Fix:* the author settles friends-only rows once for both rankings, and the Rows row and `duel-rules.md` follow.
- **[medium]** "AMIS" now names three things: the Échanges sub-tab (which holds trades, not friends), the Amis screen in the player menu (where the bell's "Voir" leads) and the feed's AMIS tab. *Fix:* rename the sub-tab after its content ("ÉCHANGES" / "ENTRE AMIS") or say why the overlap is accepted.
- **[medium]** In English, "Rating" names both the stat "Note" and the duel rating "Cote" (Copy deck, 178-179), and both appear on the same screens (the ranking, the profile, the duel). *Fix:* "Reviews" or "Score" for the stat, or "Duel rating" for the Elo figure.
- **[medium]** Blocked copies on someone else's cards. The Reserved tag names the decks that hold a card (411), and the card picker shows the friend's side too. Naming a friend's decks, or flagging which of their cards are in decks, reveals their defence deck before an open challenge or a friend duel. `duel-rules.md` only says the owner's own trade, sale and entrust screens name the decks. *Fix:* deck names and the "Dans un deck" tag for the owner only; on the friend's side, a blocked copy simply counts as unavailable.
- **[low]** The spec's Open Questions still list "Duel UX: where duels live in navigation, the duel capsule's name and colour, and every duel string" (spec-gameble.md:434). The UX now answers the first two, and the copy is drafted pending review. *Fix:* narrow it to "duel copy pending the author's review".
- **[low]** Component names are not shared, although DESIGN says they are (DESIGN.md:751): DESIGN has "Defence deck", "Deck grid", "Round outcome" and "Échanges sub-tabs"; EXPERIENCE has "Deck list", "Deck editor", "Duel" and "Duel result". *Fix:* use the same names on both sides (for example, DESIGN's "Deck grid" becomes the Deck editor's slots and the "Defence deck" tag sits under "Deck list").
- **[low]** Round numbering differs between screens: the half counts "MANCHE <n> / 5", while the result lists ten alternating rounds, so the same round carries two numbers. *Fix:* the result labels each round "Manche n / 5 · <caller>", or the half shows the duel's round number.
- **[low]** The Player menu row says "the MARCHÉ sub-tab and DUELS included (author, 2026-11-09)" (475), but DUELS dates from 2026-11-14. *Fix:* fix the date.
