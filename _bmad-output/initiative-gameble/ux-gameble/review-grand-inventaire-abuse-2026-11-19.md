# Abuse and privacy review: Le Grand Inventaire

Reviewed 2026-11-19 against EXPERIENCE.md (IA rows 54 to 56, Copy deck row 182, Le Grand Inventaire 356 to 374, Names 395, Delete dialog 525, State Patterns 645 to 653), DESIGN.md (Grand Inventaire), `.memlog.md` 889 to 912, `spec-gameble/spec-gameble.md` (CAP-8, CAP-13 pioneer line, CAP-15, the goals rule), `change-profile-privacy/change-profile-privacy.md`, `spec-gameble/market-economy.md` and `spec-gameble/duel-rules.md`.

## Verdict

The screen reuses rules that already exist: the pioneer's name on a card is public (CAP-13, author 2026-11-09), banned and deleted players read "anonyme", the catalog is public. What is new is the **aggregation**: per-player counts, ranks, a time-ordered log of discoveries, and live copy counts, all open to visitors. Most findings come from that, and the largest from the decision that private players appear by name (memlog 906), which goes against what "private" promises everywhere else.

## Findings

- **[high]** A private player's activity is published even though the feed and presence hide it (EXPERIENCE.md Head pace line 363, Hub panel 373, Pioneer boards · Semaine 365, Grid default order 369; Copy deck 182 "Dernière : {carte}, par {joueur}, il y a 12 min"). The privacy change says a private player shows "no presence or activity", even to friends, and the feed already hides their discoveries (`change-profile-privacy.md` §1; spec CAP-8 Friends tab). The pace line and the hub panel name the private player with "il y a 12 min", which shows they are online right now. The weekly board shows how active they were this week. The grid sorted "latest discoveries first", together with the card detail's discovery date, is a public, dated log of their discoveries. All of this is open to visitors and linked from the landing. *Fix:* for a private player, never fill the latest-discovery line (show the latest discovery by a public or friends-only player, or the card alone, with no name), leave them out of both boards, and keep their found cards in the grid's "found" order but do not name them there either. Their name stays only on the card detail, where CAP-13 puts it.

- **[medium]** A private player is listed by name with a rank and a count in a ranking anyone can page through (Names 366, Classement des pionniers 368; memlog 906). The settings summary promises "Profil privé : vous seul le voyez" (Copy deck 168, State Patterns 652), and Le Chattomètre gives a private profile "no row, not counted, no rank" (354). The paginated ranking also shows, to visitors, that this Steam persona name plays gameble. A private profile otherwise never has a public page, so it could not be found this way before. Saying this is "the game's rule for every name" covers the bare name, not whether the player is listed at all. *Fix:* rank players the way Le Chattomètre does (a private profile has no row and no rank; its discoveries still count in the Brotherhood totals). If the author keeps private players on the boards, the summary in Profil et paramètres and the privacy notice must say that the pioneer name and rank stay public. That needs the author's approval, because it reverses the 2026-11-09 Chattomètre rule.

- **[medium]** Small copy counts reveal a private player's holdings and point buyers at them (Found card 370, `[ASSUMPTION: held today, across editions, x0 shown]`). On a card with "x1", the one copy most likely sits with the named pioneer. A private player's collection is supposed to be hidden from everyone, friends included. Because trades with a private player are closed, buyers who want the card will look for them off-platform through the Steam persona name. *Fix:* hide the badge below a threshold, or show ranges ("x1–4", "x5–19", "x20+"). Or, when the pioneer is private, show the count only from 5 copies up.

- **[medium]** The live, exact copy count is a public map of supply, which makes it easier to corner a card and push its price up (Found card 370). With it, anyone can find the scarce Epics and Legendaries (x2 to x5), see when they hold every copy, and watch entrusting happen in real time (the count drops, then reaches "x0"). The average price is a trimmed mean of the last 5 sales (`market-economy.md`, Average price), so three sales between alt accounts can set the price of a card someone has cornered. The duel stat deliberately counts holders, not copies, recalculated on Mondays (`duel-rules.md` line 20). This badge does the opposite: it counts copies, live, at the exact figure. *Fix:* count holders rather than copies, update it weekly like circulation, and show ranges above a small number. Add "one account bought most of an edition's listings" to the alt-account signals in `market-economy.md`.

- **[medium]** Banned and deleted players keep a ranked "anonyme" row (Names 366, Pioneer boards 365, Classement 368). A player banned for automation keeps their rank and pushes honest players down. When the ban is lifted, the row gets its name back, which shows publicly that it was a ban and not a deletion, although the profile rule hides the difference on purpose (653). A deleted player's "anonyme" row keeps the same count and rank, so anyone who knew them can tell who it was, and the Delete dialog's "what stays" line does not mention a ranking (525). *Fix:* leave banned and deleted accounts out of both boards and the full ranking (no row, no rank, not counted in the ranks). Their cards stay found, with "anonyme" under them, as CAP-13 says.

- **[medium]** Names on the boards can't be reported (Names 366; Report dialog entry points, IA 50 and 51). Names are Steam persona names (spec CAP-8), so a player can pick an offensive one. A private or friends-only name on a board has no link, so a viewer never reaches a SIGNALER button, and the boards sit on a page linked from the landing. Le Chattomètre already has this gap for friends-only rows; this screen extends it to private players and to a page more visitors see. *Fix:* put a report action on a board row (a "⋯" button that opens the Report dialog for that name, signed in only), or apply the private-exclusion fix above.

- **[medium]** Visitors can scrape a full file on every player (Visitor 372, Classement 368, Grid 369). Without an account, a script can page through every ranking (fifty rows a page), read every card's pioneer from the HTML and accessible names ("Outer Wilds, Épique, découverte par Léa"), and the discovery date from each card detail. Together that gives each persona name's discoveries, dates and activity pattern, private players included. *Fix:* rate-limit and cache the visitor version of both pages, show visitors only the top of the full ranking (e.g. the first page), and keep exact times off this screen (the "il y a 12 min" line shows only the card for a visitor).

- **[low]** The boards reward the number of capsules opened, which encourages scripted opening and alt accounts (Pioneer boards 365). The pioneer count grows with the capsules an account opens, so a public status board rewards the "near-maximum capsule opening" that `market-economy.md` already lists as an alt-account signal. Ties go to whoever reached the count first, which also favours automation. Alt accounts can't pass their pioneer status to a main account, but idle accounts that get the free duel capsule (`review-duels-abuse-2026-11-14.md`) can fill the board with throwaway names. *Fix:* hide from the boards any account that staff flagged on an alt-account signal until staff review it, and ask staff to watch weekly board leaders as one more signal.

- **[low]** Top pioneers become targets (Pioneer boards 365, Hub panel 373). A public top 3 on a visitor-reachable page, with profile links for public players, invites friend-invite spam and pressure to trade. Usually the target is the pioneer of a card that has very few copies. *Fix:* rate-limit friend invitations per sender if they aren't already; the copy-count range fix above takes away the "only copy" lure.

- **[low]** Cached pages may lag behind a visibility change or a ban (Head 363 "Read on load"; Visitor 372). Pages this large (the season's whole grid) will probably be cached for visitors. A player who goes private, or a player who is banned, must disappear from them at once (architecture: "a ban that takes effect late; a private profile shown"). *Fix:* expire every cache on the screen and the full ranking when a player's visibility changes, on a ban, on an unban and on a deletion, or don't cache names at all.

## Checked, no finding

- **Undiscovered card faces spoil nothing.** The whole catalog is public: a visitor sees every card's face, plain, in Toutes les cartes and on the card detail (EXPERIENCE.md 40, 95, 334). The "À découvrir" list can't be used to aim at a card either: draws pick a card uniformly within a rarity tier (`catalog-and-rarity.md` line 44), and goals never change draw odds (spec, goals rule).
- **Friends-only players named without a link** match Le Chattomètre's rows (354) and the shared Names rule (395).
- **The pioneer name on found cards and in the Set de la Confrérie** matches CAP-13 for players who are not private.
