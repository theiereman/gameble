# gameble — Experience Spine

## Foundation

A responsive web game for desktop and phone (portrait only), in French and English, also carried by iOS and Android apps that wrap its screens (see Responsive & Platform, Native apps). Every flow works on both form factors; the layouts differ: a hub with a numbered menu on desktop, a bottom tab bar on the phone. This document names no UI component library: the stack renders pages on the server and embeds interactive components for the capsule opening and the trade board (see the spec's architecture and `ux-gameble.md`, For the architecture). `DESIGN.md` is the visual identity reference; this document is the experience. Tokens are referenced by name, for example `{colors.accent}`; component names are the same in both documents.

Visual references: [mockups/key-screens.html](mockups/key-screens.html) (desktop prototype: every screen, the chosen "Explosion A" opening on the capsule screen, the signed-out public card page), [mockups/mobile.html](mockups/mobile.html) (phone prototype), [mockups/key-roadmap-page.html](mockups/key-roadmap-page.html) (the roadmap page), [mockups/key-card-detail-pioneer.html](mockups/key-card-detail-pioneer.html) (the card detail's pioneer row) and [mockups/landing.html](mockups/landing.html) (the visitor landing, desktop and phone, with its motion and reduced-motion form; its REVUE toolbar and note pins are a review aid, not part of the design). Goals, custom sets and the Steam library set (author, 2026-10-30): [mockups/collection-goals-and-custom-sets.html](mockups/collection-goals-and-custom-sets.html) (the Collection entry, approved), [mockups/custom-set-editor.html](mockups/custom-set-editor.html) (option B, chosen; option A is rejected), [mockups/goal-markers.html](mockups/goal-markers.html) (album button, grid and OBJECTIFS filter, capsule, card detail; its « ENTRE DANS VOTRE OBJECTIF » line is dropped) and [mockups/goals-palettes.html](mockups/goals-palettes.html) (palette A chosen). Trade suggestions, the Personnalisation page and four achievement backs (author, 2026-10-31): [mockups/trade-suggestions.html](mockups/trade-suggestions.html) (variant 2 of the suggestion row retained; variant 1 is kept for the record; its blue outline around a focused row and its `href="#"` openers are not to be copied), [mockups/personalization.html](mockups/personalization.html) (the chosen page, variant A; the focus ring it draws on a tapped back is dropped, and its at-rest strip, exposed to assistive technology there, is hidden as Accessibility Floor, Back picker says) and [mockups/achievement-backs.html](mockups/achievement-backs.html) (chosen: Luck Is a Marketing Concept A, Leave Request Denied A, Market Concentration C, A Strictly Professional Relationship B; the other candidates are rejected, and so is every name or word the mock shows that the spines do not). DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md take precedence over any mock when they conflict. The mocks are a reference for look and timing, never source code. Their sample data (a 16 480-card season, three-hour timers, demo odds, a scripted first capsule) is illustrative; the spec's numbers rule.

In this document, "capsule" is the player-facing word for the spec's "pack", and "réserve" for its "bank". In the lore (`../spec-gameble/lore.md`), each card is an archive card in the Brotherhood's proprietary format and this space-terminal interface is its authorized archive card reader; the interface still says "carte". Items tagged `[ASSUMPTION]` are not confirmed in the decisions log. Open questions close the file.

## Voice and Tone

This section covers microcopy only. Brand voice lives in `DESIGN.md` (see Brand & Style). All copy ships in French and English; the French below is the product's approved copy.

- **Terminal labels:** short uppercase labels for actions and states ("OUVRIR LA CAPSULE", "TOUT RÉVÉLER", "◀ COLLECTION", "SCELLÉE", "RÉSERVE VIDE").
- **Plain sentences:** short and factual, always addressing the player as "vous", never "tu".
- **The Brotherhood speaks as an institution:** a corporate, falsely benevolent voice, a satire of capitalism and corporate culture, serious and never joking on purpose. Its presence is secondary: light in the collection, capsules, navigation, system messages and hub; stronger possible in communications from Management, events, achievements and economic announcements. Functional copy stays plain, and each new Brotherhood line in the interface comes through its own issue. The player gets no title or form of address. The landing is the one approved exception (author, 2026-10-14): it presents the Brotherhood, its mission and its story to come at length; inside the app the rule stands.
- **The landing speaks in the app's voice** (author, 2026-10-14; no landing-only exception): short and factual, few or no exclamation marks, always "vous". It never hammers on money ("0 €", "pas d'argent", "pas de dépense"), never sells with game figures (the starting reserve, cards per daily capsule, a price of 0), and never cites technical details ("chaque image est pixelisée puis rendue"). The author kept two figures because they explain how an opening works: the final call's "Dix capsules vous attendent." and 03's five cards per capsule. It promises nothing the game does not have when the landing ships: what comes later carries "Bientôt" or sits in the roadmap.
- **The name** is written "gameble", in lower case everywhere, at the start of a sentence too; capitals come only from style (a kicker, the wordmark).
- **French punctuation:** a narrow no-break space (U+202F) before « : ; ? ! » and inside « », so no sign wraps onto a line of its own.
- **Errors say what happened and what to do,** inline where the action is.
- **Market words (author, 2026-11-09):** see `../spec-gameble/market-economy.md`, Words. "Mon maximum", never "automatique". "Confier" and "cartes confiées", never "vendre à la Confrérie" or "détruire". Amounts carry the PiCoin mark, never a euro sign. Exact (31 500) on the Annonce, forms, dialogs, results, bell lines, feed entries and a row's "Mon maximum"; compact (31,5k, 7,2M) from 1 000 up on listing tiles, a row's price, the Balance chip and the price under a card, always truncated, never rounded up, so a player never reads more than they hold. One shared component draws every amount: compact text visible, the exact amount and "PiCoins" as its accessible text, in the page's language (31.5k in English), with U+00A0 as the separator. Money fields accept digits and spaces only. Values such as 10 %, 3 minutes and the durations are read from the settings, never written as constants.
- **"Objectif" names only the player's goals** (author, 2026-10-30): the community weekly goal is the weekly mission, "MISSION HEBDO", in every player-facing text, French and English; code names (the `capsule-goal` token) stay.

## Opening protocol

1. Activating the canister sends the opening request and locks the trigger: a second activation does nothing, and each request carries an idempotency key.
2. The canister charges ("ARMEMENT", about 300ms of squash) while the answer arrives; this masks the wait.
3. **Success:** LEDs and hazard band turn orange, the caps blow off and the five cards are dealt.
4. **Refusal** (no capsule in reserve, or any error): the canister disarms without blowing its caps, the status returns to "SCELLÉE" or "EN ATTENTE", and the reason appears inline under the open button and in a toast. Nothing opens.
5. The art of each card starts loading on the answer; a card never flips before its art is ready or known to be missing.
6. During the opening and each reveal, in-app back controls, the tab bar and Esc navigation are disabled. Browser Back is allowed and loses nothing: the five cards are already in the collection.

## Interaction Primitives

- **One activation reveals:** click, tap, Enter or Space on a face-down card reveals rarity and game together. One surprise per card.
- **Guard:** a just-revealed card ignores activation for 400ms, so a double activation never skips the reveal or leaves the screen.
- **Tilt:** cards lean toward the pointer, up to about 10° (16° on the capsule screen); on the phone only under a finger held still for 150ms. The card eases toward the pointer as it enters (about 600ms to settle) and back to flat as it leaves (about 650ms); the varnish's reflection rides that eased tilt in and out, sliding opposite it, so nothing jumps and nothing fades. Cards in scrolling content keep vertical panning (`touch-action: pan-y`); in a row that scrolls sideways (the landing's 01 and 06 on the phone) cards do not tilt and keep both pannings (`pan-x pan-y`); only the capsule stage captures every gesture.
- **Hover is a lift; touch is a press.**
- **Drag (landing only):** with a fine pointer, a moving card follows a pointer drag and stays where it is dropped. Nothing else is dragged, and nothing needs a drag.
- **Swipe, always with a button:** on the phone a revealed card swipes into the tray ("RANGER" does the same); in the card detail, swipe or the ◀ ▶ buttons move between cards. Long-press is never required.
- **Keyboard:** Tab order follows reading order. Keys 1 to 8 open menu items only while focus is in the hub menu. On the landing, the side gauge's markers are ordinary links, after the page content in the tab order; a sideways-scrolling row (01 and 06 on the phone) is one tab stop only while it overflows, the arrow keys scroll it, and its items are not focusable. Arrow keys move focus within the collection grid (Up and Down move one row, whatever the column count), the card picker grid, and radio and segmented groups; in a stepper they change the quantity. Enter submits the friend code.
- **Esc:** closes the topmost overlay (dialog, sheet, zoom, legend, toast) and nothing else. With no overlay open, it cancels a counter-proposal edit after confirmation. Otherwise, when no edit is pending and no text field has focus, it returns to the hub (except during an opening). A typed maximum, ticked cards or a filled sale form count as a pending edit: on Annonce, Confier and Mettre en vente, Esc only closes the topmost overlay.
- **Not in the MVP:** bulk opening, live trades, card marks, a guide (a tour of what to do; the rules reference "Comment ça marche" is not one, author 2026-10-13), and any input at opening that improves the odds (noted for later).

**Reduced motion.** Applies when "ANIMATIONS RÉDUITES" is OUI in Profil et paramètres, or when it is SYSTÈME (the default) and the system asks for it; NON plays the full motion even when the system asks for less. For a visitor without an account, it applies when the landing's motion switch is on (its cookie), otherwise when the system asks for it; the phone's landing has no switch, so there a cookie set earlier on a desktop still applies and otherwise the system decides. The rule: nothing translates, rotates, scales, shakes, flashes, blinks, sweeps or loops; state changes are 150 to 220ms crossfades; the ticker stops and shows its latest items still; the LIVE marker stays lit; the star field, rays, the Légendaire's glitter twinkle, sheen, canister breathing and idle floats stop; flashes are removed (not dimmed); tilt is off. Exceptions, spelled out:

- **Opening:** the canister fades out in 200ms and the cards fade in (240ms, 40ms apart).
- **Reveal:** each card crossfades to its face in 220ms; a Légendaire shows a still "LÉGENDAIRE !" banner for 1.4s instead of its sequence.
- **Trade swap:** the two sides crossfade to their new contents.
- **Back picker:** a hovered back does not lift; the rest is still at full motion too (no shake, the condition in the strip).
- **Set celebration:** no entrance: each result crossfades in over 200ms already in its final state (gauge at the new count, tick lit, ribbon in place) and stays still; no gloss, glint, spark or glow. The COMPLET stamp keeps its fixed −6° tilt (a static tilt is not motion) and never drops, bounces or rotates into place.
- **Screen changes, sheets, sub-views, toasts, feed entries:** instant or crossfaded, no slide.
- **App start:** no brightening.
- **Landing:** no moving cards; the lamp does not travel: while the pointer is over the field it jumps to the pointer and finds the card there, otherwise it rests on the first card, with the first two cards already found; modules fade in over 180ms without rising, under the same trigger as the full power-on, and their brackets, gauges and pips appear at once; the reveal demo crossfades in 220ms; new feed entries appear without sliding; the star field stays still; the 03 countdown stops (as in the mock).
- **Card reflection:** every card face keeps its studio reflection, still, at its rest position (the flat card's); a Légendaire's glitter stays as still, faint flakes.

## Accessibility Floor

Behavior only. Contrast values live in `DESIGN.md` (see Colors).

- **Keyboard:** every action works with the keyboard; focus order follows reading order; the focus ring (`{components.focus-ring.css}`) shows on every focusable element, distinct from hover; scroll padding keeps it clear of sticky bars, the back picker's strip included, at its current height, while it sticks. Focus destinations: see Focus after each step. A list update that removes the focused element (a sent suggestion, a Turbo Stream that replaces a list) moves the focus as that table says, never leaving it on `body`; suggestions worked out again never remove the focused row under the player: the update waits until the focus leaves it, or moves the focus the same way.
- **Screens and dialogs:** inactive screens are inert and hidden from assistive technology; each screen has one h1, a main region and a navigation region; dialogs are named by their title, trap focus and return it on close.
- **Capsule live region:** a dedicated polite region announces each reveal (Copy deck, Capsule live region). "Tout révéler" posts one summary of the cards it revealed. A Légendaire posts its signal at the start and the result on landing. Opening refusals are announced there too. After the last reveal message (or the Tout révéler summary, or a Légendaire's result), the set results are posted once, as one message that lists every result in the order the banners show them, including when the banner folds them into "n PALIERS ATTEINTS". The region appends each message as a new node and never replaces one posted less than 2s earlier. The reveal caption plate and the banners are not live regions.
- **Feed and ticker:** the feed list is not a live region; while the feed is not focused, a status line announces new entries in batches at most every 30s; with "ANIMATIONS RÉDUITES" on, these announcements stop. The ticker is hidden from assistive technology.
- **Toasts** are polite status messages; errors also stay inline until the next action.
- **Goals** are never carried by colour alone (author, 2026-10-30): the bookmark always prints the goal's number, and the set's name is in the visible text or the accessible name, "objectif n : <set>". The bookmarks are hidden from assistive technology; a card's accessible name ends with its goals, a goal row and a goal set's tile carry the goal in their names, the goal button's name gives the number and the set, and each OBJECTIFS filter chip is named "Objectif n : <set>" (Copy deck, Goals).
- **Trade suggestions:** each suggestion row holds one opener, a link to the suggestion's draft on the trade board, its address made of tokens, never database ids: opening writes nothing and shows the prefilled draft, so the opener is never a form button. The opener is a sibling of "ENVOYER", stretched over the row by CSS, never an element that contains it; "ENVOYER" is a separate button with its own name, after it in keyboard order `[ASSUMPTION]`. The opener's name is the Copy deck's row name followed by the presence (Copy deck, Trade suggestions). The visible name, presence and summary lines, which that name repeats, are hidden from assistive technology, as are the goal bookmarks, the "×n" counts and the avatar, so each word of the row is read once; "ENVOYER" stays exposed. The opened row carries `aria-current="true"`, as the set progress row does. The "PROPOSITION TRANSMISE" toast is a polite status message like every toast.
- **Back picker:** each back is a button whose name holds everything the strip shows for it, in the strip's order: its name, its state, the full sentence of how it was earned or can be earned, then the date or the set progress (Copy deck, Back picker, accessible names). The strip shows nothing the name lacks, so it stays hidden from assistive technology, at rest too. The strip's content comes on keyboard focus and on a tap as well as on pointer hover, never on hover alone. Equipping happens on `click`, never on `pointerdown` or `touchstart`, so a scroll that starts on a back cancels it. Equipping is announced by a hidden polite status message, never a toast: the status region is in the page from load, empty, and each announcement changes its text only, cleared first so a repeat is announced again; a season button announces the same message with the season (Copy deck, Back picker, equipping). That region is the only announcement: no `aria-live` on the mosaic or the strip. The focus ring follows `:focus-visible` alone: it shows on keyboard focus and not after a click or a tap, where the active back's lime frame is the only mark (author, 2026-10-31); the focus stays on the back (no `blur()`, no `outline:none` on `:focus`), and the ring comes back with the next key press. Counts written with a slash ("2 / 3", "14 / 55 débloqués") are read through visually hidden text, "2 sur 3" ("2 of 3"). The season buttons under a back are a group named "Marque de saison" `[ASSUMPTION]`.
- **Rarity** is never carried by color alone: shape and name accompany it; where only the icon fits (rarity counter, tray, thumbnails), the name is in the accessible name and in the "RARETÉS" legend.
- **Card text order:** title, studio (the developer), year, genre, rarity, number, then state (copies, NOUVEAU, reserved count). A card's accessible name always holds the full title, the studio, the year and the number, whatever its face shows or cuts with an ellipsis. Cover canvases, thumbnails, rays and particles are hidden from assistive technology; avatars have empty alt text next to a visible name; rarity icons are hidden when the name is beside them. In a signed-in player's Toutes les cartes, another player's full collection and a set album, a card not owned adds "non possédée" ("not owned") to the end of its accessible name; on the card detail its copies row says so. The lock is hidden from assistive technology.
- **Set celebration:** each result is a link whose accessible name repeats its live message and ends with its hint, "Palier adepte atteint : Roguelike, 16 cartes sur 62. Voir le set."; the folded line's ends with "Voir les sets en cours." (Copy deck, Set celebration). Inside the link, the ribbon's marks, the gauge and its ticks, the stamp frame, the sparks and the link's arrow are hidden from assistive technology: the name carries the tier and the count. The phone's "▸" is a button named "Résultat suivant". The focus ring goes around the whole result, inside the plate on the phone.
- **Gauges** are progress bars with a text value, except the set celebration's, decorative inside its link, and the Chattomètre's, an image named in words. Countdowns are not live; their accessible text changes at most once a minute.
- **Market:** one polite status region per screen, empty at load, carrying the latest state of Copy deck, Market accessible text at most every 10s, so the last update is never dropped; an extension and the auction's end bypass the 10s; it never moves focus. An open dialog holds its own status line. Only the live part of the sentence under the maximum is in the region, after 500ms without typing, and an unchanged text is not posted; ticking cards and the sale form's totals post one short status the same way. Results: see Focus after each step, with no second announcement when focus moves. Status chips and the "En tête" marks are words, never colour alone. The time left is not live; its accessible text gives the end time in a `<time>` element, and a bidder's status line posts once at the last hour and once at 3 minutes left. A name with no link is plain text, not a disabled link. The Confier and Mettre en vente grids are one tab stop with arrow keys inside, each cell's name holding its edition, state and value. The Confier and sale bars pin only from 560 CSS px of viewport height. Live changes do not animate beyond a colour change of under a second, and not at all with "ANIMATIONS RÉDUITES", which also stops the status region's posts about other players' bids (author, 2026-11-09), never the result of the player's own bid or the auction's end.
- **No information only in a tooltip** (`title`).
- **Moving content:** the ticker pauses on hover and stops with "ANIMATIONS RÉDUITES"; every looping or blinking effect stops with it; nothing blinks faster than once a second. A visitor without an account stops them with the landing's motion switch on a desktop, and through the system setting on a phone (see Landing, Motion switch).

## Key Flows

### Flow 1 — First session (Léa, on her PC, clicks a link Hugo posted on Discord)

1. Léa clicks Hugo's link. Discord's preview showed the card's title and rarity, no image.
2. Signed out, she lands on the public card page: Hollow Knight large and tilting under her pointer, its publisher, "LÉGENDAIRE", genre, year, number, the short description and "VOIR SUR STEAM ↗". The wordmark leads to the landing; she does not follow it (Flows 5 and 6 do).
3. Below, the LIVE strip shows pulls and trades with their rarities.
4. She presses "OUVRIR MES PREMIÈRES CAPSULES", the terms line beside it: Steam's sign-in opens at once and she signs in.
5. She lands on the Pont de commandement. The capsules chip reads 10 CAPSULES with "Réserve pleine"; menu 01 carries a lime "10"; the empty showcase invites her to open her first capsule.
6. She opens the capsule screen and activates the canister: it charges, the caps blow off, five cards land face down wearing the Standard card back.
7. She reveals one: a Commune, announced as she sees it. She reveals the next, then presses "TOUT RÉVÉLER" for the rest.
8. "OUVRIR LA SUIVANTE". Capsule after capsule, the rarity counter ticks up.
9. **Climax:** before her tenth capsule ends, a card trembles, rings and bursts in blue light: a Rare or better, the first prize her collection holds.
10. The reserve runs out and the capsule screen shows when the next one arrives. She goes back to the Pont, opens 02 and browses her sets: owned cards with "NOUVEAU", missing ones naming the game and its year.

## Implementation notes

Build rules found while prototyping and reviewing; they bind the build. Architecture-level items are listed in `ux-gameble.md` (see For the architecture).

- **Mocks are never source code.** Port the look and the timings, not the scripts. Listeners and timers are registered when a component connects and removed when it disconnects; caches are bounded.
- **Tokens are CSS values.** Use the exact values in `DESIGN.md` frontmatter (pixel outlines, borders, bevels, scanlines) as custom properties.
- **3D card stack:** never put 3D transforms on a `<button>` that holds card faces, and never put a grouping property (opacity below 1, filter, overflow, clip-path, mask, mix-blend-mode, isolation) on or between the tilt and flip layers: either one flattens the 3D and shows the mirrored back. Use plain layers (tilt, then flip), both faces with `backface-visibility: hidden`, and a flat transparent button on top for focus and activation. One reveal path for a single activation and for "Tout révéler". A visual regression test covers a tilted, revealed card.
- **Hide every descendant of an inactive screen,** not only the screen: a child made visible on purpose (a dealt card) otherwise shows through.
- **Content timing:** card content reaches the browser when the capsule is opened; the reveal is only an animation; counters update at each reveal so the interface never spoils a card.
- **Dialogs and Esc:** every modal and bottom sheet is a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` by a Stimulus controller; the hotkeys controller ignores events already handled, events while a dialog is open and events from form fields, so one Esc never both closes a dialog and navigates. Single-key shortcuts can be turned off.
- **No inline style:** server HTML carries no `style` attribute (the CSP blocks it); progress bars and gauges get their values as CSS custom properties set by a controller.
- **Large grids:** a collection grid beyond about 60 slots is paginated or virtualized, and only visible cards are filtered.
- **Fonts:** the app moves from Space Mono to JetBrains Mono in its own ticket (see Landing ship conditions). The three latin font files (JetBrains Mono 400 and 700, VT323) are preloaded with `font-display: block` and a short block period, so a fallback swap never reflows two-line card titles.
- **Daily capsule arc:** runs from `turbo:before-stream-render` (both positions known before the DOM is replaced) and dispatches `gameble:bank-changed`.
- **Landing ship conditions:** the landing goes live (the root switch, the landing's last story) only once all of these hold: the lore text exists in French and English; the pioneer feature is built, so the « Découvrez » tile tells the truth; the repository is public with a license file, so 07's and the footer's open-source claims hold; the roadmap (in the database, curated in the back-office) and the switch to JetBrains Mono are built first (author, 2026-10-14, triage).
- **Landing address:** the landing lives at the root only, /fr and /en, for a signed-out visitor; a signed-in player gets the hub at the same address. There is no /landing, /home or other address of its own (author, 2026-10-21): one indexable address per language, with `hreflang` between the two. The provisional /:locale/landing the tracer story serves is removed at the root switch and redirects permanently to the root for as long as a link to it could exist.
- **Landing roadmap data:** the roadmap the landing and the roadmap page show lives in the database: one milestone per initiative epic is created on deploy and never edited or deleted by that pass, and staff curate wording in both languages, visibility, status, order and stories in the back-office (author, 2026-10-19). Its build is its own ticket (see Landing ship conditions).
- **Landing data load:** the landing is the most visited page. Each data block (feed, season facts, weekly mission, sample cards, roadmap) is cached per locale with a short cache lifetime, and signed-out GETs of the root are throttled like the other public pages. Like them, the landing also serves link previews and older browsers. The feed list subscribes to its updates only once module 04 comes into view.
- **Landing motion cost:** the lamp's veil is one oversized element with a fixed hole, moved by `translate3d` (never a mask repainted each frame); its frame loop runs only while the lamp runs (see Component Patterns, Landing, Lamp field). The field's bottom fade is a ground-colored gradient laid over it, not a mask. Lamp, moving-card, gauge and power-on maths live in pure modules with unit tests.
- **Landing without inline style or script:** modules are visible by default and the power-on controller arms only those not yet in view when it connects; the hero entrance is pure CSS; per-element values (lamp slots, rotations, staggers) come from CSS classes or are set through the CSSOM by a controller; fonts are self-hosted subsetted woff2, never `data:` URIs. The reveal demo is a Stimulus controller over server-rendered card faces, not the booster screen's controllers, and its announcements are rendered by the server.
- **Landing covers:** a small set of games is reused across the lamp, 01, the moving cards and the examples, so cached covers and filtered bitmaps are reused; covers draw lazily and modules use `content-visibility: auto`. Their cost is measured on the reference low-end phone (load, frame time while the lamp sweeps, bytes).
- **Landing mock deviations (do not copy from `mockups/landing.html`):**
  - its 34px « Révéler » on the phone (`{spacing.button-height-sm-phone}` applies);
  - its footer links wrapping with a 4px gap (`{spacing.touch-gap}` applies), and its 24px-tall wordmark link on the phone;
  - its `nowrap` example rows that clip at 320px, and its example feed rows « Inès complète Dark Souls » and « Nina a reposté Hades » without a period (the Feed copy applies);
  - its side gauge marker names without the visible number, which break WCAG label-in-name (see Copy deck, Landing, Side gauge), and its marker numbers in `{colors.dim}`: they are the markers' only visible text, so they use `{colors.muted}`;
  - its rotated 11px text in the COMPLET stamp of 05 (rotated text only in VT323 at 24px or more, see `DESIGN.md`, Collection, COMPLET stamp);
  - its mission end « Fin lundi à 00:00 UTC » (the visitor's own time zone applies, never "UTC"), and its second weekly mission (« Tous les joueurs ») in 04 (the mission is live only in 02);
  - its module power-on triggered by a share of the module's area (the fixed 85% line applies, see Component Patterns, Landing, Module), its feed list re-rendered every 8s, and its mask-repainting lamp;
  - its « TERMINAL EN LIGNE » status in the top bar: no decision settles it `[ASSUMPTION: dropped]` (see Open questions, Landing top bar).
- **Daily bonus capsule:** it holds 3 cards, so the revealed counter and hints count to 3 for it ("Cartes révélées : n / 3").
