# Accessibility review — gameble 1.4 duels

Scope: the release 1.4 additions of commits `1c796569` (spec: CAP-39 to CAP-43, `spec-gameble/duel-rules.md`) and `3f6e8b09` (UX): decks, weekly card stats, the asynchronous duel half, the duel result and ranking, the steel duel capsule and defence tag, and the phone tab bar change (MARCHÉ becomes a sub-tab of ÉCHANGES, DUELS takes the fifth tab). Read against the Accessibility Floor of `EXPERIENCE.md` and the earlier reviews (`review-charts-accessibility-2026-11-10.md`, the market review). Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Line numbers are those of the working copy on 2026-11-14. The duel half, the deck editor and the duel result have no mock yet, so most findings are gaps in the spines to close before the mock, not defects in a drawing.

## Overall verdict

**Not ready to mock or build as written; ready once the high findings are written in.** The visual layer is sound: every new text and mark clears its ratio by a wide margin (steel 11.5:1 to 14.7:1 on the dark grounds, lime 8.3:1 to 10.6:1, muted 5.1:1 to 6.6:1, outline on steel 15.3:1), outcomes are words, the change arrows are uncoloured shapes, and the defence deck is a written tag. The Floor's new Duels bullet (`EXPERIENCE.md`:732) is a good start: buttons for the hand, directions named in words, a polite outcome line. What is missing is the duel half's interaction model for anyone not using a mouse and eyes: the Focus after each step table has no duel row at all, the stat choice has no semantics, the irreversible call can be fired by a double Enter or a tap landing where the direction buttons appear, and moving focus to "SUIVANTE" the instant the outcome posts will often silence that outcome on VoiceOver. The navigation change is sound in its layout but the AMIS / MARCHÉ control inherits the filter chips' `aria-pressed` semantics while the two parts are separate addresses, and none of the new or summed badges has an accessible name in the Copy deck. Timing is acceptable (no timer within a half; the 48 h window is beyond WCAG 2.2.1's 20-hour exception), but the spec's "one sitting" contradicts the UX's resume rule and should be settled in favour of resuming.

## What already holds

- Outcomes are words ("Gagnée", "Perdue", "Nulle"; "Victoire", "Défaite", "Égalité"), with lime and muted as reinforcement only.
- Week-to-week arrows are text arrows in `text`, never green or red; the direction is the glyph's shape.
- The defence deck is a written "DÉFENSE" tag, the draft a written "<n> / 12".
- Directions are two buttons named in words, never an arrow alone; the hand is a group of buttons.
- No per-call timer and no time pressure inside a half; leaving keeps the half and resuming returns to the same round (`EXPERIENCE.md`:386). The 48 h window is above WCAG 2.2.1's 20-hour exception.
- Countdowns follow the Floor's once-a-minute rule; the Monday feed entry stays out of the ticker.
- Deleting a deck and "RETIRER DES DECKS" both ask a confirmation.
- The sub-tabs inherit a 44px phone hit area from the Tabs and filters rules; the Duels screen's rows are 44px.

## Findings

### Critical

None.

### High

- **[high]** H1. The Focus after each step table has no duel, deck or sub-tab row (`EXPERIENCE.md`:752–795). The duel half replaces parts of the screen five times (stats appear, directions appear, the opponent's card turns, the hand redraws), the deck editor fills and empties slots, and the half ends on a different screen. Without destinations the build will leave focus on a removed node or on `body`, which the Floor itself forbids (`EXPERIENCE.md`:728). (2.4.3, 2.4.11.) *Fix:* add rows: "Duel opened or resumed → the round heading 'MANCHE n / 5'"; "Card chosen → stays on the card (`aria-pressed="true"`), the stats group follows it in the DOM"; "Stat chosen → stays on the stat (`aria-expanded="true"`), the directions group follows it"; "Call sent → the outcome heading (see H3)"; "SUIVANTE → the card just drawn in the hand"; "Fifth call's outcome → 'VOIR LE DUEL' / the waiting panel's heading 'En attente de <name>'"; "Lancer un duel opened → the sheet's title; COMMENCER → the duel's round heading"; "Card added or removed in the deck editor → stays on that collection card"; "Deck full refusal → stays on the card, refusal announced"; "Deck deleted → the 'MES DECKS' heading"; "Sub-tab AMIS or MARCHÉ followed → the screen's h1".

- **[high]** H2. The irreversible call can be fired by accident (`EXPERIENCE.md`:386, "the call is sent at once, with no confirmation"). Three routes: a keyboard user who presses Enter on a stat and then Enter again (or holds it, since buttons fire on a repeating keydown) lands on a direction if focus moves there; on the phone the direction buttons appear right after a tap on a stat, often under the same finger, and a second tap or a ghost click commits; the two directions are adjacent, so a slip picks the losing side of a stat. The author's choice of no confirmation can stand, but the house already guards a comparable irreversible tap (Interaction Primitives, Guard, `EXPERIENCE.md`:650). (2.5.2, 3.3.4 in spirit; cognitive and motor users.) *Fix:* state in the Duel row and the Floor: (1) the card and the stat stay choosable again until a direction is pressed, so only the direction commits; (2) focus never moves onto a direction button by itself (H1); (3) direction buttons ignore activation for 400ms after they appear, as the reveal Guard; (4) they commit on `click`, never on `pointerdown`/`touchstart`; (5) on the phone they appear below the stat list, never in the place of the stat just tapped, stacked full width, each at least 44px tall and 16px apart; (6) each direction's name carries the stat and the card's value so the choice is read before it is made (H4).

- **[high]** H3. The outcome announcement races the focus move and says too little (`EXPERIENCE.md`:732). Posting to a polite status line and moving focus to "SUIVANTE" in the same tick makes VoiceOver on iOS and often NVDA read the button and drop or truncate the queued message, so the round's result, the core of the game, may never be heard. The example message also omits the opponent's card and the score. (4.1.3, 1.3.1.) *Fix:* on the call, render the outcome as a heading inside the round panel ("Manche 2 gagnée") and move focus to it (`tabindex="-1"`); the panel's text, read in order, is "Votre carte : <game>, <value>. Carte de <name> : <game>, <value>. Sortie, la plus ancienne. Score : 2 – 0." with "SUIVANTE" next in Tab order. Drop the separate status line for this step (the Market rule: no second announcement when focus moves). Post the outcome only once the opponent's card is face up (M7). Write the sentence in both languages in the Copy deck.

- **[high]** H4. The duel half's controls have no stated semantics beyond the hand (`EXPERIENCE.md`:386, 732). "Choosing a card shows its five stats" and "choosing a stat offers its two directions" say nothing of what a stat is to assistive technology or what its name holds; a screen-reader user who hears only "Popularité" cannot play, since the choice depends on the card's values. (1.3.1, 4.1.2, 2.4.6.) *Fix:* the hand is a group named "Votre main" of three toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`), each named "<game>, atout : <stat>"; the stats are a group named "Statistiques de <game>" of five toggle buttons with `aria-expanded`, each named with the stat and its percentile in words ("Sortie : plus ancienne que 97 % des cartes"), the trump adding "atout"; the directions are a group labelled by the stat ("Sortie de <game> : choisissez le sens") of two buttons whose names are the direction words. State these names in the Copy deck, French and English.

### Medium

- **[medium]** M1. Shown values can contradict the outcome (`EXPERIENCE.md`:386, 388; `duel-rules.md`, Stats). The round is decided on percentiles, but the screen shows "both values" and Flow 11 reads raw years ("1998 contre 2014"). Circulation is ranked within the card's rarity tier, so a Commune with 50 copies can lose "LA PLUS RÉPANDUE" to a Légendaire with 10; equal percentiles fall back to exact values. A player who hears "50 contre 10, perdue" cannot understand the rule. (3.3, cognitive; 1.3.1.) *Fix:* show and announce each value with its percentile in words ("50 exemplaires, plus répandue que 40 % des Communes"); when the exact value broke a tie, the outcome says "départagée à la valeur exacte".

- **[medium]** M2. The 0–100 bar and its words do not say which end is strong (`EXPERIENCE.md`:385; `DESIGN.md`:1256). A trump can be extreme low (3 % popularity, "la plus confidentielle"): a short bar then marks a strong card. The track is `panel-sunken` on `panel` (1.06:1) and invisible if the duel rows sit on the card detail's `panel-sunken` fact rows, so the bar's full length cannot be seen; the Colors rule asks `line-deep` for progress tracks (`DESIGN.md`:613). (1.4.11, 1.3.1, cognitive.) *Fix:* define for each stat which end is 100 and write the words toward the stronger side ("Plus confidentielle que 97 % des cartes", never "Plus populaire que 3 %"); give the track a 1px `line-deep` edge (3.20:1 on panel, 3.38:1 on panel-sunken) or put the block on `panel`; hide the bar from assistive technology (the words carry the value) or make it `role="meter"` with `aria-valuetext` equal to the words, not a progress bar.

- **[medium]** M3. The AMIS / MARCHÉ control has the wrong semantics and an ambiguous name (`DESIGN.md`:1252; `EXPERIENCE.md`:37–38, 87). It is drawn "over the Tabs and filters rules", which make each part a button with `aria-pressed` (`DESIGN.md`:785), but MARCHÉ is its own address (the teaser keeps it) and each part is a screen a link opens. "AMIS" now names three different things: this sub-tab (trades with friends), the Amis screen in the player menu (the friends list) and the feed's "AMIS" scope chip. (1.3.1, 4.1.2, 2.4.6; 3.2.4 in spirit.) *Fix:* a `<nav>` named "Échanges" holding two links, the current one with `aria-current="page"`, Tab between them (not arrow keys); rename the sub-tab "ENTRE AMIS" (or keep "AMIS" visibly and name it "Échanges entre amis"); add both names to the Copy deck.

- **[medium]** M4. No new or summed badge has an accessible name (`EXPERIENCE.md`:271, Copy deck). The Count badge row requires the number in the item's name, and the avatar's summed badge is itemised, but the Copy deck has nothing for the DUELS tab and menu 04, the ÉCHANGES tab and menu 03 now summing trades and auctions, or the AMIS sub-tab; "Marché, <n> enchères dépassées" (`EXPERIENCE.md`:187) was written for a tab that no longer exists. The Bell copy (`EXPERIENCE.md`:198) lacks the two duel lines the Bell row adds. (1.3.1, 4.1.2.) *Fix:* add to the Copy deck: "Duels, 2 duels à jouer" ("Duels, 2 duels to play"; zero: "Duels"); "Échanges, 1 échange et 2 enchères à traiter" (itemised, each part omitted at zero); "Entre amis, 1 échange en attente"; "Marché, 2 enchères dépassées"; bell lines "<name> vous a défié en duel." / "Votre duel contre <name> est terminé." with "Jouer" and "Voir", in both languages. The native apps' tab items carry the same accessibility label (`EXPERIENCE.md`:816).

- **[medium]** M5. Disabled controls hide their reason and greyed text may fail contrast (`EXPERIENCE.md`:382, 638–642). "LANCER UN DUEL" and "COMMENCER" are disabled with the reason under them, "DÉFI OUVERT" is disabled, drafts and friends in a duel are "greyed". A native `disabled` button leaves the tab order, so a keyboard or screen-reader user never meets the reason; drafts and friends greyed in `disabled-text` read at 2.84:1, under 4.5:1 for "<n> / 12" and "Duel en cours". (4.1.2, 1.4.3.) *Fix:* use `aria-disabled="true"` with `aria-describedby` pointing to the visible reason, so the control stays focusable and explains itself; a greyed draft or friend keeps its name and reason in `muted` (5.98:1) and dims only its thumbnail or avatar; each unavailable option is a radio with `aria-disabled` and its reason in its name ("Vieux briscards, brouillon, 9 sur 12").

- **[medium]** M6. The 12-slot deck editor is hard to operate without a pointer and silent (`EXPERIENCE.md`:384; `DESIGN.md`:1255). A card can only be taken out by finding it again in the sorted, filtered collection; nothing announces "added", the slot count or the refusal "Le deck est complet."; the collection's keyboard model is not named; with four rows of `card-xs` (600px and more) the slots fill a 740px phone screen, so a sighted player adding from the collection below cannot see the deck change either; autosave failures have no stated feedback. (2.1.1, 4.1.3, 3.3.1.) *Fix:* each filled slot is a button named "Retirer <game> du deck"; collection cards are toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`, "DANS LE DECK" as the pressed state) in the card picker's grid model (one tab stop, arrow keys, `EXPERIENCE.md`:655); a polite status region posts "<game> ajoutée, 7 sur 12", "<game> retirée, 6 sur 12", "Le deck est complet." and a save failure; on the phone a sticky line under the top bar shows the deck's name and "<n> / 12"; the name field saves on blur and shows its 1–24 refusal inline with `aria-describedby`.

- **[medium]** M7. The Motion table has no duel rows (`EXPERIENCE.md`:659–706). The opponent's card "turns face up" (a rotation) and "SUIVANTE draws the next card": the generic reduced-motion rule forbids rotation but the house spells out every exception, and nothing says whether a Légendaire opponent card plays its reveal sequence (flash, shake). (2.3.3, 2.2.2.) *Fix:* add "Opponent card | about 300ms | a plain flip, no burst, no flash, no shake, whatever its rarity" and "Card drawn | about 200ms | slides into the hand"; under reduced motion both crossfade in 220ms, as the Reveal; the outcome text appears and is announced once the card is face up (H3).

- **[medium]** M8. Sizes break reflow and leave targets unsized (`DESIGN.md`:1255, 721–728; `EXPERIENCE.md`:386). "3 × 4 at the smallest card size" does not say which way: four columns of `card-xs` (424px with gaps) overflow a 360px phone, and even three (316px) overflow a 320 CSS px viewport (400 % zoom of a 1280px window). The hand of three cards plus the opponent's card has the same problem. The direction labels are long ("LA PLUS CONFIDENTIELLE", "DÉCOUVERTE EN DERNIER") and will not sit two abreast at 328px under text spacing. (1.4.10, 2.5.8, 1.4.12.) *Fix:* write "three columns, four rows"; below 340px the slots and the hand use a thumbnail tile (at least 44 × 66) instead of a card face; directions stack full width on the phone (H2); name a size token for the hand and for the slots, and keep every slot a 44px target 8px apart.

- **[medium]** M9. Repeated actions are not told apart (`EXPERIENCE.md`:381–383). Each row of "À VOUS DE JOUER" has a "JOUER", each recent result opens a result, "DÉFIER EN DUEL" sits on friend rows: a screen-reader user tabbing or listing buttons hears "Jouer, Jouer, Jouer". (2.4.6, 2.4.4.) *Fix:* names in the Copy deck, as for "Relever, <card>": "Jouer contre Hugo, reste 31 heures", "Victoire contre Hugo, 6 à 4", "Défier Léa en duel".

- **[medium]** M10. In English "Rating" names two things (`EXPERIENCE.md`:178–179). The stat "Note" is "Rating" and the Elo "Cote" is "Rating" too; on the ranking, the profile and a duel round read aloud they cannot be told apart. (3.1, 2.4.6, cognitive.) *Fix:* the Elo "Cote" becomes "Duel rating" (or "Rank points") in English; the stat stays "Rating" (or "Review score").

- **[medium]** M11. The half's timing is contradictory and the caller's own deadline is unstated (`spec-gameble.md` CAP-41 and `duel-rules.md` "in one sitting"; `EXPERIENCE.md`:386 "coming back within the 48 hours resumes"). The resume rule is the accessible one: a player who needs a pause, switches assistive technology or is interrupted keeps the half. A build following the spec's "one sitting" could add a session timeout. It is also unsaid what happens if the player who started never comes back. (2.2.1, 2.2.6.) *Fix:* settle the spec on the UX's wording ("in their half, a player makes 5 calls, and may leave and resume"); state in the Floor "no timer within a half; the only limit is the 48 h window, and the duel screen shows the end time"; say whether the caller's own unfinished half plays automatically at 48 h and that the bell warns once at 6 h left.

- **[medium]** M12. The change arrows' names are underspecified (`EXPERIENCE.md`:384, 732; `DESIGN.md`:1256). "The previous percentile in its accessible name" gives a number without the stat, the direction or the current value, and a "▲" glyph is read as "black up-pointing triangle". Five arrows on a `card-xs` face also risk text under the 10px floor. (1.1.1, 1.3.1, 1.4.4.) *Fix:* the glyph is hidden from assistive technology; a visually hidden text reads "Popularité en hausse : 62, contre 55 lundi dernier"; the slot's or card's accessible name ends with its changes; the arrows sit in a row under the card, not on its face, at 10px or more.

### Low

- **[low]** L1. Slashes, dashes and abbreviations read badly (`EXPERIENCE.md`:178–179). "MANCHE 2 / 5", "<n> / 12", "MES DECKS · 3 / 5", "6 – 4" and "reste 31 h" are read as "2 slash 5", "6 4" or "6 tiret 4", "31 h". The house already reads slashes through hidden text (`EXPERIENCE.md`:736). *Fix:* visually hidden "2 sur 5" / "2 of 5", "6 à 4" / "6 to 4", "31 heures" / "31 hours" (in a `<time>` element), in the Copy deck.

- **[low]** L2. Direction labels written in capitals in the source would be spelled out by some screen readers ("LA" read letter by letter). *Fix:* write every new label in sentence case in `fr.yml` and `en.yml` and uppercase it with CSS, as the rest of the interface does.

- **[low]** L3. The empty slot's dashed `line-soft` frame reads 1.43:1 on `panel-sunken` (`DESIGN.md`:1255). The "<n> / 12" text carries the count, so this is not a failure, but the slot is the place a card lands. *Fix:* draw it in `line-deep` (3.38:1).

- **[low]** L4. Steel carries no distinction by colour (`DESIGN.md`:622, 996–997). It sits at 1.15:1 and 1.30:1 from body text and white, 1.31:1 from `tier-silver` and 1.03:1 from the canister's steel caps, so the duel capsule's label band blends into the top cap and the "DÉFENSE" tag reads as a white tag. The printed names carry the meaning, as required. The duel emblem has no shade colour and no contrast check against the emblem window, unlike the sun and the cup. *Fix:* give the label band a 1px `outline` rule against the caps; give the crossed blades a shade and check them as the cup was checked; keep "CAPSULE DE DUEL" on every surface that shows the colour (chip, tab, panel while opening).

- **[low]** L5. `aria-current` is unclear for the new roots (`EXPERIENCE.md`:274). "DUELS on its own, with `aria-current="true"` there and `"page"` on the hub alone" can be read as forbidding `"page"` on the Duels screen itself. *Fix:* "`aria-current="page"` on a tab whose root is shown, `"true"` on PONT over Fil d'activité and Le Chattomètre, on DUELS over its sub-screens and on ÉCHANGES over the trade board and the Annonce".

- **[low]** L6. Desktop menu key 4 changes meaning (`EXPERIENCE.md`:85). Keys 1 to 8 open menu items; 4 opened MARCHÉ and now opens DUELS, and the market moves to 3. *Fix:* nothing in the shortcuts; note the change in the 1.4 release notes and in Comment ça marche.

- **[low]** L7. The duel result's ten rounds are a "list" of many fields (`EXPERIENCE.md`:388). *Fix:* the summary first ("Victoire contre Hugo, 6 à 4, départagée à l'écart de 12 points"), then an ordered list where each round is one sentence ("Manche 3, Hugo appelle Popularité, la plus populaire : Portal 2, 98 %, contre Hollow Knight, 91 %. Perdue."), or a table with column headers; explain "Départagé à l'écart" in words the first time ("par l'écart total des manches gagnées").

- **[low]** L8. The Monday stats entry's rises and falls have no stated form (`EXPERIENCE.md`:391). *Fix:* write "en hausse" / "en baisse" with the old and new percentile in the text; any arrow is decorative and uncoloured, as in the deck editor.

- **[low]** L9. Esc on the duel screen is unstated (`EXPERIENCE.md`:656). With no overlay, Esc returns to the hub; a keyboard user pressing it to drop a chosen card leaves the half (it resumes, so nothing is lost). *Fix:* on the duel screen, Esc first clears a chosen stat, then a chosen card, and only then follows the general rule.

- **[low]** L10. Deck list rows show twelve thumbnails (`EXPERIENCE.md`:383). *Fix:* the thumbnails are hidden from assistive technology and the row is one link named "<deck>, deck de défense, 12 cartes" or "<deck>, brouillon, 9 sur 12".
