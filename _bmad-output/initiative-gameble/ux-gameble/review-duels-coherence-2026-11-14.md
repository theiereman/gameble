# Coherence review — gameble 1.4 duels

Reviewed on 2026-11-14 against commits `6bb5b1b6` (forged idea), `1c796569` (spec, `duel-rules.md`, brief, addendum, `later-roadmap.md`) and `3f6e8b09` (`DESIGN.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`), with the rest of the spec, `achievements.md`, the reserved-copies plan and a light read of `architecture-gameble.md`.

## Verdict

**Coherent in substance, not yet buildable.** The forged idea, CAP-39 to CAP-43, `duel-rules.md` and the UX agree on almost every number: 12-card decks, one blocked copy, 5 decks, the five stats and how each is ranked, Monday 00:00 UTC, freeze at start, 5 calls from a hand of 3, the caller choosing the direction, tie rules, 48 h then automatic play, no forfeit win, 10 duels and one per pair, the UTC-day capsule at daily bonus odds, Elo from 1,000 on open challenges, 5 ranked duels to appear, and the season reset. The market move is applied wherever the old MARCHÉ tab and menu 04 appeared in the two UX files. What is missing: five rule gaps or contradictions a builder would hit on day one (which deck a friend defends with, the "one sitting" rule against a resumable half, private players against CAP-8, deck blocks on the `reserved` count, export and deletion), plus some medium gaps and stale lines.

Counts: 0 critical, 5 high, 11 medium, 11 low.

## Critical

None.

## High

- **[high]** A friend duel never says which deck the friend defends with. `duel-rules.md:38` "the player picks a friend and a deck, and plays their half at once", and `:30` "The defending player's card comes from the top of their own shuffled deck". The defence deck only governs open challenges (`:25` "Without one, a player receives no open challenge"). `EXPERIENCE.md:382` lists every friend and greys out only "a friend already in a duel with the player". The challenger plays first, so the friend's deck has to be fixed at the start. Nothing says whether it is the friend's defence deck, nor what happens with a friend who has no complete deck. Nothing says either that each player calls and defends with the same deck. *Fix:* in `duel-rules.md` and CAP-41, state that a friend duel uses the friend's defence deck and that a friend without one cannot be challenged (greyed out in Lancer un duel with a reason line), or record the author's other choice. Add one line: "a player calls and defends with the deck frozen at the start".

- **[high]** "One sitting" contradicts a resumable half, and the challenger has no deadline. On one side: forge:11 "each player makes 5 calls in one sitting", `duel-rules.md:29`, and CAP-41 (`spec-gameble.md` CAP-41) "two halves of 5 calls, each played in one sitting". On the other: `EXPERIENCE.md:386` "Leaving keeps the half where it stands; coming back within the 48 hours resumes at the same round". The only deadline (`duel-rules.md:40`) is the opponent's. A challenger who leaves mid-half keeps the duel open forever, holding a pair slot and one of the 10. Nothing says when the opponent's 48 h start (at the duel's start, or once the challenger's half is done), nor when the opponent is notified. *Fix:* the author picks: either a half really is one sitting (leaving ends it, and the remaining calls are played automatically), or it can be resumed. Then give the challenger the same 48 h with automatic completion, start the opponent's clock and bell line when the challenger's half ends, and align CAP-41, `duel-rules.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md`.

- **[high]** Private players can duel friends, which contradicts CAP-8, and their name is treated differently from the market. On one side: `spec-gameble.md:94` "a friend sees nothing beyond the friendship: no presence or activity, no collection, no profile, and no trade either way", and architecture AD-14 (`architecture-gameble.md:183`) "`open_to_friends?` … Trades, presence … ask it; nothing else needs it". On the other: `duel-rules.md:42` "A private player can duel friends", forge:17. A duel shows the private player's deck cards, results and bell activity to friends. Separately, `spec-gameble.md:419` shows a private player in the market "by name with no link", while `duel-rules.md:42` and `EXPERIENCE.md:644` show them as "Membre discret" in open challenges. Nothing says what going private does to duels in progress or to the defence deck, or how a friends-only opponent from an open challenge is shown. *Fix:* amend CAP-8 with an explicit duel exception, or close duels for private players as trades are. Keep one rule for a private name across the market and duels, and say what going private does to duels and the defence deck. Update AD-14's `open_to_friends?` note when the architecture is updated.

- **[high]** The forge's decision on deck blocks landed nowhere, and it would break built code. Forge:31 "Built on holdings' reserved count; `Game::ReservedCopies` must tell a deck block from a trade". Neither the spec nor the architecture carries this. The class built in `faa9c88a` (`plan-collection-reserved-copies.md:29`) derives "copies in a trade = reserved minus on sale", so a deck block would show "1 en échange". The architecture's reservation releases (`architecture-gameble.md:194` going private "releasing their reservations", `:195` deletion "releasing reservations") would silently free deck blocks. *Fix:* add an architecture decision before the build: either a deck-block count kept apart from trade reservations, or a derived split like listings. Update `Game::ReservedCopies` to name "Dans un deck" (`EXPERIENCE.md:411`). Make going private release trade reservations only.

- **[high]** Export and deletion don't cover decks, duels or ratings. `spec-gameble.md` CAP-14 (line 146) and the constraints at `:354` ("Market and personal data: the export (CAP-14) includes a player's PiCoin history, listings and bids") and `:358` (Steam data) list every other kind of personal data. Nothing covers decks, duel history, the season rating or past final ranks. Deletion also has to keep a deleted player's frozen deck so that "a banned or deleted player's duels in progress finish by automatic play" (`duel-rules.md:42`), then show them as "anonyme". *Fix:* add a "Duels and personal data" constraint: the export includes decks, duel results and ratings, and deletion drops decks and the rating once the duels in progress have finished, with the frozen duel snapshot and the opponent's results kept under "anonyme". Mirror it in the deletion handler of architecture AD-14.

## Medium

- **[medium]** Decks open before any stat exists, and the launch timing was never decided by the author. `spec-gameble.md:356` and `duel-rules.md:53` "Duels open on the first Monday after release 1.4, once the first stat computation has run; decks can be built from the release". Between the release and that Monday, the editor's "sorted by any stat … filtered by trump" (`EXPERIENCE.md:384`), the card detail's DUEL block (`:385`) and the change arrows have no values. The "Duels not open yet" state (`:638`) only disables duels. The forge says nothing about launch timing; the spec memlog derives it ("so the first stat computation has data"). *Fix:* run the first computation at deploy (simplest), or add a "no values yet" state to the editor and the detail. Put the timing to the author in `pending-approvals.md`.

- **[medium]** It is unclear which edition a deck slot and a duel profile are. Stats are per card-season: `duel-rules.md:9` "review count of the card's season", discovery per edition (`spec-gameble.md:142` "Each edition has its own pioneer"), circulation within the edition's tier. But the card detail shows one card per game across editions (`spec-gameble.md:75`, `:85`), and `EXPERIENCE.md:385` does not say which edition's profile shows. CAP-40 says "12 distinct cards", while `duel-rules.md:23` says "each card-season at most once", which allows S1 and S2 of the same game in one deck. *Fix:* say that a deck slot is an edition (card-season), that the S1 and S2 editions of a game count as two cards (or not), and that the detail's DUEL block shows the published season's edition, like "Prix moyen" (`EXPERIENCE.md:311`), or one profile per owned edition.

- **[medium]** The duel cap is undefined for open challenges a player receives. `duel-rules.md:41` "At most 10 duels in progress per player, and one at a time between the same two players"; `:39` matches "at random with a player who has a defence deck". Nothing says whether received challenges count toward a defender's 10, or that matching skips defenders at the cap or already in a duel with the challenger. An inactive defence deck would otherwise pile up duels. `EXPERIENCE.md:642` disables "DÉFI OUVERT" only "when no other player has a defence deck". *Fix:* state that every duel counts toward both players' caps and that matching draws only among defenders under the cap and not already facing the challenger. Update the "No open opponent" state to match.

- **[medium]** The Discovery stat is undefined for cards nobody has pulled. `duel-rules.md:12` ranks discovery "over the season's catalog", but only pulled card-seasons have a first-discovery row (`architecture-gameble.md:143`), and percentiles shift as new cards are discovered. *Fix:* rank discovery over the season's discovered card-seasons only, and state it in `duel-rules.md`.

- **[medium]** The duel ranking points to Flukeometer rules that the spec never defines, and that `EXPERIENCE.md` states two ways. CAP-43 (`spec-gameble.md:345`) is the spec's only mention of the Flukeometer; no capability defines it. `duel-rules.md:49` "a friends-only player is 'Membre discret' to non-friends" matches the copy deck (`EXPERIENCE.md:177` "an anonymous row 'Membre discret'") and the built `fortune_registers/_row.html.erb`. But `EXPERIENCE.md:345` (Rows) says "A friends-only player seen by anyone else shows name, avatar and figures with no link (author, 2026-11-09)". *Fix:* correct the Chattomètre Rows rule to match the build, or the reverse after asking the author. Write the visibility rule into CAP-43 or `duel-rules.md` instead of a pointer.

- **[medium]** Nothing says what happens to a defence deck that becomes a draft or is deleted. `duel-rules.md:25` "One complete deck may be the defence deck". `EXPERIENCE.md:411` "RETIRER DES DECKS … takes it out of every deck", and `:384` "SUPPRIMER LE DECK", can turn the defence deck into an 11/12 draft or remove it. Nothing says whether it stays the defence deck or what open challenges then do. *Fix:* a defence deck that becomes a draft keeps its tag but receives no open challenge until it is complete again (or loses the role), with a confirmation that says so.

- **[medium]** The "AMIS" sub-tab name collides with existing names, and the Échanges chrome was not updated. `EXPERIENCE.md:47` makes the Échanges sub-tab "AMIS" (trade suggestions, trade list, trade board). `:49` is the Amis screen (the friends list in the player menu); the feed has an AMIS tab (`:428`); and the apps' back example "◀ AMIS" (`:822`) now reads two ways. The Échanges top-bar subtitle "Entre amis · une proposition expire après 7 jours" (`DESIGN.md:926`) is wrong on the MARCHÉ sub-tab. The two-column Échanges layout (`DESIGN.md:701`) does not place the segmented control. *Fix:* rename the sub-tab "ÉCHANGES ENTRE AMIS" or "PROPOSITIONS", or confirm the clash with the author. Make the subtitle depend on the sub-tab, and lay out the control above both columns.

- **[medium]** The market's visitor access and the default sub-tab are not covered by the merge. `EXPERIENCE.md:91` and `:559` let a visitor open Marché "from the landing only". Marché is now a sub-tab of a screen whose other sub-tab needs an account. Nothing says what a visitor sees in the segmented control, or which sub-tab ÉCHANGES and menu 03 open on (the badge sums both). Separately, the duel ranking is "open without an account" (`EXPERIENCE.md:64`), but CAP-18 (`spec-gameble.md` CAP-18) "Every other page … leads to the landing" lists neither it nor the Chattomètre (an existing gap). *Fix:* a visitor's Marché shows no segmented control (or a greyed AMIS); ÉCHANGES opens on AMIS, or on the sub-tab with the badge. Add the duel ranking (and Le Chattomètre) to CAP-18, and give a visitor a way to reach the ranking.

- **[medium]** The badge accessible names still name the old MARCHÉ tab. `EXPERIENCE.md:187` (Market accessible text): badge "Marché, <n> enchères dépassées". `:271`: "Menu 03 and the ÉCHANGES tab carry the sum of the trades and auctions badges", and "Its number is part of the item's accessible name". No copy names the summed ÉCHANGES badge or the DUELS badge. *Fix:* add "Échanges, <n> échanges en attente, <m> enchères dépassées" and "Duels, <n> à jouer", with English, to the copy deck. Keep "Marché, …" for the sub-tab.

- **[medium]** Alt accounts can farm the duel capsule. CAP-42's intent is "without turning duels into a capsule farm", yet a friend duel counts toward the first win of the day (forge:35, `duel-rules.md:46`), and friend duels are unranked. A player with an alt friend who throws every half gets a second daily 3-card capsule at bonus odds. The market has alt-account signals (CAP-35 to CAP-38); duels have none. *Fix:* ask the author whether only open challenges should give the capsule, or add a duel alt signal to the back-office.

- **[medium]** The architecture is not updated for 1.4 (flagged only, not deep-reviewed). `architecture-gameble.md:37` lists the `Game` domain's contents without decks, duels, stats or ratings. `:113` "Each pack kind (regular, daily bonus, weekly reward)" lacks the duel capsule. No decision covers the weekly stat computation, the frozen duel snapshot, the 48 h automatic-play job, Elo, or the open-challenge draw. Yet the spec's Architecture constraint says "the rules of `architecture-gameble.md` bind every build". *Fix:* run an architecture update for 1.4 before slicing stories.
