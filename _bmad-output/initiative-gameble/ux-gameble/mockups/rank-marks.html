Rank marks

Reference for ranked set tiers (approved 2026-10-19): the rank marks, the set row, the album's tier bar on the simplified header, and the set celebration at every rank. Chosen marks: G1a (author, 2026-10-19) on a 1px grid: Novice, Apprenti, Adepte are 1 to 3 chevrons (9px wide, 2px stroke, stacked every 3px), Expert one 9px star, Maître two 7px stars. One neutral colour (text-bright) for every rank; only COMPLET keeps lime. A mark not yet reached is drawn solid in line-deep.

Ranks count from the top: the last tier before completion is always Maître. 5–9 cards: Maître 50 %. 10–19: Expert ⅓, Maître ⅔. 20–59: Adepte 25, Expert 50, Maître 75. 60–199: Apprenti 10, Adepte 25, Expert 50, Maître 75. 200+: Novice 10, Apprenti 25, Adepte 50, Expert 75, Maître 90. The back tier (first at or above half) is Maître, Expert or Adepte, so it always celebrates at gold's level or above. DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md win on conflict.
