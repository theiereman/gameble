Reaction icons

The five feed reactions — pouce levé, cœur, GG, wow, doigt d'honneur — redrawn after the author found the first drawing very poor (staging, 2026-11-03). For each reaction, two or three drawings, each shown as its exact pixel grid (×8), then at its real size in the reaction bar: resting, hover and lit (the player's own reaction). The recommended set is framed in blue; the composer at the bottom puts any mix on a feed entry, desktop and phone, beside the icons the feed already uses.

Half the defect is the size, not the drawing. The bar's stylesheet forces every icon to height: 1.25em of an 11px label, 13.75px: an 11-row icon then gets 1.25 CSS px per art pixel, a 7-row one 1.96, a 9-row one 1.53. Pixels come out 1 or 2 px at random, the five icons sit at three different scales, and DESIGN's rule (one art pixel is 2 CSS px, never chunky) breaks. Every new drawing here is 11 art pixels tall at 2 CSS px, 22px — the size of the phone tab bar's icons — and needs that one declaration dropped so the SVG keeps the size the export gives it. Without it, no drawing can come out crisp.

Click a reaction in a feed mock to light it, as the app does: one per player per entry, a second click removes it.
