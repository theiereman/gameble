Reaction icons

The five feed reactions — pouce levé, cœur, GG, wow, doigt d'honneur. Round 3 follows the author's change of direction (2026-11-03): no hand-drawn pixel grids any more. Each reaction is drawn as a precise vector image inspired by the emoji, in flat colour, and the app's own filter, pixelcrush (the one that pixelates the card art), turns it into pixel art. So the icons are pixelated the way the cards are, not drawn pixel by pixel. For each reaction: the vector source, then its pixelcrush output at three grid sizes and two palette sizes, enlarged and at its real size in the 34px button and the 44px touch button. The plain vectors, in colour and in monochrome, are the alternative. Rounds 1 and 2 are collapsed at the bottom.

Approved by the author on 2026-11-03: the pixelcrush output at 16 pixels square and 8 colours, shown at 1 CSS px per pixel (16px), with T7, H7, G5, W6 and a new middle finger, F8. Every image keeps a whole number of CSS px per pixel. At 2 CSS px, DESIGN's art pixel, the image would be 9 pixels to stay near 18px, and pixelcrush leaves a smudge at 9 (the 9 rows below). At 16 and 20 pixels the shapes survive, and 16px sits with the 11px count the way round 2 asked. With 8 colours the outline and one shade survive; with 4, the thumb loses its outline. The app has one theme, dark, so every sample is on its panel.

Click a reaction in a feed mock to light it, as the app does: one per player per entry, a second click removes it.
