Social, on every width

Working mock, 2026-11-04. The bell's weekly-mission line led a desktop player to the Social screen, which only the phone was meant to show. Decision: no route is phone-only, only the layout changes. Social becomes a desktop screen too; the hub's three social entries merge into one; the weekly mission stays on the hub and also opens Social, at the very top of the page; a preview of the activity feed with a Voir le fil link comes right under it, on desktop and phone alike. Strings are the shipped ones; those in gold are new.

The menu goes from nine entries to seven. 04 SOCIAL carries what its three entries carried: the friends-online count, then two orange badges side by side, the trades waiting for the player and the invitations waiting for an answer, each absent at zero. Keys 1 to 7 follow the new numbers.

5 · Trade quick rows: cards on each side

Each row of Échanges in Social shows how many cards each side puts in, with a small rarity mark. On the left, the cards you give; on the right, the cards the friend gives. The mark replaces today's "◆ Épique" top-rarity label. On the phone the mark moves under the name, so the name keeps its width. Sample rows: Léa, you give a commune, a rare and an épique for a peu commune and a légendaire; Malik, one rare for one rare; Inès, two communes for an épique.

Chosen · idea 1, with each side named as in idea 2

One small bar per card, highest rarity first, and the count beside it. Reads at a glance, both the size and the value of each side. Past 6 cards a side shows six pips and "+n".

6 · Trade quick rows, compact on the phone

At 360px, the one-line row (avatar, name, both sides, chip) leaves the chip no room: "À VOUS" breaks on two lines. Three ways to make it compact. All three drop the 32px avatar on the phone (the name already says who), keep a 44px touch target, and cut a long name with an ellipsis ("Maximilien" is the long-name sample).

Compact 1 · two short lines
Name and chip on top, both sides below, labelled. Everything stays, about 56px a row.
