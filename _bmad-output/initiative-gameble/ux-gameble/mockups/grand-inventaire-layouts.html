Le Grand Inventaire — three layouts

Desktop at the wide page width, with the phone beside each. Same content in all three: head stats (discovered / total, per rarity, pace), sets, weekly and season pioneer boards, then the grid with search and filters, latest discoveries first. A found card carries its pioneer's name; a card nobody has found is the Card not owned face. Numbers and names are samples. "Vous" is the viewer.
