Set backs: the word, colours and motif of each

Ticket 9.3 asks the author to approve, before the export is drawn, the word, two colours and motif of every theme and hall of fame set back. This page draws each proposal with the real anatomy of the export: frame, field, inner line, corner brackets, a ring with a motif, and the 5 × 7 word. Nothing here is exported yet. Tick OK on a row you approve, or write a correction in its field; the bar at the bottom keeps the counter, the filter and the copy button in view.

Reference and rules

  • Key set-<kind>-<key>, asset back-<kind>-<key>-<size>.png in the five sizes xs, s, m, l, xl (50 × 75 up to 120 × 180 art px).
  • Word: one word, A-Z and 0-9 only, at most 9 letters, decorative. The export prints it only when it fits the width minus 16 art px, so a long word disappears on the small sizes (each row shows where). The existing CONFRERE and CENTURION behave the same.
  • Colours: two tokens of the DESIGN palette. The first colours the frame, the field (a dark shade of it), the ring and the motif. The second colours the word, the corner brackets and the ring ticks. Hex values are shown in each row.
  • Motif: one pixel motif (9 × 9) from a small shared family per kind, drawn in the ring: 18 motifs for the themes, 4 for the hall of fame. The variation between backs is colour, motif and word, not 46 separate pictures.
  • Hall of fame backs wear a gold double inner line to tell them from the themes.
  • Which sets have a back: themes and hall of fame sets. Franchise, studio and UFO sets grant nothing.
  • Unlock: the set's back tier (its first tier at or above half its cards; a set under 5 cards, on completion).

Questions for the author

  1. The seed list db/default_themes.yml holds 43 themes, the ticket says 42: the extra one is adult. Does it get a back (46 + 1), or none?
  2. Words longer than 5 letters vanish on size xs, longer than 6 on size s (the existing backs do too), and words of 8 or 9 letters touch the corner brackets on size m (see WASTELAND, MALAIMES, PHENOMENE). Accept, or restrict every word to 7 letters or fewer?
  3. An administrator can add a theme later; the catalogue test then fails until a back exists. Accept that rule, or give new themes a neutral fallback back?
  4. The 18 shared theme motifs: enough variety, or do you want some themes to get an own picture?
  5. Asset names keep the key as is: back-theme-souls_like-m.png, back-hall_of_fame-mal-aimes-m.png (underscore and hyphen). Fine?
