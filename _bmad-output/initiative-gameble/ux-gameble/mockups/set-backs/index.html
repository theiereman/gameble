Set backs: the word, colours and motif of each

Ticket 9.3 asks the author to approve, before the export is drawn, the word, two colours and motif of every theme and hall of fame set back. This page draws each proposal with the real anatomy of the export: frame, field, inner line, corner brackets, a ring with a motif, and the 5 × 7 word. Every motif is now drawn on a finer 17 × 17 grid and centred on the ring. Nothing here is exported yet. Tick OK on a row you approve, or write a correction in its field; the bar at the bottom keeps the counter, the filter and the copy button in view.

Round 2: the 34 backs you approved are pre-ticked and keep their drawing; the 13 others are redrawn per your answers (JUMP, PUZZLE, RACING and ADVENTURE show two proposals each: pick one with its radio button).

Reference and rules

  • Key set-<kind>-<key>, asset back-<kind>-<key>-<size>.png in the five sizes xs, s, m, l, xl (50 × 75 up to 120 × 180 art px).
  • Word: one word (SHMUP becomes BULLETS), A-Z and 0-9 only, at most 9 letters, decorative. The export prints it only when it fits the width minus 16 art px, so a long word disappears on the small sizes (each row shows where). The existing CONFRERE and CENTURION behave the same.
  • Colours: two tokens of the DESIGN palette. The first colours the frame, the field (a dark shade of it), the ring and the motif. The second colours the word, the corner brackets and the ring ticks. Hex values are shown in each row.
  • Motif: one pixel picture per set, hand-placed on a 17 × 17 grid (finer than the first 9 × 9 pass), drawn in the ring with the dark outline and a light and shadow rim. Its bounding box is centred on the ring centre; tick “Centre guides” in the bar to see the cross. The largest whole pixel scale that keeps it inside the ring is used: 2 px per cell at size m, 3 at l and xl, but only 1 px per cell at xs and s, where the ring is too small for a bigger picture (the row shows the scale per size). 47 motifs: 43 themes and 4 hall of fame.
  • Hall of fame backs wear a gold double inner line to tell them from the themes.
  • Which sets have a back: themes and hall of fame sets. Franchise, studio and UFO sets grant nothing.
  • Unlock: the set's back tier (its first tier at or above half its cards; a set under 5 cards, on completion).

Questions for the author

  1. On sizes xs and s the ring is too small for a bigger picture, so the 17 × 17 motif is drawn at 1 px per cell: fine detail, but small. Accept, or draw a simpler 9 × 9 version of each motif for those two sizes?
  2. Words longer than 5 letters vanish on size xs, longer than 6 on size s (the existing backs do too), and words of 8 or 9 letters touch the corner brackets on size m (see WASTELAND, PHENOMENE). Accept, or restrict every word to 7 letters or fewer?
  3. An administrator can add a theme later; the catalogue test then fails until a back exists. Accept that rule, or give new themes a neutral fallback back?
  4. Asset names keep the key as is: back-theme-souls_like-m.png, back-hall_of_fame-mal-aimes-m.png (underscore and hyphen). Fine?
