Exotic card: black, violet glow, polished metal

These faces use the app's own stylesheets (tokens.css, card.css, card-tilt-effects.css) and its own tilt and glitter code, bundled as tilt.js. Only exotic.css is new: it is what would be added to the game. Move the pointer over a card, or drag a finger on a phone: the face tilts, and a band of polished violet metal slides across the black frame and the body, with white and violet flakes that flash as the light reaches them. Under reduced motion there is no tilt and the glow stays still. The portraits are placeholders; the real ones are the testers' photos, pixelated.

4. How often it drops

Today a pack holds five cards and a card is a Légendaire 5 times in 10,000 in a regular pack (0.05 %, about one in 400 packs). The exotic must be rarer. Players open about a pack a day, so the rate decides whether anyone ever sees one. The six cards would be drawn evenly once an exotic drop happens.

OptionWhat it isExpected waitEffect
1 · Regular packs, 1 in 10,000 recommended An exotic can come out of any regular pack card, with a share of 1 in 10,000 (a fifth of the Légendaire rate), taken from the Commune share. About one in 2,000 packs per player. A daily player waits years; across 100 active players someone gets one roughly every 20 days. A rare event that gets talked about, with no new way to obtain it. Needs the draw odds and the pool to accept a card outside the season.
2 · Regular packs, 2 in 10,000 Same as above at twice the rate (two fifths of the Légendaire rate). About one in 1,000 packs per player. Still very rare, but a committed player can see one in a few years.
3 · A beta-builder capsule A special capsule origin (like the daily and goal ones) that holds one exotic card, granted on an event, a milestone or by hand. Set by the author per grant. The author controls who gets one and when, and the rarity of the drop does not touch the odds. Fits the special packs the game already has.

Questions still on the table for the author: which look, which drop option, and whether the exotic counts in the collection totals and set completions (the cards form a set of their own with no season, so the totals could simply ignore it).
