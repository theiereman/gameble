## Brand & Style

gameble looks like the terminal of a ship that carries every Steam game: the colors of Steam's own client, panels and readouts inspired by the interaction terminals of Helldivers 2, and real game covers turned into fine pixel art. The future suits Steam games better than the past. It is elegant and quiet, neither a sci-fi cliché nor cluttered: dark panels, thin blue lines, one lime action per screen, gold kept for what is rare.

The look comes from the author's own canvas, *Steam TCG — Concepts UI* (`imports/claude-design-concepts/`), made calmer and less dense. Its screenshots show the hub (`imports/ref-01-hub-command-bridge.png`), the booster opening (`imports/ref-02-booster-opening.png`), the collection (`imports/ref-03-collection.png`) and a four-tier rarity scale (`imports/ref-04-rarity-scale.png`, superseded by the five tiers below). The canvas filler (combat, decks, card stats, market, currencies) is not part of the product. The Hearthstone card-back reference (`imports/hearthstone-card-back-reference.png`) belonged to the abandoned medieval direction and no longer shapes the card backs.

Three ideas hold it together:

- **Fine pixel art over high-quality material.** It should feel as if a 16-bit filter sat over truly detailed assets, in the spirit of Balatro or Dead Cells. One art pixel is 2 CSS px (`{components.art-pixel.size}`) for everything drawn: covers, card backs, the canister, icons, the planet, particles. Never chunky, no exception.
- **A terminal, lightly.** Corner brackets, hazard stripes, segmented gauges, status lights, a faint scanline and a vignette. Each is used sparingly; a screen never stacks them all.
- **"Plus la carte est rare, plus elle prend vie."** Common cards are calm. Effects grow with rarity.

Movement is fluid; small effects tick in steps; only genuinely live things blink. Timings live in `EXPERIENCE.md` (see Motion).

Visual references: [mockups/key-screens.html](mockups/key-screens.html) (desktop prototype, every screen, including the chosen "Explosion A" opening on the booster screen) and [mockups/mobile.html](mockups/mobile.html) (phone prototype). DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md take precedence over any mock or import when they conflict. The mocks are a reference for look and timing, never source code.

## Colors

Dark only. Text reaches a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, and interface boundaries and the focus ring at least 3:1, against their background, measured with every overlay applied. `{colors.dim}` is the only exception and is decorative.

- **Ground (`{colors.ground}`)** fills every screen. Outside the app frame, `{colors.backdrop}`. The star field uses `{colors.star}`.
- **Steam bar (`{colors.steam-bar}`)** carries the top bar, the LIVE ticker and the phone tab bar, with a 2px `{colors.line}` rule toward the content.
- **Panels:** `{colors.panel}` for panels and the card body, `{colors.panel-sunken}` for quiet boxes and tracks, `{colors.panel-raised}` for title bands, selected rows and gauge tracks. `{colors.input}` is the text-field well.
- **Lines:** `{colors.line}` and `{colors.line-soft}` separate and frame panels (decoration). Every interactive boundary (fields, tabs, segments, radio squares, menu rows, stepper, progress tracks) uses `{colors.line-deep}` (3.2:1 on a panel) or brighter: `{components.control-border.css}`.
- **Accent (`{colors.accent}`)** is the terminal's voice: kickers, menu numbers, corner brackets, hazard stripes, gauge fills, hover fills and the LIVE label. Text on an accent fill is `{colors.on-accent}` (9.1:1). `{colors.accent-hi}` lights the edge of a hovered menu row and the card-back sheen; `{colors.accent-press}` is a pressed menu row. Rarity-colored text (Rare shares the accent hue) is never interactive.
- **Text:** `{colors.text}` for running text (12.8:1 on the ground), `{colors.text-bright}` on buttons, `{colors.text-max}` for titles and the focus ring, `{colors.muted}` for secondary text (6.0:1 on a panel), `{colors.publisher}` for the publisher line (4.95:1 on the title band). `{colors.dim}` (3.2:1) is for decoration only, never for words a player needs.
- **Outline (`{colors.outline}`)** draws every pixel border and the gap inside the focus ring.
- **Lime** is the single primary action: `{colors.primary}` as the button fill (text `{colors.on-lime}`, 6.2:1), `{colors.lime}` for count badges, the online dot, reserve pips, "PRÊT", "NOUVEAU" and the active card back. Never more than one lime button per view.
- **Gold (`{colors.gold}`)** belongs to the Légendaire tier, the "PREMIÈRE DÉCOUVERTE" chip, the quantity badge and the "DERNIÈRE OBTENTION" kicker. It is never a general accent. The phone's Légendaire caption plate sits on `{colors.plate-legendaire}`.
- **Orange (`{colors.alert}`)** is for alerts only: a trade waiting for the player (badge text `{colors.on-alert}`), the armed canister, expiry, "RÉSERVE PLEINE", report and delete, inline errors.
- **Danger:** `{colors.danger-bg}` with `{colors.danger-text}`, turning orange on hover; only for deleting the account.

### Rarity

Five tiers, each with a color, a pixel shape and a name. Color never stands alone.

| Tier | Name (FR) | Color | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Commune | `{colors.rarity-commune}` | Circle (solid disc) |
| 2 | Peu commune | `{colors.rarity-peu-commune}` (jade) | Double circle (a ring inside a ring) |
| 3 | Rare | `{colors.rarity-rare}` | Diamond |
| 4 | Épique | `{colors.rarity-epique}` | Triangle |
| 5 | Légendaire | `{colors.rarity-legendaire}` | Star |

Each tier has a `-hi` and `-lo` shade for the lit and shaded edges of its icon. The five silhouettes must stay distinct at 7 × 7 art pixels and in grayscale (Commune and Épique have almost the same luminance, as do Peu commune and Rare, so shape alone must separate them); every icon set is checked as a grayscale render before it ships. Peu commune is a darker, bluer jade (hue about 153°) so it never reads as the lime action; it is never used on a button. On `{colors.panel-raised}`, every rarity color reads at 5:1 or more.

## Typography

Two open-licensed (OFL) fonts, self-hosted (subsetted woff2 with their license texts), each with one job. Both cover French accents.

- **VT323** (400): the large text only, never under 20px: the logo and screen titles, the hero and public titles, the player's and showcase names, the reveal banner, big numbers and timers, the card title at xl and the friend code: `{typography.screen-title}`, `{typography.hero}`, `{typography.public-title}`, `{typography.banner}`, `{typography.banner-small}`, `{typography.showcase-name}`, `{typography.count-large}`, `{typography.number}`, `{typography.card-title-xl}`.
- **Space Mono** (400, 700): every small text. Bold for the card titles at s, m and l (`card-title-s` to `card-title-l`, uppercase) and the buttons (`{typography.button}`, `{typography.button-sm}`); regular for the menu, kickers, labels and chips in capitals with a light letter spacing (`{typography.menu}`, `{typography.kicker}`, `{typography.label}`), the card lines (`{typography.card-meta}` / `{typography.card-meta-xl}`), the publisher line (`publisher-m` to `publisher-xl`) and the running text, hints, descriptions and feed lines (`{typography.body}`, `{typography.body-large}`, `{typography.body-small}`).

Rules:

- **Readability floor:** no meaningful information below 10px: publisher, rarity, numbers, labels, status chips, tab labels, card lines. VT323 never goes under 20px, where it stops being readable; anything smaller is Space Mono. Only decorative text (`{typography.decorative-small}`, for example the word on a card back) may be smaller than 10px. There is no alternate-font setting.
- Multi-line running text (the `body` styles) runs at a line height of 1.5; the large VT323 titles sit at 1.0 to 1.1, and the card title at xl at 22px on a 20px line so two lines fit its band.
- Uppercase comes from `text-transform` over sentence-case source strings.
- Card titles wrap to two lines at most; the title band may grow rather than clip.
- **Glyphs the fonts lack** (◀ ▶ ▸ ◆ ⇄ ↗ ✕ ✓ ⧗) are drawn as pixel SVG icons, never typed as characters (◀ ▶ can render as color emoji).
- **Digit grouping** uses U+00A0 (no-break space): "31 420", "#4 215".
- **Phone ramp:** `{typography.screen-title-phone}`, `{typography.showcase-name-phone}`, `{typography.public-title-phone}`, `{typography.kicker-phone}`, `{typography.count-large-phone}`, `{typography.body-large-phone}`; other sizes are shared.
- Text sizes are set in rem from a 16px root; the art pixel stays in px. Rows holding text use a minimum height, never a fixed one.

## Layout & Spacing

The spacing scale is built on the 2px unit: 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32.

- **Gutters:** `{spacing.gutter-desktop}` on desktop, `{spacing.gutter-phone}` on the phone. Panels pad `{spacing.panel-padding}`.
- **Booster opening at the top (author, 2026-10-07):** the opening starts at the top of the page like every other screen, with more room between its components; its hazard stripe is the first thing on screen, and nothing is resized. This replaces the earlier vertical centring, which made the stripe look misplaced.
- **No scaling, anywhere.** The page never shrinks or grows to fit the window: every element keeps its size. A short window scrolls the page vertically, and the top bar and LIVE ticker scroll with it (they are not fixed). A narrower window reflows by width breakpoints. The booster stage is not scaled either.
- **Width:** content (and the top bar's contents) is capped at `{spacing.content-max}` and centered; the ground, star field, top bar and ticker backgrounds run full width. The exact cap is 1280px; the decision is "below 1440px". Tablets follow the same width rules.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is drawn in pixels, not in soft shadows.

- **Pixel outlines:** cards carry `{components.pixel-outline-4.css}`; buttons, badges and dialogs `{components.pixel-outline-3.css}`.
- **Bevel:** the card frame has `{components.card-face.bevel}`; buttons have `{components.button-primary.inset}` along the bottom, which moves to the top when pressed.
- **Panels** are flat, separated by an inset line, not by elevation.
- **Ground shadows:** a showcased card floats over a flat dark bar that breathes with its idle float.
- **Glow is rare:** a Légendaire gets a blurred gold glow only inside the booster opening. Rays (pixel sunbursts) turn behind Épique and Légendaire cards in the booster, the card detail, the hub showcase and the public card page; never behind lower tiers. Dialogs and toasts have one soft drop shadow so they sit above the screen.
- **Atmosphere, under the content:** a star field of 2 and 4px stars that stays still when the screen changes and only twinkles softly, a few stars at a time (no sideways shift); faint scanlines (`{components.scanlines.css}`); a vignette (`{components.vignette.desktop}`, phone `{components.vignette.phone}`). All three sit on the background and the art only, never over text, bars, toasts, dialogs or the tab bar.
- **Flash and shake** exist only inside the booster opening and the trade swap.
- **Blinking** is reserved for genuinely live things: the LIVE marker. Status lights, kickers, badges, timers, the focus frame and decorative pulses are steady. Nothing blinks faster than `{components.motion.blink-max-rate}`.

## Shapes

Corners are square (`{rounded.DEFAULT}`). The UI is built from rectangles, pixel outlines and stepped edges. Round and angled forms appear only as pixel art: rarity icons, rays, rings, the planet, the canister, card-back motifs.

#### Pixel filter

The cover is loaded from Steam's CDN at the vertical capsule address recorded at import (see `ux-gameble.md`, For the architecture), never copied, and transformed in the browser at display time:

1. Crop the vertical image to the art box ratio (nearly square), centered horizontally and anchored `{components.pixel-filter.crop-anchor-y}` from the top.
2. Downscale in halving steps to half the box size in CSS px (one image pixel per art pixel).
3. Reduce to a 32-color palette computed once per game (median cut), with no dithering.
4. Draw at 2 CSS px per pixel with nearest-neighbor scaling (`image-rendering: pixelated`).

A placeholder image (too small or single-channel) counts as missing. If cross-origin reading fails, the image is drawn without the color reduction, still at 2 CSS px. The same filter makes the thumbnails in the feed and trade lists.

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use Steam's colors and keep panels quiet and elegant | Pile on terminal gadgets, or frame screens in a heavy hardware bezel |
| Take inspiration from Helldivers 2's terminals | Use Helldivers names or trademarks ("Ordre majeur", "Super Earth", stratagem arrow codes) |
| Show the real Steam cover through the fine pixel filter (2 CSS px, 32 colors, no dither, nearest-neighbor) | Invent card art, store modified copies, or use chunky pixels |
| Draw everything on the 2 CSS px art pixel, the canister included | Make an exception for decor |
| Keep the card face to title, publisher, cover, genre, year, rarity and number | Add stats, cost, attack, health or effect text |
| Show rarity by shape, name and color together; check icons in grayscale | Rely on color alone, or let two silhouettes look alike |
| Keep information at 10px or more, VT323 at 20px or more | Put a rarity, number, label or publisher below 10px |
| Outside the booster screen, keep holo for the Légendaire only, without glow | Make every card shine, or glow outside the opening |
| Open the booster as a metal canister whose solid-edged caps blow off | Draw a glowing seam or cut line, notches on the caps, or sparks along a line |
| Move things fluidly and quickly; tick small effects in steps | Use slow, floaty animations |
| Keep idle float, the planet included, to 2px at most | Make cards or scenery bob strongly |
| Blink only what is live (the LIVE marker) | Blink status lights, badges, kickers or decorative pulses |
| Keep scanlines and vignette under the content | Lay them over text, bars, toasts or dialogs |
| Use one lime primary action per view | Use lime or jade as decoration |
| Keep gold for the Légendaire and true rewards | Use gold as a general accent or as a focus color |
| Use Valve's official sign-in image, untouched | Restyle, pixelate or translate the Steam sign-in button |
| Keep the Brotherhood to one line on the community goal, none in the feed | Give the player a title ("Capitaine", "Confrère") |
| Stay in the space-terminal direction | Bring back parchment, leather, brass, wax seals, dust or any medieval decor |
| Leave out what is not in the spec's scope | Show decks, combat, a market, sales, currencies or recycling from the generated canvas |
