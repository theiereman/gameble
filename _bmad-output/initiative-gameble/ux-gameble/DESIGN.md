## Brand & Style

gameble looks like the terminal of a ship that carries every Steam game: the colors of Steam's own client, panels and readouts inspired by the interaction terminals of Helldivers 2, and real game covers turned into fine pixel art. The future suits Steam games better than the past. It is elegant and quiet, neither a sci-fi cliché nor cluttered: dark panels, thin blue lines, one lime action per screen, gold kept for what is rare. In the lore (`../spec-gameble/lore.md`), each card is an archive card in the Brotherhood's proprietary format, and this space-terminal interface is the Brotherhood's authorized archive card reader.

The look comes from the author's own canvas, *Steam TCG — Concepts UI* (`imports/claude-design-concepts/`), made calmer and less dense. Its screenshots show the hub (`imports/ref-01-hub-command-bridge.png`), the capsule opening (`imports/ref-02-booster-opening.png`), the collection (`imports/ref-03-collection.png`) and a four-tier rarity scale (`imports/ref-04-rarity-scale.png`, superseded by the five tiers below). The canvas filler (combat, decks, card stats, market, currencies) is not part of the product. The Hearthstone card-back reference (`imports/hearthstone-card-back-reference.png`) belonged to the abandoned medieval direction and no longer shapes the card backs.

Three ideas hold it together:

- **Fine pixel art over high-quality material.** It should feel as if a 16-bit filter sat over truly detailed assets, in the spirit of Balatro or Dead Cells. One art pixel is 2 CSS px (`{components.art-pixel.size}`) for everything drawn: covers, card backs, the canister, icons, the planet, particles. Never chunky, no exception.
- **A terminal, lightly.** Corner brackets, hazard stripes, segmented gauges, status lights, a faint scanline and a vignette. Each is used sparingly; a screen never stacks them all.
- **"Plus la carte est rare, plus elle prend vie."** Common cards are calm. Effects grow with rarity.

Movement is fluid; small effects tick in steps; only genuinely live things blink. Timings live in `EXPERIENCE.md` (see Motion).

Visual references: [mockups/key-screens.html](mockups/key-screens.html) (desktop prototype, every screen, including the chosen "Explosion A" opening on the capsule screen), [mockups/mobile.html](mockups/mobile.html) (phone prototype) and [mockups/landing.html](mockups/landing.html) (the visitor landing, desktop and its 390px phone form; its gray REVUE toolbar, note pins and editable outlines are a review aid, not part of the design). DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md take precedence over any mock or import when they conflict. The mocks are a reference for look and timing, never source code.

## Colors

Dark only. Text reaches a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, and interface boundaries and the focus ring at least 3:1, against their background, measured with every overlay applied. `{colors.dim}` is the only exception and is decorative.

- **Ground (`{colors.ground}`)** fills every screen. Outside the app frame, `{colors.backdrop}`. The star field uses `{colors.star}`.
- **Steam bar (`{colors.steam-bar}`)** carries the top bar, the LIVE ticker and the phone tab bar, with a 2px `{colors.line}` rule toward the content.
- **Panels:** `{colors.panel}` for panels and the card body, `{colors.panel-sunken}` for quiet boxes and tracks, `{colors.panel-raised}` for the card face's line bands, selected rows and gauge tracks. `{colors.input}` is the text-field well.
- **Lines:** `{colors.line}` and `{colors.line-soft}` separate and frame panels (decoration). Every interactive boundary (fields, tabs, segments, radio squares, menu rows, stepper, progress tracks) uses `{colors.line-deep}` (3.2:1 on a panel) or brighter: `{components.control-border.css}`.
- **Accent (`{colors.accent}`)** is the terminal's voice: kickers, menu numbers, corner brackets, hazard stripes, gauge fills, hover fills and the LIVE label. Text on an accent fill is `{colors.on-accent}` (9.1:1). `{colors.accent-hi}` lights the edge of a hovered menu row and the card-back sheen; `{colors.accent-press}` is a pressed menu row. Rarity-colored text (Rare shares the accent hue) is never interactive.
- **Text:** `{colors.text}` for running text (12.8:1 on the ground), `{colors.text-bright}` on buttons, `{colors.text-max}` for titles and the focus ring, `{colors.muted}` for secondary text (6.0:1 on a panel), `{colors.publisher}` for the studio line on card faces, over the card veil (4.5:1 or more there, measured over the brightest art). `{colors.dim}` (3.2:1) is for decoration only, never for words a player needs.
- **Outline (`{colors.outline}`)** draws every pixel border and the gap inside the focus ring.
- **Lime** is the single primary action: `{colors.primary}` as the button fill (text `{colors.on-lime}`, 6.2:1), `{colors.lime}` for count badges, the online dot, reserve pips, "PRÊTE", "NOUVEAU" and the active card back. Never more than one lime button in view at once: a long page such as the landing may hold several, far apart (author, 2026-10-14).
- **Gold (`{colors.gold}`)** belongs to the Légendaire tier, the "PREMIÈRE DÉCOUVERTE" chip, the quantity badge and the "DERNIÈRE OBTENTION" kicker. It is never a general accent. The phone's Légendaire caption plate sits on `{colors.plate-legendaire}`.
- **Orange (`{colors.alert}`)** is for alerts only: a trade waiting for the player (badge text `{colors.on-alert}`), the armed canister, expiry, "RÉSERVE PLEINE", report and delete, inline errors.
- **Danger:** `{colors.danger-bg}` with `{colors.danger-text}`, turning orange on hover; only for deleting the account.
- **Capsule origins (`{colors.capsule-daily}`, `{colors.capsule-goal}`)** colour a special capsule by where it came from: the daily capsule yellow, the weekly goal reward red (author, 2026-10-28). `{colors.capsule-daily}` has the hex of `{colors.gold}` but is a separate role: gold stays the Légendaire tier's. `{colors.capsule-goal}` is not `{colors.live-red}`, which belongs to the LIVE marker alone, nor `{colors.alert}`, which belongs to alerts. A later origin adds its own token. Text on either fill is `{colors.outline}`.
- **Live red (`{colors.live-red}`)** is only the blinking square of the LIVE ticker's marker, on the accent fill (3.5:1) (author, 2026-10-12).

### Rarity

Five tiers, each with a color, a pixel shape and a name. Color never stands alone.

| Tier | Name (FR) | Color | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Commune | `{colors.rarity-commune}` | Circle (solid disc) |
| 2 | Peu commune | `{colors.rarity-peu-commune}` (jade) | Double circle (a ring inside a ring) |
| 3 | Rare | `{colors.rarity-rare}` | Diamond |
| 4 | Épique | `{colors.rarity-epique}` | Triangle |
| 5 | Légendaire | `{colors.rarity-legendaire}` | Star |

Each tier has a `-hi` and `-lo` shade for the lit and shaded edges of its icon. The five silhouettes must stay distinct at 7 × 7 art pixels and in grayscale (Commune and Épique have almost the same luminance, as do Peu commune and Rare, so shape alone must separate them); every icon set is checked as a grayscale render before it ships. Peu commune is a darker, bluer jade (hue about 153°) so it never reads as the lime action; it is never used on a button. On `{colors.panel-raised}`, every rarity color reads at 5:1 or more.

## Typography

Two open-licensed (OFL) fonts, self-hosted (subsetted woff2 with their license texts), each with one job. Both cover French accents.

- **VT323** (400): the titles, never under 20px: the screen titles, the hero and public titles, the landing's hook and module titles, the player's and showcase names, the reveal banner, the card title at xl and the friend code: `{typography.screen-title}`, `{typography.hero}`, `{typography.public-title}`, `{typography.banner}`, `{typography.banner-small}`, `{typography.showcase-name}`, `{typography.card-title-xl}`, and on the landing `{typography.landing-hook-medium}` and `{typography.landing-module-title}`.
- **JetBrains Mono** (400, 700): every other text, and every figure (author, 2026-10-13). Bold for the card titles at s, m and l (`card-title-s` to `card-title-l`, uppercase), the buttons (`{typography.button}`, `{typography.button-sm}`), the figures, and every style at 12px and below: kickers, labels and chips in capitals with a light letter spacing (`{typography.kicker}`, `{typography.label}`), the card lines (`{typography.card-meta}` / `{typography.card-meta-xl}`), the studio line (`publisher-m` to `publisher-xl`) and the small running text (`{typography.body-small}`, sentences included), and on the landing its tile titles and figures (`{typography.landing-tile-title}`, `{typography.landing-figure}`, `{typography.landing-figure-small}`). Regular for the menu (`{typography.menu}`) and the running text, hints, descriptions and feed lines (`{typography.body}`, `{typography.body-large}`).

Rules:

- **Readability floor:** no meaningful information below 10px: studio, rarity, numbers, labels, status chips, tab labels, card lines. Every style at 12px and below that carries information is bold; hierarchy among small text rests on colour (`{colors.text}`, `{colors.muted}`, `{colors.publisher}`, accent), size and capitals, not on weight. VT323 never goes under 20px, where it stops being readable. Only decorative text (`{typography.decorative-small}`, for example the word on a card back) may be smaller than 10px. There is no alternate-font setting.
- **Figures** (counts, clocks, quantities, odds, card numbers) are in JetBrains Mono bold, never in VT323. A figure VT323 used to set is sized to match VT323's digit height. Its digits stand 0.75 em tall against VT323's 0.5625 em, so the former VT323 size times 0.75, rounded to 0.5px: `{typography.number}` 18px (was 24px), `{typography.clock}` 19.5px (the screen-title size, 26px), `{typography.count-large}` 34.5px (46px), `{typography.count-large-phone}` 30px (40px). The large counts keep their former line heights, so the rows around them do not move. Figures inside card lines and labels keep their style's size.
- Multi-line running text (the `body` styles) runs at a line height of 1.5; the large VT323 titles sit at 1.0 to 1.1, and the card title at xl at 22px on a 20px line so two lines fit over the art.
- Uppercase comes from `text-transform` over sentence-case source strings.
- Card titles wrap to two lines at most, then end with an ellipsis; the full title stays in the accessible name and the card detail.
- **Glyphs the fonts lack** (◀ ▶ ▸ ◆ ⇄ ↗ ✕ ✓ ⧗) are drawn as pixel SVG icons, never typed as characters (◀ ▶ can render as color emoji).
- **Digit grouping** uses U+00A0 (no-break space): "31 420", "#4 215".
- **Phone ramp:** `{typography.screen-title-phone}`, `{typography.showcase-name-phone}`, `{typography.public-title-phone}`, `{typography.kicker-phone}`, `{typography.count-large-phone}`, `{typography.body-large-phone}`, `{typography.landing-module-title-phone}`; other sizes are shared.
- Text sizes are set in rem from a 16px root; the art pixel stays in px. Rows holding text use a minimum height, never a fixed one.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is drawn in pixels, not in soft shadows.

- **Pixel outlines:** cards carry `{components.pixel-outline-4.css}`; buttons, badges and dialogs `{components.pixel-outline-3.css}`.
- **Bevel:** the card frame has `{components.card-face.bevel}`; buttons have `{components.button-primary.inset}` along the bottom, which moves to the top when pressed.
- **Panels** are flat, separated by an inset line, not by elevation.
- **Ground shadows:** a showcased card floats over a flat dark bar that breathes with its idle float.
- **Glow is rare:** a Légendaire gets a blurred gold glow only inside the capsule opening. Rays (pixel sunbursts) turn behind Épique and Légendaire cards on the capsule screen, the card detail, the hub showcase and the public card page, and, as an approved exception (author, 2026-10-14), behind the Épique and Légendaire moving cards of the landing; never behind lower tiers. Dialogs and toasts have one soft drop shadow so they sit above the screen.
- **Atmosphere, under the content:** a star field of 2 and 4px stars that stays still when the screen changes and only twinkles softly, a few stars at a time (no sideways shift; on the landing only, it drifts slightly with the scroll, see `EXPERIENCE.md`, Motion); faint scanlines (`{components.scanlines.css}`); a vignette (`{components.vignette.desktop}`, phone `{components.vignette.phone}`). All three sit on the background and the art only, never over text, bars, toasts, dialogs or the tab bar.
- **Flash and shake** exist only inside the capsule opening and the trade swap.
- **Blinking** is reserved for genuinely live things: the LIVE marker. Status lights, kickers, badges, timers, the focus frame and decorative pulses are steady. Nothing blinks faster than `{components.motion.blink-max-rate}`.

## Shapes

Corners are square (`{rounded.DEFAULT}`). The UI is built from rectangles, pixel outlines and stepped edges. Round and angled forms appear only as pixel art: rarity icons, rays, rings, the planet, the canister, card-back motifs.

#### Pixel filter

The cover is loaded from Steam's CDN at the vertical capsule address recorded at import (see `ux-gameble.md`, For the architecture), never copied, and transformed in the browser at display time:

1. Crop the vertical image to the art box ratio (a little taller than wide), centered horizontally and anchored `{components.pixel-filter.crop-anchor-y}` from the top.
2. Downscale in halving steps to half the box size in CSS px (one image pixel per art pixel).
3. Reduce to a 32-color palette computed once per game (median cut), with no dithering.
4. Draw at 2 CSS px per pixel with nearest-neighbor scaling (`image-rendering: pixelated`).

A placeholder image (too small or single-channel) counts as missing. If cross-origin reading fails, the image is drawn without the color reduction, still at 2 CSS px. The same filter makes the thumbnails in the feed and trade lists.

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use Steam's colors and keep panels quiet and elegant | Pile on terminal gadgets, or frame screens in a heavy hardware bezel |
| Take inspiration from Helldivers 2's terminals | Use terms, slogans or elements from other games or fictional universes (e.g. Helldivers: "Ordre majeur", "Super Earth", stratagem arrow codes) |
| Show the real Steam cover through the fine pixel filter (2 CSS px, 32 colors, no dither, nearest-neighbor) | Invent card art, store modified copies, or use chunky pixels |
| Draw everything on the 2 CSS px art pixel, the canister included | Make an exception for decor |
| Keep the card face to cover, title, studio, genre, year, rarity, season and number, as each size allows | Add stats, cost, attack, health or effect text |
| Show rarity by shape, name and color together; check icons in grayscale | Rely on color alone, or let two silhouettes look alike |
| Keep information at 10px or more, bold at 12px and below; keep VT323 for titles, at 20px or more | Put a rarity, number, label or studio below 10px, or set a figure in VT323 |
| Give glitter and iridescence to the Légendaire only, and keep it without glow outside the capsule screen | Make every card shine, or glow outside the opening |
| Open the capsule as a metal canister whose solid-edged caps blow off | Draw a glowing seam or cut line, notches on the caps, or sparks along a line |
| Move things fluidly and quickly; tick small effects in steps | Use slow, floaty animations |
| Keep idle float, the planet included, to 2px at most | Make cards or scenery bob strongly |
| Blink only what is live (the LIVE marker) | Blink status lights, badges, kickers or decorative pulses |
| Keep scanlines and vignette under the content | Lay them over text, bars, toasts or dialogs |
| Use one lime primary action per view | Use lime or jade as decoration |
| Keep gold for the Légendaire and true rewards | Use gold as a general accent or as a focus color |
| Use Valve's official sign-in image, untouched | Restyle, pixelate or translate the Steam sign-in button |
| Keep the Brotherhood secondary, in its institutional voice (see `EXPERIENCE.md`, Voice and Tone); the landing alone presents it and its story to come (approved exception, author 2026-10-14) | Give the player a title ("Capitaine", "Confrère"), or carry the landing's Brotherhood story into the app |
| On the landing, let a few moving cards pass down the gutters beside the content on wide desktop with a fine pointer, draggable aside | Fill the landing with cards, lay them over the content column, tilt them with the pointer, or show them on the phone or to touch |
| On the landing, say what the game is and why it is free and open, in the app's voice | Break the landing's copy rules (see `EXPERIENCE.md`, Voice and Tone) |
| Give each landing module a numbered header, its title, then its subtitle below | Put status words, status lights or gray labels beside a module title |
| Stay in the space-terminal direction | Bring back parchment, leather, brass, wax seals, dust or any medieval decor |
| Leave out what is not in the spec's scope | Show decks, combat, a market, sales, currencies or recycling from the generated canvas |
