- (decision) Art direction: decaying sepia 'secret archives of the Brotherhood' mixed with 16-bit pixel art (Balatro-like), pixel style everywhere in the UI (party-mode session)
- (decision) Commons photos are dithered at import to a limited sepia palette at low resolution (Obra-Dinn-like) to unify heterogeneous images; dithered images are CC BY-SA derivatives: mark as modified and share alike
- (decision) Reveal: flipping turns a card from dithered sepia into saturated 16-bit colour, intensity scaled by rarity; legendary is the most magnificent
- (decision) Face-down cards are covered in dust; dust thickness = age of the French Wikipedia article, 5 tiers (<1y none, 1-5y light, 5-10y marked, 10-20y thick, >20y buried), frozen at mint
- (decision) Card back carries a Brotherhood seal showing exact rarity: faded ink stamp, dated stamp, red wax, wax+ribbon, golden 'ARCHIVES SCELLÉES — NE PAS OUVRIR'
- (decision) Interaction: hover (desktop) / finger slide (mobile) / keyboard focus wipes dust to reveal the seal; click/tap/Enter flips and blasts remaining dust off in a pixel burst. Double surprise: rarity at dusting, subject at flip
- (decision) Card content is sent to the client only at flip (per-card reveal) so devtools cannot spoil the subject
- (decision) Fonts: open-licensed (OFL) pixel font in repo, must support French accents and stay legible small; pixel font for titles, legible font for body text; a purchased font could only be loaded optionally at deploy time, never committed
- (decision) Collection cards do not tarnish over time (rejected)
