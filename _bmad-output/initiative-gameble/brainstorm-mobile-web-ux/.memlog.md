- (decision) Stance: creative partner; techniques chosen in chat by the coach, no browser composer page
- (technique) started One Feature Only (navigation)
- (idea by user) If only one tab: the capsule (booster). It is the main reason to sign in on mobile: no waiting to get to a desktop to open packs
- (insight by user) A mobile session runs in order: open every capsule until none is left, then check set progress, then social (add friends, trades), finally cosmetics (card backs etc.)
- (idea by coach) Order the bottom bar like the session: Capsule, Sets, Social (friends, trades, feed), Customize; profile/settings reached from the avatar
- (idea by user) The command bridge could be replaced by the capsule screen as the default; open question: where to claim the daily capsule and follow the weekly progress
- (idea by coach) The weekly mission is a collective goal (all players), so it fits the Social tab; the daily capsule shows as a ready slot in the reserve on the capsule screen; when the reserve is empty the capsule screen shows the former hub content (next daily time, sets close to complete)
- (question) Constraint: a GET never writes, so the daily capsule cannot be auto-collected by merely opening the screen; needs a tap or a job
- (decision by user) The daily capsule is collected with a tap, as on desktop; no auto-collect
- (idea by user) When the reserve is empty, propose other things to do on the capsule screen; the idea applies to desktop too
- (idea by user) Empty-capsule screen proposes, in order: collection/sets, then friends/trades, then personalization/achievements
- (idea by coach) Each of the three proposals carries a live hook: sets close to complete, a friend holding a card you need, a newly unlocked cosmetic or achievement
- (technique) started Crazy 8s (phone header)
- (idea by user) Phone header: drop the page title, people know where they are
- (idea by user) Phone header: keep a back button
- (idea by user) Phone header: keep only the G of Gameble as a home button
- (idea by user) Phone header: merge standard and special capsule counters into one, told apart by colour or something else
- (idea by coach) Keep the page title as a visually hidden h1 for screen readers; tell the two capsule kinds apart by shape or icon as well as colour; the combined counter opens a small detail on tap
- (idea by user) The header could almost always disappear as long as the important information stays; maybe only the back button remains
- (idea by coach) Top-level tab screens need no header; only drill-down screens show a floating back button. Reserve count becomes a badge on the Capsule tab; fourth tab 'Me' groups avatar, cosmetics, achievements and settings
- (decision by user) The header stays on the home (top-level) screens; elsewhere it can shrink to a back button
- (idea by coach) On long lists the header hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up, to win back height
- (question) Where do cosmetics, achievements and settings live: a 'Me' tab, or under the header avatar?
- (decision by user) A 'Me' tab is a good idea; the number of hidden things must be limited
- (idea by coach) Four tabs: Capsule, Sets, Social, Me. 'All cards' becomes a view inside Sets. Me shows inline previews (achievement progress, current card back) instead of bare links, and no entry sits deeper than two taps
- (technique) started Cross-Pollination (long card lists)
- (idea by user) Long card list: very smooth infinite pagination plus quick filters
- (insight by coach) Infinite list must restore scroll position when returning from a card detail; art pixelation cost (2-4 jobs at once) bounds smoothness; pages stay server-rendered, appended on demand
- (decision by user) Quick filters on the long list: goal, rarity, state (three pastilles)
- (idea by coach) The goal filter turns the collection into a to-do list; cards missing for a goal that a friend holds lead into trades; each pastille opens a thumb-reach sheet; one row replaces today's stacked search and filters
- (technique) started Backcasting (one-handed trade flow)
- (decision by user) Filters and pickers open as a bottom sheet over the view; only the search stays at the top
- (idea by user) One-handed trade: tap a missing card, a Trade button proposes possible trades with friends who hold it, send it with our side left empty, then wait; on return a notification shows the trade was updated and opens it directly to validate or edit the cards
- (insight by coach) The open request (empty own side) and suggestions from missing cards already exist as designed epics; the mobile work is presentation plus the notification deep link, which a future native push would reuse
- (idea by user) A bell icon opens a popup listing all new notifications, plus an indicator on the interface
- (question) Does the bell popup drop from the top (out of thumb reach) or reuse the bottom sheet grammar?
- (decision by user) Notifications drop down from the top under the bell; notifications are kept on desktop too (shared component)
- (insight by coach) Principle: bottom sheets for choosing (actions), a top dropdown for glancing (read-only notifications)
- (technique) started Reverse Brainstorming (opening packs on a phone)
- (idea by user) Pitfall to invert: having to pick a capsule every single time
- (idea by user) Pitfall to invert: not being able to chain straight into the next capsule
- (idea by user) Pitfall to invert: not seeing the cards you open
- (idea by user) Pitfall to invert: not seeing the whole content of the capsule
- (idea by user) Pitfall to invert: no animation at all
- (idea by user) Pitfall to invert: slow and buggy
- (idea by user) Pitfall to invert: a screen so full of information that you no longer know how to move forward
- (idea by coach) Inversions: next capsule preselected and one tap to open; a Next capsule button chains until the reserve is empty; large readable card; end-of-capsule grid of all cards; short skippable animation honouring reduced motion; warm the next capsule's art during the current reveal; one primary thumb button at the bottom
