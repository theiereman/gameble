# Brotherhood tone

Referenced by Constraints, CAP-12, CAP-13, CAP-23, CAP-24.

- **Community name:** *La Grande Confrérie des Gamblers* (English: the Grand Gamblers' Brotherhood).
- **Discreet:** the Brotherhood speaks only at collective moments: the weekly goal ("objectif communautaire") and the seasonal allocation, at most one line per screen. The feed and every other label carry none.
- **The landing is the exception (CAP-24):** it presents the Brotherhood, its mission (games were lost; the Brotherhood finds them) and its story to come at length. The rules below still hold there; inside the app the Brotherhood stays discreet.
- **Addressing the player:** always "vous", never "tu". Players are not called "Confrères" and get no title ("Confrère", "Capitaine").
- **Register of its lines:** a light mock-solemn touch in the spirit of Helldivers 2, aimed at a community of gamblers, never a nation or country.
- **Where the satire lives:** in those lines only. It never adds a mechanic.
- **Satire target:** the games industry's drift toward investor profit over game quality, kept light.
- **Languages:** the copy carries this tone in both French and English.
- **Hard limits:**
  - No real company names in the copy. Publisher names appear only as card credits and set names; Steam and Valve appear in the no-affiliation notice (legal page, landing footer) and Valve's sign-in image.
  - No Helldivers terms or trademarks ("Ordre majeur", "Super Earth", "Managed Democracy", stratagem arrow codes).
  - No "players are shareholders" theme.
- **Example line (weekly goal):** "Toute la Confrérie ouvre 50 000 boosters, ensemble." (the number is the week's target).
- Microcopy rules and the approved French copy: `EXPERIENCE.md`, Voice and Tone.
