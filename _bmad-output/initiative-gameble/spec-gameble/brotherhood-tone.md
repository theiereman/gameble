# Brotherhood tone

Referenced by Constraints, CAP-12, CAP-13.

- **Community name:** *La Grande Confrérie des Gamblers* (English: the Grand Gamblers' Brotherhood). Players are addressed as "Confrères" ("Brothers").
- **Register:** mock-solemn, over-the-top patriotism in the spirit of Helldivers 2, aimed at a community of gamblers, never a nation or country.
- **Where the satire lives:** in the copy only (confirmation messages, goal announcements, feed lines). It never adds a mechanic.
- **Satire target:** the games industry's drift toward investor profit over game quality, kept light.
- **Languages:** the copy carries this tone in both French and English.
- **Hard limits:**
  - No real company names in the copy. Publisher names appear only as card credits and set names.
  - No Helldivers trademarks (e.g. "Super Earth", "Managed Democracy").
  - No "players are shareholders" theme.
- **Example line:** "Confrère, ta contribution a rapproché la Confrérie de la victoire."
