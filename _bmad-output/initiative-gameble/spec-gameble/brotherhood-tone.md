# Brotherhood tone

Referenced by Constraints, CAP-11, CAP-12, CAP-13, CAP-17, CAP-23, CAP-24, CAP-26. Reference: GitHub issue theiereman/gameble#57, adopted by the author on 2026-10-15; the world is in `lore.md`.

- **Community name:** *La Grande Confrérie des Gameblers* (English: *The Great Brotherhood of Gameblers*).
- **Satire:** an exaggerated satire of capitalism and corporate culture, aimed at large corporations, monopolies, speculation, hidden fees and questionable business practices. It may draw on real situations without naming real companies.
- **Where the humour comes from:** the gap between the Brotherhood's real interests and its official justifications.
- **Register:**
  - Corporate and institutional: professional language, management jargon, HR-style messages, promises of prosperity.
  - Falsely benevolent: every decision is presented as a benefit to members, even when it works against them.
  - Absurd but serious: the Brotherhood never jokes on purpose; it states the most ridiculous things with perfect professionalism.
  - Hypocritical and opportunistic: it may consult members, admit mistakes or announce compensation, and always turns the situation to its advantage.
  - Gambling: it celebrates perseverance and merit, even when chance decides everything.
- **Not satirical all the time:** it speaks like a genuine institution keeping a positive image. Its hypocrisy shows mostly when it justifies economic decisions, its privileges or its failures, so players first meet a welcoming, passionate organization and discover its profit-driven nature over time.
- **Writing rules:**
  - Always "vous", never "tu".
  - Players get no name, rank or form of address in official communications ("Confrère", "Capitaine"). Achievement titles (CAP-17) and profile titles (CAP-22) never address the player.
  - Short, readable, natural texts. Subtle irony over explicit jokes. No long history, dialogue or characters.
  - Both languages keep the humour and the intent rather than a literal translation.
- **Presence:**
  - Secondary: never in the way of navigation or of understanding a mechanic. A functional screen stays plain; not every action needs a reference.
  - Light: collection, capsules, navigation, system messages, hub. Stronger possible: communications from Management, events, achievements, economic announcements, trading and crafting.
  - Text only: no characters, mascots or storylines. An official seal or signature may be considered. A welcome message may come; players start collecting at once.
  - Each integration in the interface is decided in its own issue; this file changes no screen by itself.
- **The landing is the exception (CAP-24):** it presents the Brotherhood, its mission (games were lost; the Brotherhood finds them) and its story at length. The rules above still hold there.
- **Hard limits:**
  - No real company names in the copy. Publisher names appear only as card credits and set names; Steam and Valve appear in the no-affiliation notice (legal page, landing footer) and Valve's sign-in image.
  - No terms, slogans or elements from other games or fictional universes (for example Helldivers: "Ordre majeur", "Super Earth", "Managed Democracy", stratagem arrow codes).
  - No "players are shareholders" theme.
  - No tax or fee before the market (`later-roadmap.md`); #57's examples are tone samples, not copy.
- **Achievements (CAP-17, #66):** each title and flavor text speaks as the Brotherhood; drafts in `achievements.md`.
- **Trade suggestions (CAP-11, #64):** the Brotherhood presents itself as a helpful go-between connecting collectors.
- **Example lines (weekly goal, #61):** "La Confrérie invite ses membres à unir leurs efforts pour ouvrir 50 000 capsules cette semaine." (the number is the week's target); to contributors once it is reached: "La Direction vous remercie pour votre participation."
