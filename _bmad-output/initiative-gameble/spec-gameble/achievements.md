# Achievements

Referenced by CAP-17 and CAP-20. Sources: GitHub issues #66 (achievement list, condition types, renames) and #68 (lazy opener, hardworking), chosen and approved by the author on 2026-10-30. Titles are the working English titles of #66; the final French and English titles and flavor texts follow `brotherhood-tone.md` and land in `EXPERIENCE.md`.

## Rules

- Every achievement has a title in the Brotherhood's tone, a short condition and a flavor text, in French and English. The title never addresses the player.
- An achievement is granted once, when its condition is met, and stays earned.
- A standard capsule is a standard pack (CAP-4), not the daily bonus or the weekly goal reward.
- A condition on games reads the game, whatever edition its copy was minted in.
- A new achievement is granted at its release to every player whose recorded history already meets it; one whose history is not recorded starts counting at the release.
- Achievements reward recognition and, for some, a card back (CAP-20); never a capsule or a currency. They stay light: no quest system or achievement economy.
- The flavor text shows in an achievement's detail or when it is earned, never crowding the list (#66).

## Draft flavor texts (#66)

- Packaging Irregularity: "Our Quality Assurance Department confirms that this is not a mistake, but an additional collecting opportunity."
- Excellence in Mediocrity: "Five perfectly ordinary cards. An extraordinary experience. The Brotherhood thanks you for your enthusiasm."
- Luck Is a Marketing Concept: "The Brotherhood reminds you that every card has value. Some simply have considerably less than others."
- Statistical Non-Compliance: "Our statistical department has requested an examination of your file. Please remain within the solar system."
- Historical Asset Management: "Decades of gaming history, conveniently packaged together. Production costs remain confidential."
- Market Concentration: "You have clearly understood the benefits of market concentration. The Brotherhood approves."
- Diversified Portfolio: "Your ability to recognize a valuable investment is remarkable. Even when making it five times."
- First Claim, All Rights Reserved: "Congratulations on this historic discovery. The Brotherhood naturally reserves all communication rights."
- A Discovery of Ours: "Thank you for sharing our groundbreaking discovery. Your personal contribution has been recorded in the supplementary archives."
- A Strictly Professional Relationship: "The Brotherhood celebrates this lasting relationship, built on trust, mutual interest, and a complete absence of personal feelings."
- Leave Request Denied: "Your exemplary dedication has been noted. Human Resources is reviewing your eligibility for rest days."

The ten renamed achievements and the two #68 ones have no flavor text yet; they are written with the final copy.
