# D1 digest: rotating / daily / weekly item shops and reset-rush avoidance

Round 1, agent 1. Accessed 2026-11-24. 15 tool calls; 8 pages read in full or in part, the rest from search-result summaries (marked).
Several key pages could not be fetched (dotesports Marvel Snap guide: 403; marvel-snap.fandom and genshin-impact.fandom: 402). Facts I believe from training data but could not evidence this run are listed under "Looked for, not found", not as findings.

## Findings

### Warframe: Baro Ki'Teer and Ducats (destroy-to-earn sink, rare visiting trader)

1. **Claim:** Baro Ki'Teer comes every two weeks and trades for up to 48 hours. A countdown on the Ducat kiosks shows time to arrival or departure, a Star Chart icon appears 24 hours before he arrives, and players get an inbox message when he does. First appearance was Update 15.6 (2014-12-11).
   - Source: https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Baro_Ki%27Teer
   - Publisher: Official Warframe Wiki. Date: n.d. (live page). Accessed 2026-11-24.
   - Confidence: high. Class: mechanism.
2. **Claim:** Ducats come only from selling Prime parts and blueprints at always-open kiosks. Selling removes the item, so the currency is minted by destroying collection items. The rate is fixed by drop rarity: Common 15, Uncommon 45, Rare 100, with 25 and 65 for items that were once rarer. Ducats cannot be sold or traded. Prime parts themselves can be traded.
   - Source: https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin_Ducats
   - Publisher: Official Warframe Wiki. Date: n.d. Accessed 2026-11-24.
   - Confidence: high (values). Medium for "destructive": the page implies it but does not state it.
   - Class: mechanism / number.
3. **Claim:** Ducats are spent mainly on Baro's items, plus a few small permanent sinks: an Orbiter decoration, a Conclave sacrifice, Paracesis crafting. The sink runs on a schedule, while the faucet (kiosks) is always open, so currency piles up between visits.
   - Source: https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Orokin_Ducats
   - Publisher: Official Warframe Wiki. Date: n.d. Accessed 2026-11-24.
   - Confidence: high (list). Low for "piles up": my own inference, not stated.
   - Class: mechanism.

### Hearthstone: Arcane Dust (rarity-scaled disenchant/craft pair)

4. **Claim:** Dust values depend only on rarity and golden status. Regular cards craft / disenchant at: Common 40/5, Rare 100/20. Beta-era blog: Epic 400/100, Legendary 1600/400. Disenchanting gives back about 1/8 to 1/4 of the crafting cost, and that gap is the built-in sink.
   - Sources: https://us.battle.net/support/en/article/20456 (Blizzard support, n.d.) and https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/blog/10245930/ (Blizzard blog, beta era, about 2013; the month was not confirmed and the numbers were labelled "not final"). Cross-check: https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Arcane_Dust.
   - Accessed 2026-11-24.
   - Confidence: high for Common/Rare (official support page, seen via search summary). Medium for Epic/Legendary (old blog plus wiki).
   - Class: number.
5. **Claim:** After a balance change to a card, Blizzard sometimes lets players disenchant it for its full crafting value for about two weeks, but only when the patch notes say so.
   - Source: https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/hearthstone-arcane-dust-how-do-you-get-it-fast
   - Publisher: playnews.gg (third party). Date: n.d. Accessed 2026-11-24.
   - Confidence: medium. Class: mechanism.

### Marvel Snap: Token Shop / Card Shop

6. **Claim:** March 2023: Series 3 cards left the Token Shop for a separate "choose your card" section, one free pick per month. The Token Shop then held only Series 4, Series 5 and Ultimate Variants. Players not yet done with Series 3 earned 4x more tokens. A known issue mentions the screen being blank "when the shop rolls", which confirms the shop rolls on a timer.
   - Source: https://marvelsnap.com/?p=90212 (official patch notes, 2023-03). Accessed 2026-11-24.
   - Confidence: high. Class: mechanism.
7. **Claim:** April 2023: a Weekly Spotlight section was added. A new Series 5 card is featured in its own section for its first week.
   - Source: https://marvelsnap.com/?p=90197 (official patch notes, 2023-04), seen only through a search-result summary.
   - Accessed 2026-11-24.
   - Confidence: medium. Class: mechanism.
8. **Claim:** April 2025: the Token Shop became a "Card Shop". It added Snap Packs (each guarantees one unowned card with no duplicates, plus two bonus rewards), token packs bought with Gold, and daily login tokens. "Spotlight & Rotating Pinnable Cards" stay at the same token prices. A "Daily Cards" section has three pinnable cards on a refresh timer. The cadence and whether the shop is per player are not stated.
   - Source: https://marvelsnap.com/?p=91009 (official patch notes, 2025-04). Accessed 2026-11-24.
   - Confidence: high (what is stated). Class: mechanism.

### Fortnite: Item Shop

9. **Claim:** Epic's official help page says the shop "constantly rotates Items" and tells players to "check back frequently, as there is a good chance you can buy it later". Battle Pass items released after 2024-08-07 may come to the shop at least 18 months after the pass ends, "but there is no guarantee."
   - Source: https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/c-33726977/when-will-items-return-to-the-fortnite-shop-a20534496
   - Publisher: Epic Games. Date: n.d. (after 2024-08). Accessed 2026-11-24.
   - Confidence: medium (seen via search summary). Class: mechanism.
10. **Claim:** Critics say the 18-month, no-guarantee return still leaves Epic "benefit[ing] from FOMO".
    - Source: https://digitaltrends.com/?p=3665536 (Digital Trends, about 2024-08). Accessed 2026-11-24.
    - Confidence: medium. Class: sentiment.
11. **Claim:** Epic was reported to add a per-item timer showing when each cosmetic leaves the shop, which press read as easing FOMO and impulse buying. Earlier, removing the single global refresh countdown (refresh "usually" at 7pm ET) drew complaints that Epic could change refresh times without warning.
    - Sources: https://www.videogamer.com/news/fortnite-seems-set-to-make-the-biggest-change-to-the-item-shop-yet and https://www.videogamer.com/news/fortnite-item-shop-massive-change
    - Publisher: VideoGamer. Date: n.d. Accessed 2026-11-24.
    - Confidence: low-medium (press report of a planned change; I did not verify that it shipped). Class: mechanism / sentiment.

### Genshin Impact: Paimon's Bargains

12. **Claim:** The shop resets on the first of each month at server reset (reported as 4:00 UTC+8 for one region), not at calendar midnight. That rolls the featured 4-star characters and weapons and the monthly purchase limits. Its two currencies, Masterless Starglitter and Stardust, come mostly from duplicate pulls: another destroy-to-earn faucet. Rotations repeat on a known schedule, so players can plan months ahead.
    - Sources: https://gamewith.net/genshin-impact/article/show/22383 (GameWith, n.d.) and https://buffget.com/news/genshin-impact-starglitter-shop-2026-best-characters-guide (Buffget, 2026, a top-up seller).
    - Accessed 2026-11-24.
    - Confidence: medium for the monthly reset and limits. Low for the exact hour and the per-item limits. Seen via search summary only; the official wiki was blocked.
    - Class: mechanism.

### Path of Exile 2: vendors

13. **Claim:** PoE2 vendors refresh part of their stock each time the player levels up. The shop is per player and triggered by progress, not by the clock, so there is no shared reset moment. A one-time vendor (the Ferryman) never refreshes. Kirac's map vendor charges gold to refresh, a cost that rises with progress and caps at 10,000 gold (guide, not official).
    - Sources: https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Vendor (community wiki, n.d.) and https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/gold-explained (Mobalytics, n.d.).
    - Accessed 2026-11-24.
    - Confidence: medium (level-up refresh). Low (gold refresh cost).
    - Class: mechanism.
14. **Claim:** In PoE1, a player recalls that regular vendors "used to refresh every level up, this has been changed".
    - Source: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3444679
    - Publisher: official forum, user post. Date: n.d. Accessed 2026-11-24.
    - Confidence: low. Class: sentiment.

### Shared global limited stock, first come first served: Neopets restocking

15. **Claim:** In Neopets NPC shops, item rarity sets how often an item is in stock, and players compete to grab rare restocks: "In a Neopets restock war, milliseconds matter." The author calls it "high-frequency trading... performed by millions of children" and argues that scalping "is actually good for the game", because it gives new players a way to earn.
    - Source: https://www.alexirpan.com/2018/11/10/neopets-economy.html
    - Publisher: Alex Irpan (blog). Date: 2018-11. Accessed 2026-11-24.
    - Confidence: medium. Class: outcome / sentiment.
16. **Claim:** Shops reportedly restock about every 8 minutes. Neopets' own news says bots and third-party programs are banned, and its 2025 task force names anti-bot work a priority. A user forum post says autobuyers are "still rampant" despite a 5-second rule. A fan guide warns against running an autobuyer for more than 8 hours at a stretch.
    - Sources: https://portal.neopets.com/news/may7-neopian-task-force-play-fair (Neopets official, 2025-05); https://forum.pinkpt.com/viewtopic.php?p=566151 (forum, n.d.); https://maxrsguide.carrd.co/ (fan guide, n.d.).
    - Accessed 2026-11-24.
    - Confidence: medium for the official ban and priority. Low for "rampant" (sentiment).
    - Class: outcome / sentiment.
