# Cards drift over the landing

## Description

Adds the few cards passing over the content faster than the scroll on desktop with a fine pointer and room in both gutters, draggable aside until reload, faded while focus is in the content, hidden from assistive technology, absent on the phone and under reduced motion.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: On a wide desktop the cards pass over the content and can be dragged aside, they never show on a phone, a narrow window or under reduced motion, and tabbing through the content fades them.
