# The lamp finds the lost games

## Description

Builds the hero's lamp field: cards under a veil, the lamp following a fine pointer and sweeping on its own on touch, the found-games count, the veil moved by translate3d, its maths in a tested pure module, and its reduced-motion form.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: With a mouse the lamp follows the pointer and sweeps after it stops, on a phone it sweeps, the count stops once every card is found, and under reduced motion the lamp jumps to the pointer with nothing animating.
