## Intent

**Problem:** A visitor cannot ask for reduced motion before signing in, and a sign-in does not carry the choice to the account.

**Approach:** The landing's top bar shows the « Animations réduites » switch to visitors; it POSTs to the motion cookie the server already reads from the first paint, and a Steam sign-in with the switch on sets the account's reduced motion to on, leaving it untouched when off.

</frozen-after-approval>

## Code Map

- `app/controllers/motions_controller.rb`, `app/policies/motion_policy.rb`, `config/routes.rb` -- `POST /:locale/motion` (`reduced=on|off`), visitors only, writes the cookie through `write_motion_cookie`, 303 to the landing.
- `app/views/shell/_top_bar.html.erb`, `app/frontend/styles/shell.css`, `config/locales/{en,fr}.yml` -- the `aria-pressed` toggle button in a Turbo-disabled form so `html[data-motion]` reloads whole.
- `app/controllers/sessions_controller.rb` -- `carry_visitor_motion_switch` calls `Accounts::UpdateSettings` when the cookie is `reduced`.

- Given the switch on, when the visitor signs in, then the account's reduced motion is on; given it off, the account keeps its setting.
