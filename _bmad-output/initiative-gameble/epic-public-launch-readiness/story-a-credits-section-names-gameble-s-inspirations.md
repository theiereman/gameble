# A credits section names gameble's inspirations

## Description

A credits section, reachable from the app, names every inspiration recorded in the planning documents and the contributors to the lore, each with what it inspired, worded as inspiration and never as affiliation, in French and English.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: Every inspiration listed in the planning documents (a sweep of the brief, the forge, the spec, the UX spines and their logs) appears with what it inspired, rejected references excluded; the section is reached from where the author chose; the no-affiliation notice still holds and the copy respects the tone's limit on company names.
