# Walk through every screen with the author

## Description

In one complete session, the author and an agent walk through every screen of the Information Architecture (signed out, as a player, as staff; French and English; phone and desktop), list what works and what does not on each, and turn each finding into a ticket before the release, a deferred-work entry, or a decision to keep it.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verify: The session's findings file lists every screen of the Information Architecture as walked through, and every finding carries the author's decision and, when it is to be built before the release, the id of its ticket.
