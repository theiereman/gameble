# Duel stats and decks

## Description

Every card gets a duel profile: five stats, each a percentile computed from the catalog and from what the Brotherhood does, and a trump, shown on the card's detail and moving each Monday, when a feed entry names the cards that moved most. Players build up to five named decks of twelve cards from a new DUELS tab, with an editor that sorts and filters the collection by stat and trump, shows what a deck covers and what moved since last Monday. A card in a deck blocks one copy of its edition, which the trade, sale and entrust screens name and can free. ÉCHANGES takes the market as a sub-tab beside the friend trades, which frees the fifth phone tab for DUELS. No duel is played yet: epic 43 adds live duels on top of these decks and stats.

## Outcome

For the players: a reason to look at their cards again, through a profile that changes each week, and decks ready to duel the day epic 43 lands. For the release: the stats table, the decks and the copy block that live duels read, on staging.

## Requirements

- R1 — `game_duel_stats` holds one row per card-season with a rarity of every published regular season: for each of the five stats its exact value, its percentile (midrank, one decimal) and its distance from the median; the trump, the card's most extreme stat in either direction; the percentiles of the last weekly run; `computed_at`. Tribute and unrated card-seasons have no row. Rarity and market price are not stats. (CAP-39; `duel-rules.md`, Stats; AD-53)
- R2 — The measures: popularity is the edition's season review count and release its release date, each ranked over the edition's season catalog; rating is `Catalog`'s whole percent positive, the review count breaking an equal percent; discovery is the first pull's stored time in `game_first_discoveries`, ranked among the season's pulled editions; circulation is the count of distinct holders with a positive quantity, the Archives excluded, ranked among the held editions of its rarity tier and season. An edition nobody has pulled has no discovery or circulation value. (CAP-39; `duel-rules.md`, Stats; AD-53)
- R3 — `Game::ComputeDuelStats`, an `ApplicationOperation`, replaces every row in one transaction and publishes `Game::DuelStatsComputed` with its reason (first, season, weekly); `Game::ComputeDuelStatsJob` runs it one at a time. It runs as a data step of the deploy when the table is empty, from the `Catalog::SeasonPublished` handler, which enqueues the job after commit, and every Monday at 00:00 UTC from the `development` and `production` blocks of `config/recurring.yml`. Only the first and the weekly runs rotate the last weekly percentiles. (CAP-39; AD-12, AD-53)
- R4 — One query reads the table for the card detail, the deck editor, and later the duel snapshot and the automatic moves; nothing else computes a percentile. (AD-53)
- R5 — The card detail shows, after ÉDITIONS, a DUEL block for the edition its "Prix moyen" reads: five rows, each the stat's name, a 0–100 bar toward the stat's first direction, the percentile in words naming the card's stronger side and the raw value; the trump's row marked "ATOUT"; "Valeurs du <date>". Every viewer sees it, a visitor and a non-owner included; the public card page and the card face do not. The bars are hidden from assistive technology; the words carry the value. (CAP-39; `duel-rules.md`, Decks; EXPERIENCE.md, Duels, Duel profile, and Collection, Card detail; DESIGN.md, Duels, Duel profile; Copy deck, Duel stats; Écrans des Duels board 9)
- R6 — Each Monday one Brotherhood feed entry with no player and no card image, "Le cours des cartes a bougé.", names the three largest rises and the three largest falls, a card's move being its largest change of one stat's percentile since the previous weekly run (the first Monday compares with the deploy's run), each card linked to its page with the stat and both percentiles in words. It shows under TOUS and the TOUT filter only, never in the ticker. The entry has the kind `duel_stats`, `player_id` made nullable for that kind only (expand/contract), the moved cards stored on it, one per week by a unique index, and is made by the feed handler from a weekly `DuelStatsComputed`; the feed queries know the kind. (CAP-39; AD-13, AD-53 Costs; EXPERIENCE.md, Duels, Monday stats entry; DESIGN.md, Duels, Monday stats entry)
- R7 — Decks live in `Game`: `game_decks` (token, player, name of 1 to 24 characters) and `game_deck_cards` (deck, card-season, the card-season's card, slot 1 to 12), unique on deck and slot and on deck and card, so a game appears once whatever its edition; database checks hold the name length and the slot range; at most five decks per player. A deck and its contents are seen only by its owner. (CAP-40; AD-52)
- R8 — A deck holds exactly 12 cards the player owns to duel; a shorter deck is a draft. No rarity or theme constraint. Each editor change is one `Game` operation taking `player:`, under the player's bank lock, in AD-10's lock order (decks and their cards before holdings), refusing with a code. (CAP-40; `duel-rules.md`, Decks; AD-10, AD-52)
- R9 — An edition in at least one of the player's decks holds exactly 1 of its holding's `reserved`, whatever the number of decks: raised when it enters the first deck, lowered when it leaves the last. An edition already in another deck needs no free copy; any other needs one (`Failure(:no_free_copy)`), and the editor shows a fully reserved card with the Reserved tag. An edition with no stats yet cannot enter a deck (`Failure(:not_yet_ranked)`) and shows "Stats lundi" in place of its trump. Deleting a deck frees only the copies this deck alone blocked. (CAP-40; `duel-rules.md`, Stats and Decks; AD-52, AD-53)
- R10 — The deck editor: the deck's name (1 to 24 characters, saved on blur, refused inline when empty, too long or already used by the player, the previous name kept); 12 slots; the player's collection, one entry per owned edition with its season beside its trump. Choosing a card puts it in the first empty slot; a card in the deck shows "DANS LE DECK" and choosing it takes it out; another edition of a game already there and a full deck are refused with their lines; each filled slot is a button that takes its card out. Each change saves at once; a failed save stays on screen, posts "Enregistrement impossible, réessayez." and is retried with the next change. "SUPPRIMER LE DECK" asks a confirmation. On the phone a line under the top bar keeps the name and "<n> / 12" in view. The collection is one tab stop with arrow keys, each card a toggle button; status lines and focus follow the UX. (CAP-40; EXPERIENCE.md, Duels, Deck editor; State Patterns, Draft deck, Deck name refused, Five decks; Focus after each step; Accessibility Floor, Duels; DESIGN.md, Duels, Deck editor; Copy deck, Decks; Écrans des Duels board 8)
- R11 — The editor sorts the collection by any stat in either direction and filters it by trump. Each deck card shows a text arrow on each stat that moved by 5 points or more since the last weekly run, hidden from assistive technology, with a visually hidden sentence ending the card's name. Above the slots, the coverage strip "COUVERTURE" lists the ten calls (five stats, two directions) with the deck's best percentile in each, a call with no card at 90 or above marked "Manque"; choosing a gap sorts the collection by that call. "COMPLÉTER LES MANQUES" fills the empty slots with the player's best free card for each gap in turn, then the best remaining trumps. One object, `Game::DeckCoverage`, gives the strip and the fill. (CAP-40; AD-52; EXPERIENCE.md, Duels, Deck editor; Accessibility Floor, Duels; DESIGN.md, Duels, Deck editor and Duel profile)
- R12 — `Game::ReservedCopies` splits `reserved` into in a deck (1 when the edition is in a deck), on sale and in trade. The Reserved tag reads "Dans un deck"; on the card picker, Confier and Mettre en vente the owner sees the decks named ("Dans : <decks>", `Game::DecksHolding`) and "RETIRER DES DECKS", which takes the edition out of every deck after a confirmation (`Game::RemoveFromDecks`). On a friend's side a deck-blocked copy is unavailable and never offered; nobody else sees deck names. Trade and market releases subtract only what they reserved, so they never free a deck block; going private leaves decks untouched. (CAP-40; `duel-rules.md`, Decks; AD-52; EXPERIENCE.md, Échanges, Reserved tag; State Patterns, Card blocked by a deck; Flow 11, failures)
- R13 — Spare copies (free copies beyond one per game) come from one function, used by trade suggestions and the market desk alike, which counts a deck-blocked copy as reserved. (CAP-40; AD-52 Costs)
- R14 — The DUELS tab: the phone's five tabs are PONT · CAPSULE · SETS · ÉCHANGES · DUELS, DUELS with the crossed-blades icon exported by `script/pixel_art/export.mjs` from the grid in DESIGN.md; the desktop hub menu reads 03 ÉCHANGES and 04 DUELS, in MARCHÉ's place; the native apps' five tabs follow. ÉCHANGES holds two sub-tabs, ENTRE AMIS at the trade list's address and MARCHÉ at the market's, links in a `nav` named "Échanges", so every link to the market keeps working; it opens on the sub-tab the player last used, kept per player, ENTRE AMIS the first time. The ÉCHANGES tab and menu 03 carry the sum of the trades waiting and the auctions needing the player, each sub-tab its own, named per the Copy deck. The DUELS tab is `aria-current="true"` over the deck editor. A visitor keeps two tabs and sees the market alone, with no sub-tab bar. (CAP-40; EXPERIENCE.md, Information Architecture rows Échanges, Marché and Duels; Desktop and Phone navigation; Chrome, Count badge and Tab bar; Native apps; Accessibility Floor, Duels; Focus after each step; DESIGN.md, Tab bar (phone), Special capsules (Duel pixel grids); Copy deck, Duel and Échanges badges; Navigation des Duels boards A1 and A2)
- R15 — The Duels screen is the DUELS tab's root, with no back. In this epic it holds "MES DECKS · <n> / 5": up to five deck rows (the name, "<n> / 12" for a draft, the twelve covers as thumbnails hidden from assistive technology, the row one link named "<deck>, 12 cartes" or "<deck>, brouillon, 9 sur 12"), "+ NOUVEAU DECK" while the player has fewer than five, the five-deck line at five, and "+ NOUVEAU DECK" alone with no deck. The deck editor shows "◀ DUELS" when opened from it. On desktop the screen sits in a 1120px column, the decks in the wide column three per row, each with its cards in a 4 × 3 grid. (CAP-40; EXPERIENCE.md, Duels, Duels screen and Deck list; State Patterns, No deck and Five decks; DESIGN.md, Duels, Duels screen, Deck list and Desktop; Écrans des Duels boards 1 and 11)
- R16 — The player's export lists their decks and their cards; deleting the account drops them. (CAP-40; spec Constraints, Market and personal data; AD-14)
- R17 — Every action has a Pundit policy; decks are named by tokens, never ids; each failure code has its `errors.game.<code>` key in both locales; duel labels are written in sentence case in the locale files and set in capitals by CSS. (AD-14, AD-15, AD-18; Accessibility Floor, language)

## Done when

1. On staging, every card's detail, for a player and for a visitor, shows the five duel stats, the trump and the date of the values; the values change only after a Monday run or a season publication.
2. On staging, a player builds and names a 12-card deck from the DUELS tab, sorted and filtered by stat and trump, with the coverage strip and the week's arrows; a thirteenth card, a second edition of one game, a sixth deck and an edition with no stats yet are refused.
3. A deck card's copy cannot be traded, listed or entrusted: the card picker, Confier and Mettre en vente name its decks and offer to take it out of them, while the player's other copies stay free.
4. The first Monday after the deploy, the feed carries one "Le cours des cartes a bougé." entry naming three rises and three falls.
5. ÉCHANGES opens on the sub-tab last used, and every former market link still opens the market; a visitor's navigation is unchanged.

## Boundaries

Stats, decks and navigation only. No challenge, duel, presence, record, capsule or achievement: epic 43 adds the challenges, live duels and their parts of the Duels screen (DÉFIER UN AMI, EN LIGNE, a duel in progress), epic 44 the duel capsule, the record, DERNIERS DUELS and the duel achievements. Submitting the native apps to the stores with their new tab is the author's, outside this epic. No ranked or deferred duel (spec Non-goals).
