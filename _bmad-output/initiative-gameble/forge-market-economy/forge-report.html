Market and economy (V2)

An auction house whose only money is earned by entrusting cards to the Brotherhood, tuned for a standard player opening 40 to 50 capsules a day.

Outcome

The idea survived and is specific enough to plan. The simulation (365 days, about 2,000 players, assumed behaviour) checked the money and the prices. It does not model the sale format, so the auction rules rest on research and judgment.

Targets and starting values

Prices are promised in days of play, for a standard player. All values are adjustable from the back-office.

Locked decisions

Money

The PiCoin, minted by the 100-6-Tron, Réacteur des Briétés, only when a card is entrusted to the Archives. Never bought or withdrawn. Sinks: listing stamp and 10% commission. Amounts are integers shown compact (1.2M), with no euro sign.

Entrusting

Any copy, by multi-select batch, one validation. Recap flags only copies from Rare up, without blocking. About 1.2M a day per account.

Selling

Any copy, any edition. Minimum price per rarity, never a sale at a loss. Legendary minimum starts at 7M.

Friend trades

Free of stamp, commission, floor and levy, always.

Format

Auction with optional buy-now, lasting 12h, 24h or 3 days. Soft close of 3 minutes when the price or leader changes.

My maximum

Displayed price is the second-highest maximum plus a 5% increment, computed once per bid. Never a chain of automatic bids.

Escrow

The whole maximum is held until outbid. The winner pays the final price and gets the surplus back.

Bid history

Player names always visible, one line per bid. The leader's maximum stays secret.

Average price

Per exact edition. Last 5 sales in 90 days, highest and lowest dropped.

Alt accounts

Back-office detection plus the daily cap. No seniority gate, no limit on trades.

Back-office control

Every setting editable with no deployment: bounded, traced, new listings only, announced by the Brotherhood.

Regulation

Manual with alerts. Each indicator explains itself in plain language and proposes an action for the author to validate.

Weak points that survived

  • Unsold Legendaries: about 1,800 listings waiting at 30% resellers, 3 months of sales. Accepted, since higher minimums clog the market.
  • The Legendary price depends on resale: 25 days with no minimum and 30% resellers, about 4 days at 60%.
  • Epic resellers were not simulated. Watch the indicators.
  • The alert threshold around 40% is a guess until real data exists.
  • Alt accounts: no study measures how well any countermeasure works. A seller's alt can probe the leader's maximum, at the cost of commission and stamp if it wins.
  • Visible names let anyone follow a player's purchases and let friends avoid bidding against each other.
  • Legal: ban real-money trading, publish odds, sell nothing affecting capsules, currency or auctions.
