The duels alpha and its place in the interface

Ship duels to every player as an alpha that tests the rules, and decide where they live in the app without burying the market or the friend-trade flow.

Locked decisions

  • Scope: open alpha for everyone, labelled ALPHA, flag removed. Live friend challenges and the practice duel; no capsule, achievements or Duelliste back. Async goes to the beta.
  • Purpose: is the duel fun, and do players build varied decks. A test bench for the rules: one dominant deck means the rules change.
  • Bot: skilled, margin-aware play with a named archetype deck that changes each game.
  • Measurement: a back-office page, human and practice duels apart; the bot's archetype and the rules version stored per duel; the ALPHA banner says games are analysed.
  • Navigation E′: PONT · CAPSULE · SETS · ÉCHANGES · DUELS (ALPHA tag). MARCHÉ first and default in ÉCHANGES; players with a friend trade in 30 days keep ENTRE AMIS. Native apps get DUELS back.
  • Record: off the public profile, on the player's own Duels screen, current rules only.
  • Card detail and feed: the duel profile stays for everyone below the collection facts; the Monday standing entry ships as built.

Weak points that survived

  • A skilled bot may teach everyone the coverage deck; read decks built against the bot apart from decks built against friends.
  • About 30 active players: a few dozen human duels; archetype win rates come mostly from practice.
  • Live duels need two friends online at once until the beta.
  • Removing the rewards is migration work on built tables.
