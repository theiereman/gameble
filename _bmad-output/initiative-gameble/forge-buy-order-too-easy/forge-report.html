Buy orders that keep players talking

Is the buy order with instant sale and instant buy too easy? Tested and amended.

Outcome

Yes, the shipped design was too easy for the cards that matter: PiCoins and one press filled any gap, and anonymous orders replaced the trades where players talk. The market now splits by rarity. Common and Uncommon sell through orders, Rare and above through auctions backed by orders, and the shop is capped above Uncommon.

Locked decisions

  • Protect trades that need talk, not trade volume.
  • Common and Uncommon: sell only to orders, never at auction. An order can target one precise edition.
  • Rare and above: sell only at auction. An order is a signal with its price held, and a seller can open an auction backed by it.
  • Order-backed auction: every order reaching the rarity becomes a bid with its price as its maximum. Money is never held twice.
  • Archive shop: a per-player cap above Uncommon (for example 1 Rare a day, 1 Epic a week), set in the back-office.

Rejected

OptionWhy
Block the order for the last card of a setThe rarity split covers it; blocking it hurts the player who needs that card most
Direct sale for every rarity (today)Money and one press replace the talk for the cards that matter
Rare and above: order removed, or only an alertBuyer has no credible way to show demand
Only the best order bids, the others stay openMoney held twice, and a second order snipes at the last minute
Shop closed above UncommonRemoves the main PiCoin sink
Shop unchangedMoney bypasses every other rule

Weak points that survived

  • A seller's alt can back an auction with a fake high order. The alt signals must cover it.
  • Order-backed auctions are automatic bidding, though limited.
  • Edition orders for Commons are untested at about 30 players.
  • This reworks a design already in 1.4.0-rc.2.

To forge separately: automatic friend trades (4 duplicates for 4) and trade suggestions.
