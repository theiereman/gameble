Card series that mean something

Outcome: hardened. Tomes and the set list are replaced by recognizable series, a series-first collection and tiered rewards. Forged idea: forge-card-series.md.

Locked decisions

  • Every card belongs to at least one series; recognition, not size, decides whether a series exists.
  • Families: Theme (backbone, ~30 curated Steam tags, tiered), Franchise and Studio (small, finishable), Hall of fame from review stats (tiered).
  • A game joins every listed theme among its 5 most-voted tags; one card can advance several series. Games with none join the UFOs.
  • Bronze / silver / gold tiers keep large series motivating without volumes.
  • The collection opens on series: "In progress", then "To discover", then search. A series is an album page with empty slots.
  • The full filtered grid stays as a secondary view, for trades and sales later.

Rejected, and why

  • Tomes, publisher sets, best rated of the year: unrecognizable or absorbed by better families.
  • Era series: too vague. Editorial series: stay V3.
  • One theme per game: kills the "three series at once" moment. All 20 tags: fuzzy borders.
  • A fixed list of major series: today's 30 rows with 25 empty. A purely personal list: empty for a new player.
  • A card back per completed set: hundreds of backs to draw.

Weak points that survived

  • Steam's franchises field may be dirty or tiny; minimum series size to set on real data.
  • The UFOs must stay under ~5% of the catalog; if not, fix the theme list, not the rule.
  • Generated titles must use a no-agreement template, or they read badly in French.
  • Still to tune: theme list, hall of fame thresholds, tier percentages, link with seasons.
  • Touches built work: Catalog::SetBuilder, library screens, the card back rule of the spec.
