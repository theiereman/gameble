# Reality check: market volume update (AD-58 to AD-63, amendments of 2026-11-20)

Lens: does every committed decision rest on code, schema and platform behaviour that exist with the shape the spine claims? Read against `develop` at 9fcc3a25 plus the uncommitted spine diff, `db/schema.rb`, `app/domains/game/...`, `config/recurring.yml`, `config/initializers/*`, and the tag `v1.3.7`.

Versions in use: Ruby 4.0.7, Rails / Active Record 8.1.4.1, pg 1.7.0, strong_migrations 2.8.0, rails_event_store 3.2.0, solid_queue 1.7.2, Postgres 18 (`compose.yaml`, `config/deploy.yml`, both CI workflows).

## Verdict

**Mostly grounded, with two stale production premises.** Almost every existing name the update leans on exists with the claimed shape, and the Rails and Postgres behaviour it relies on holds in these versions. The exceptions are the slot-grant data migration in AD-62, which rests on a stale picture of production for both custom sets and decks, and a handful of existing code paths the Costs lines do not name. Without those, a builder would ship a silent regression: nightly reconciliation mismatches, a second "wanted" definition, and tie rows left behind after an account is deleted. Fix the two high findings before AD-62 is sliced. The rest can go into the tickets.

## Findings

- **[high]** The custom-set free limit of 5 is a cut from the limit live in production, so "one custom-set slot to the player who held six" is a snapshot, not a rule (AD-62, "A data migration grants free slots…"; memlog decision AD-62). `Game::CustomSet::PER_PLAYER = 10` is in `app/domains/game/app/models/game/custom_set.rb:10` and in the shipped tag (`git show v1.3.7:…/custom_set.rb`). `create_custom_set.rb:25` lets any player create up to 10 until the release ships, so by deploy time any number of players may hold 6 to 10 sets. A migration hard-coded to one slot for one player leaves them over a limit of 5. Then `BuySlot`'s "a slot other than the current limit plus one" charges them for slot 6 while they already hold 8. *Fix:* state the grant as computed at migration time: `bought = clamp(count - 5, 0, 5)` per player for `custom_set`, and likewise for any kind. Say that a player who is over the limit after the grant cannot happen, because the ceiling is 10 and the old limit was 10. Drop the "player who held six" wording from the Rule.

- **[high]** "Decks are not in production (release 1.4 unreleased)… no player ever holds more than the free three" is out of date (AD-62, last Rule bullet; memlog "Release 1.4 ships decks with a limit of three"). Decks shipped in `v1.3.7` (tagged 2026-11-20) with `Game::Deck::PER_PLAYER = 5` (`app/domains/game/app/models/game/deck.rb:7`; `git tag --contains b30ba0b7` → v1.3.7). They sit behind `require_feature :duels` (`app/controllers/decks_controller.rb:2`). That flag is a database row (`Game::EnabledFeatures` reads `FeatureFlag.where(enabled: true)`), which staff can switch on in production at any time with no deploy. Once it is on, players can hold 4 or 5 decks before `PER_PLAYER` drops to 3. *Fix:* the grant migration also computes `deck` grants (`clamp(count - 3, 0, 2)`) rather than asserting none, or the spine records a guard, for example: `PER_PLAYER` becomes 3 before the `duels` flag is enabled in production, enforced by a ticket ahead of the flag. Also update the memory note that says the latest tag is v1.3.1.

- **[medium]** `SumArchiveStocks` is a rewrite, and the stock table's check constrains how it is built (AD-43 amendment; AD-60). Today it is one `INSERT … SELECT SUM … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE` with no row locks, plus an orphan `DELETE` keyed on "no deposit exists" (`app/domains/game/app/operations/game/market/sum_archive_stocks.rb`). `game_market_archive_stocks_quantity_check` is `quantity > 0` (`db/schema.rb`). Postgres CHECK constraints are never deferrable, so an upsert that writes `deposits − withdrawals = 0`, or a `BuyFromArchives` that decrements to 0 before deleting, fails the statement. The orphan delete no longer fits once withdrawals exist. AD-43's Costs line does not mention any of this. *Fix:* add to the Costs: `SumArchiveStocks` locks `ArchiveStock.order(:card_season_id).lock`, upserts only `HAVING SUM(deposits) − SUM(withdrawals) > 0`, and deletes the rows whose net is ≤ 0. `BuyFromArchives` deletes the row when `quantity = 1` and decrements otherwise, never updating to 0.

- **[medium]** The destroyed kinds are listed in two places, and the Costs name neither (AD-41 amendment "the sum of the deltas equals minted minus … Archive purchases and slot purchases"; AD-48 amendment). They are `Game::Market::DailyFigures::DESTROYING_KINDS = %w[stamp commission forfeit]` (`queries/game/market/daily_figures.rb:5`) and a SQL literal `kind IN ('stamp', 'commission', 'forfeit')` (`queries/game/market/reconcile_wallets.rb:25`). If a builder updates only the snapshot, `ReconcileWalletsJob` reports a `CirculationMismatch` every night once a slot or Archive purchase exists. *Fix:* name both in AD-41's Costs, or make the reconciliation read the one constant.

- **[medium]** A second definition of "wanted" already lives in the web layer, and AD-61 does not retire it (AD-61 Prevents "two definitions of a wanted card"; Costs name only `WantingPlayers`). `MarketsController#wanted_card_ids` builds the market's "wanted" filter and its count from `goal_marks.needs.flat_map(&:missing_card_ids)` (`app/controllers/markets_controller.rb:40-47`, `ListingFilters.wanted`). That is goal-only and blind to orders. *Fix:* add to AD-61's Costs that the market's wanted filter and count read the viewer's own `game_market_wanted` rows, or record that the filter stays goal-only on purpose.

- **[medium]** Tied-account rows outlive a deleted account (AD-46 amendment; AD-63). `ReleasePlayer` deletes every `AltSignal` where the player is `account_id` or `other_account_id` (`operations/game/release_player.rb:11`), and `AltSignals.without_deleted_accounts` drops deleted accounts from each refresh (`queries/game/market/alt_signals.rb`). The amended `ReleasePlayer` bullet lists notices, wanted rows and capacity rows, but not `game_market_tied_accounts`. As written, staff data naming a deleted account stays until the next nightly refresh, and only if the refresh also filters deleted accounts. *Fix:* add `game_market_tied_accounts` (both sides of the pair) to `ReleasePlayer`'s deletions in AD-46, and say that the tie refresh applies `without_deleted_accounts`.

- **[medium]** The season-publication path makes a job write wanted rows directly (AD-61, "`Catalog::SeasonPublished`'s handler enqueues after commit a job that runs it once per player … under the bank lock, as `HealBanksJob` does"). `HealBanksJob` never writes: it calls `HealBank` / `FillBank` operations, which take the bank lock (`jobs/game/heal_banks_job.rb:18-19`). `RefreshWanted.call(player)` is described as a plain module function, not an operation. A job that opens a transaction, takes `BankLock` and calls it breaks the repo rule that jobs write only through an operation. *Fix:* name an operation for the job path (for example `Game::Market::RefreshPlayerWanted < ApplicationOperation`, bank lock then `RefreshWanted`, publishing nothing like the housekeeping writes). Handlers keep calling `RefreshWanted` directly, as they call `Notice.record` today.

- **[low]** `:not_enough_picoins` is a new code for a refusal the market already names (AD-58 `PlaceOrder`, AD-60, AD-62). `PlaceBid` answers `Failure(:more_than_available)` (`operations/game/market/place_bid.rb:55`) and `ListCard` answers `:stamp_not_covered`. No `not_enough_picoins` exists anywhere in `app/`. *Fix:* reuse `:more_than_available`, or note that both `en.yml` and `fr.yml` gain the hand-written `errors.game.not_enough_picoins` key.

- **[low]** "A published regular season" has no counterpart in the schema, and the refusal has no code (AD-58 `PlaceOrder`). `catalog_seasons` has only `status` (draft/published), with no kind. `Catalog::PublishedCardIds` reads card-seasons with a rarity in any published season (`catalog/app/queries/catalog/published_card_ids.rb:30`). *Fix:* drop "regular", and name the failure code (for example `:card_not_published`).

- **[low]** The supporting indexes and the foreign-key sequencing are not stated. The orders table needs indexes like the listings' `index_game_market_listings_open_ends_at` and `index_game_market_listings_sold_card_season`: a partial index on `expires_at` where `status = 'open'` for `ExpireDueOrdersJob`, and one on `(served_card_season_id, served_at)` where `status = 'served'` for `EditionPrice.sales_in_window` / `EditionSales`. The `order_id` foreign keys on the existing `game_market_ledger_entries`, `game_market_notices` and `game_feed_entries` tables must be added with `validate: false` and then validated (strong_migrations 2.8 flags `add_reference … foreign_key: true`), and their indexes built concurrently. *Fix:* one Costs line in AD-58 and AD-41.

- **[low]** Each swapped check refuses the new rows until its old version is dropped (AD-45 Costs "its check swapped expand/contract"; AD-44; AD-41). `game_feed_entries_sale_check` is `(kind = 'sale') = (listing_id IS NOT NULL)`, so an order sale entry with a null `listing_id` is refused while the old check stands. The same holds for the notices' and the ledger's kind regexes and for `game_market_notices.listing_id NOT NULL`. *Fix:* state that the contract migrations (drop the old checks, allow a null `listing_id`) ship before any code that writes order rows.

- **[low]** The average has a third reader that still reads listings only (AD-47 amendment). `EditionPrice.rarity_average` (`models/game/market/edition_price.rb:17`) feeds the snapshot's per-rarity averages from `Listing.sold` alone. `sales_in_window` and `EditionSales.call` tie-break on `id: :desc`, which means nothing across two tables. *Fix:* name `rarity_average` beside `EditionSales`, and order the merged sales by time, then by a source-stable key.

- **[low]** `PER_PLAYER` has callers beyond those the Costs list (AD-35, AD-52, AD-62 Costs). They are `app/views/library/_custom_sets.html.erb`, `app/views/pages/how_it_works.html.erb` and `app/models/deck_list.rb`. AD-35's Rule still says the library set sits "outside the 10". *Fix:* list the views and the how-it-works copy (both locales), and reword "outside the 10" to "outside the custom-set limit".

- **[low]** The new signals must join a validated list (AD-63). `AltSignal` validates `signal` against `AltSignals::SIGNALS` (`models/game/market/alt_signal.rb`), and every signal has an en/fr label (`bids_on_tied_seller` is in both locales). *Fix:* Costs: `SIGNALS` gains `archive_purchase_of_tied_deposit` and `order_sale_to_tied_buyer`, with their labels.

- **[low]** `Notice` declares `belongs_to :listing` (required) and `Notice.record(player_id:, listing_id:, …)`, and upserts `unique_by: %i[player_id listing_id]` (`models/game/market/notice.rb`). While both the old full index and the new partial index on `(player_id, listing_id)` exist, Rails picks whichever it finds first by columns (`insert_all.rb:160`). *Fix:* the Costs already say "names its index". Add `optional: true` on `listing`, and pass `unique_by:` by index name, not by columns.

- **[low]** The lock order does not place `game_market_wanted` (AD-10 amendment). It is safe only because every `RefreshWanted` runs under that player's bank lock: the event list covers only operations that hold every concerned player's bank lock, and the job takes it too. *Fix:* one clause in AD-10: "wanted rows are written only under their player's bank lock".

- **[low]** `ExpireDueOrdersJob` is in the spine's job table but not yet in `config/recurring.yml`, which lists every job twice, under `development:` and `production:`. *Fix:* a ticket note; no spine change needed.

## Checked and holding

- Rails 8.1.4.1 `upsert` / `insert_all` with `unique_by` on a partial index emits `ON CONFLICT (cols) WHERE <index predicate>` (`activerecord-8.1.4.1/lib/active_record/insert_all.rb:270-273`). Postgres 18 infers a partial unique index from that `index_predicate` (INSERT docs). AD-44's per-index upserts work.
- Postgres applies `ORDER BY` before the locking clause, so `…order(:card_season_id).lock` takes row locks in sorted order (SELECT docs, Locking Clause). The ordered locks of AD-10 and AD-43 hold. `TradeLocks.holdings` already works this way.
- Unique indexes treat NULLs as distinct by default, so the existing `index_game_market_ledger_entries_one_settlement` ignores order entries whose `listing_id` is null. A second index on `(order_id, player_id, kind) WHERE order_id IS NOT NULL` is the right shape.
- strong_migrations 2.8.0: `add_check_constraint … validate: false` then `validate_check_constraint`; concurrent indexes under `disable_ddl_transaction!`; allowing a null in a column is not flagged; a non-volatile column default is safe. This matches the spine's "expand then validate" and AGENTS.md.
- `config.active_job` sets `enqueue_after_transaction_commit = true` (`config/initializers/active_job.rb:7`), so "enqueues after commit" holds.
- Players are anonymised, never deleted (`ArchiveDeposit` comment, `ReleasePlayer`), so withdrawals, orders and ledger rows can keep the player id under the existing foreign keys.
- `ArchiveDeposit.add` sets `updated_at` on every entrustment (`models/game/market/archive_deposit.rb`). AD-63's `[ASSUMPTION]` about `updated_at` holds.
- `ForfeitBalance` already waits while `committed > 0`, and `RemovedPresence.wallets` filters `committed = 0`. "Release then forfeit" for a deleted order owner needs no new guard.
- These exist as named: `Game::BankLock`, `Ledger.post` (once, last, wallets in ascending player id), `Mint.credit`, `LedgerEntry::KINDS`, `MoveHoldings#move` (the spine's older "AcceptTrade#move" is now this module), `AuctionSettlement#refresh_edition_price` (writes `average_after_sale` on the listing), `EditionPrice.lock_row`, `WantingPlayers.call(card_season_id, except:)`, the `WantedNotices` handler on `CardListed`, `GoalsReached` (already hears `AuctionSold`), `Achievements`, `CloseDueAuctionsJob`, `DropPresenceJob`, `ReleasePlayer`, `RefreshAltSignals`, `AltSignals::MARKET_WINDOW_DAYS`, `bids_on_tied_seller` (reads exactly the one-way, outside-price and minimum-price pairs), `GoalNeeds`, `PlayerDecks.room_left?`, `ReservedCopies`, `DecksHolding`, `RemoveFromDecks`, the events `GoalAdded`, `GoalRemoved`, `CustomSetEdited`, `CustomSetDeleted`, `ListingCancelled`, `ListingWithdrawn`, `AuctionUnsold` and `Catalog::SeasonPublished`, the failure codes `:market_busy`, `:amounts_changed` and `:market_closed`, the check `game_goals_position_check` (1 to 5), and the listing sold check `(sold_at IS NULL) = (final_price IS NULL)`.
- `Settings#successor` copies every attribute, so the new version columns carry forward. `SettingsRules#amount_limits` would call `ceildiv` on nil for a null Archive price, which confirms that AD-42 needed its `[ASSUMPTION]`.
- `test/domains/game/market/ledger_write_guard_test.rb` exists. AD-61's "a test refuses any other writer, as AD-41's does" has a model to copy.
