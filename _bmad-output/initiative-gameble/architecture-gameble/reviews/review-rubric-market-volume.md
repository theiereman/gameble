# Rubric review: market-volume update of the spine (2026-11-20)

Scope: AD-58 to AD-63, the amendments of 2026-11-20 (AD-10, AD-35, AD-40 to AD-48, AD-52), the Capability map and Deferred, read against the memlog entries from "2026-11-20: update run for market volume" to the end, `spec-gameble.md` (CAP-27, 28, 37, 40, 44 to 49, Constraints), `market-economy.md`, the UX validation report of 2026-11-20, `db/schema.rb` and `app/domains/game/app/**/game/market/`.

## Verdict

**Sound, with fixes needed before the order and wanted-table stories are built.** Every market-volume capability (CAP-44 to CAP-49) and every changed capability (CAP-27, 28, 37, 40) has a governing AD and a map row. The money path follows the existing ledger, the lock order and the settings rules. Where the spine names brownfield code, it matches it: `WantingPlayers(card_season_id, except:)`, the deposit upsert that moves `updated_at`, the in-run ties `TiedBids` already reads, the `throttle_player` rate limits, and the goal check at 1..5. There are no critical findings. Three high findings would let two stories diverge on money or on events: when an order counts as expired, how several orders are ranked and assigned in the wanted table, and whether a composed operation may publish several events. Six medium findings cover notice and push semantics, the bell's seen-state, the slot grant and how the release is gated.

## Findings

- **[high]** Nothing stops a sale to an order that is past `expires_at` but that the job has not expired yet (AD-58, AD-59). `BestEligibleOrder` filters only on `status = 'open'`, and `ExpireDueOrdersJob` runs every minute. So for up to a minute (longer if the job lags) `SellToOrder` and `ApplyWantedChoices` can serve an order after its 7 days are over. The buyer gets a card after the end the spec promises, and `ExpireOrder` then finds a served order. AD-58 lists "an order left open past its end" under Prevents, but no rule enforces it at serve time. AD-45 does this for bids ("A bid at or past `ends_at` is refused"). One builder will filter on `expires_at > now` and another will not. *Fix:* in AD-59, make `BestEligibleOrder` require `expires_at > now` (with `now` read once under the locks, as `PlaceBid` does), and make `SellToOrder`/`ApplyWantedChoices` re-read it under the order lock. State that an order past its end but still `open` counts as no order everywhere: banner, table, `WantedCounts` and `BestVisibleOrder`.

- **[high]** `BestEligibleOrder` returns one order, but the wanted table needs a ranked set (AD-59). The UX (EXPERIENCE "Confier wanted table") and AD-59 say a line of several copies goes "best first and one copy each". The table also has one line per edition, so two editions of one game can each draw the same best order. Yet AD-59 names `BestEligibleOrder(card_season, except:)`, a single order, as "the only reader of eligibility". A builder will either add a second ranking query, which is the duplicate eligibility the AD prevents, or call it in a loop with exclusions. Nothing says how one order is shared between two lines. One phrase is also ambiguous: "an `order` copy left with no eligible order" is entrusted. Is a drawn order that vanished before validation a `:best_order_changed` refusal (the UX "Refused by a change elsewhere") or an entrust fallback? *Fix:* make the query `EligibleOrders(card, rarity, except:)`, ranked, with `BestEligibleOrder` as its first row. Have the table draw orders per game across all its lines, each order assigned once, best first. State that a copy is entrusted only when the table drew no order for it, and that a drawn order that is gone or changed refuses the table.

- **[high]** Composed operations publish several events without an amended AD-10 (AD-10, AD-59, AD-62). AGENTS.md says "An operation publishes one event inside its transaction", and AD-10 says "one primary … event". `ApplyWantedChoices` publishes N `OrderServed`, N `CardListed` and one `CardsEntrusted`, with no primary event, and only an `[ASSUMPTION]` in AD-59 records this. `BuySlot` for a goal publishes `SlotBought` and `GoalAdded`, with no flag at all. A builder who follows AGENTS.md will emit one event (`WantedChoicesApplied`) and rewire the notice, feed, goal and wanted handlers to it. A builder who follows AD-59 will reuse the step events. The two diverge in every handler table of AD-45. *Fix:* amend AD-10's Rule: a composed operation publishes the events of the steps it runs, in step order, and none of its own (or name the primary one). Cite `OpenPack` with the allocation as the precedent. List `ApplyWantedChoices` and `BuySlot` as the two cases, and lift the `[ASSUMPTION]` once the author accepts it. AGENTS.md's line then needs the same exception, which the author must approve.

- **[medium]** What an order's notice becomes is undecided (AD-44, AD-45 event table). A notice with an order is "unique per player and order", so `listed_above_order`, `order_served` and `order_expired` share one row per order, and each upsert replaces the last. The spine does not say that the row's kind moves, or that `listing_id` is cleared when the row becomes `order_served`/`order_expired`. If it is not cleared, the listing-end handlers' delete ("the `listed_above_order` notices pointing at it") can delete a served notice, unless they filter by kind. `OrderCancelled` and `OrderWithdrawn` are heard by "wanted refresh" only, so a `listed_above_order` line outlives its cancelled order. That line is also written even when the order cannot reach the listed edition's rarity. *Fix:* in AD-44, say that one row per (player, order) holds the latest kind. `order_served`/`order_expired` set `listing_id` to null. The listing-end handlers delete only `kind = 'listed_above_order'`. `OrderCancelled` and `OrderWithdrawn` delete the order's notice. `listed_above_order` is written only when `Order.reaching` includes the listed edition's rarity, and a `wanted` notice is written otherwise.

- **[medium]** The brownfield push channel is neither ratified nor extended (AD-44). The code pushes market notices to devices: `MarketPushNotice` keys on `BidPlaced`, `AuctionSold`, `AuctionUnsold`, `ListingWithdrawn` and `LeadVoided` through `AfterCommitBatch`. `WantedCardNoticeJob` pushes wanted cards with a one-hour quiet window (`WantedNoticePlayers.first_in`, `WantedCardNotice::QUIET_FOR`). The spine names only the bell and the private stream. AD-44 now groups wanted lines in the bell, but nothing says whether `order_served`, `order_expired` and `listed_above_order` push, or whether the wanted push keeps its per-listing quiet window, which the abuse review's list-and-cancel loop targets. Each notice story will decide on its own. *Fix:* add one bullet to AD-44 that ratifies the push path (event → `AfterCommitBatch` → job, same notices table) and lists the kinds that push. Say whether `order_served` and `order_expired` push and whether `listed_above_order` does not, and keep or replace the wanted quiet window.

- **[medium]** Where the grouped wanted line's seen-state is stored is not named (AD-44). The line "is unseen when a listing of it was created after the player last saw the line". That needs a stored "last saw" written by a POST (AD-11), and the spine names none. The brownfield has `game_bell_reads` (player, key, `read_at`, pruned to `BellRead::KEPT_PER_PLAYER = 200`) behind `MarkBellRead`/`BellLinesRead`. One builder will add a column and another will reuse a bell key. With pruning, the grouped key can fall out and the line returns as unseen. *Fix:* state that the grouped line is one fixed bell key (for example `wanted`) whose `read_at` from `MarkBellRead` is "last saw", and that pruning keeps that key.

- **[medium]** The slot grant is a snapshot, not a rule (AD-62). Today's code allows `Game::CustomSet::PER_PLAYER = 10` and `Game::Deck::PER_PLAYER = 5` (develop). "One custom-set slot to the player who held six" describes 2026-11-20. Until market volume deploys, any player can still go up to 10 custom sets. "Decks get none" depends on 1.4 lowering the deck constant to 3, and no rule binds 1.4 to that. If a player sits above the free limit at deploy with too few granted slots, `BuySlot` asks them to pay for the slot after the free limit (slot 6 or 4) that they already use. *Fix:* make the data migration's rule generic and computed when it runs: for each player and kind, `bought = max(0, count − free)`, with no ledger entry, and keep the two sentences as expected outcomes. Or record lowering `Deck::PER_PLAYER` to 3 as a 1.4 Cost in AD-52.

- **[medium]** Market volume ships into an open market with no rollout decision (AD-50, Deferred). AD-50's rollout (expand, deploy closed, open) was written for the first opening. The market is now open in production, and the spec leaves the release order "set when the work is sliced". Only the Archive shop is gated, by null prices. Orders, slots (with their default prices) and the wanted table go live on deploy. If `PlaceOrder` ships before `SellToOrder`, orders hold PiCoins for 7 days with no way to be served. The brownfield has a flag mechanism (`Game::FeatureFlag`, `KEYS = %w[duels]`, back-office features page) that the spine never names, and one story may use it while another does not. *Fix:* add a rollout bullet for market volume. Either give it a feature flag key and say what it hides (order form, slot offers, the wanted table), or bind the story order so that placing an order never ships before selling to one, and the backfill of `RefreshWanted` runs before the wanted reads ship.

- **[low]** Whether the new alt signals create ties is ambiguous (AD-63). Ties come from "one-way trades, sales outside price and minimum-price sales", computed in the same run before `TiedBids` reads them (as `alt_signals.rb` does today). `order_sale_to_tied_buyer` and `archive_purchase_of_tied_deposit` name a pair too, so a builder may add them to the tie set, which then feeds `BestVisibleOrder`. *Fix:* say that these two signals read ties and make none, and that served orders join the sale signals before the ties are computed.

- **[low]** An unban never refreshes wanted rows (AD-61, AD-46). `RefreshWanted` writes nothing for a banned player, and a banned order owner's rows are cleared by `OrderWithdrawn`. After `Accounts::PlayerUnbanned` (the event exists), their goals do not count until their next qualifying event. *Fix:* add `Accounts::PlayerUnbanned` to the refresh triggers, or accept the lag in one line.

- **[low]** Several expand/contract steps are left to AGENTS.md and not listed in Costs (AD-41, AD-42, AD-44, AD-58). They are: the ledger's "`listing_id`, `order_id`, never both" check and the new partial unique index on order, player and kind (concurrent, in its own migration); `game_market_notices.listing_id` made nullable plus the "at least one" check; the `archive_price_<rarity> >= minimum_price_<rarity>` check on the existing settings table (added NOT VALID, then validated); and the indexes on `game_market_orders` that `BestEligibleOrder` (open, card, price, created), the expiry job (open, `expires_at`) and `EditionPrice` (served card-season, `served_at`) need. *Fix:* list them in the Costs of their AD, as AD-44 already does for its indexes.

- **[low]** The new `SumArchiveStocks` hits the existing check (AD-43). `game_market_archive_stocks_quantity_check` is `quantity > 0`, so "upserts each card-season's deposits less withdrawals and deletes the rows that fall to zero" fails if the zero is upserted before the delete. *Fix:* say that the upsert takes only card-seasons with a positive remainder and the delete removes the rest. The current orphan delete becomes "no positive remainder".

- **[low]** Two ADs changed without an amendment line (AD-50, AD-51). AD-50's schedule gained `ExpireDueOrdersJob`. AD-51's `EditionSales` still lists only `sold_at`, `final_price` and `average_after_sale`, while AD-47 now has it read served orders (`served_at`, `price`). *Fix:* add "Amended 2026-11-20" to both, and in AD-51 name the served-order fields, or a shared sale shape.

- **[low]** Some job and operations details are left open (AD-50, AD-58, AD-61). The job that `SeasonPublished` enqueues for `RefreshWanted` has no name and no queue. `CheckMarketHealthJob` watches only due listings, so a stuck order expiry goes unseen. AD-50's "closing and removal jobs keep running" does not say whether order expiry runs while the market is closed. *Fix:* name the job (for example `RefreshAllWantedJob`, on the queue `HealBanksJob` uses). Extend the health check to the oldest due open order. Say that expiry runs while the market is closed.

- **[low]** The uniqueness of an open order is enforced only by the index (AD-58). `Failure(:order_already_open)` from the partial unique index alone means catching `RecordNotUnique`, which aborts the Postgres transaction after the bank lock. *Fix:* check for an open order under the bank lock before inserting, and keep the index as the backstop.

- **[low]** The spine's reading of Archive provenance is implicit (AD-60, CAP-48). The spec and the UX say staff keep "which account entrusted each Archive copy". The spine keeps depositors per edition, with no row per copy, so a withdrawal cannot be attributed to one depositor. The tied-deposit signal shows "the" entrusting account. *Fix:* state in AD-60 that provenance is the set of depositors of the edition, that the signal names the tied depositor, and that no copy is attributed one by one.
