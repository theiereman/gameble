Forge report

Brotherhood job titles

Generic per-set profile titles give way to a corporate hierarchy: five services, five grades, floating thresholds that favour early members, and a Comité de direction contested at every season.

Outcome

Hardened. The forged idea is forge-confrerie-titles.md; the mock is ux-gameble/.working/profile-titles-stats-star.html; production calibration ran on 33 members.

  • Grades at p50 / p75 / p90 / p97 of active members, with 14 / 30 / 90 / 180 days of service; the top also needs a service feat. Never demoted; thresholds float upward.
  • The worn post is the highest grade; mutation only on a strictly higher grade elsewhere. Inactive members are "en disponibilité".
  • Seats awarded at each season publication to the season's number one, from grade 3; the dethroned return to their grade.
  • Staff: Conservateur des Archives, Officier. No gender agreement, no middle-dot format, plain French job words.
  • Set titles removed under a "reorganisation of services"; a quiet launch, promotions from grade 3 in the feed.
  • Service star on every title; owner-only performance metrics with gauges, the seat race and a 12-week evolution.

Rejected, and why

  • Per-set titles: generic, about 60 per player.
  • Composed titles, parallel ladders by choice: too many titles; assignment and mutation are the satire.
  • Demotion, monthly review, double threshold: a multinational inflates titles, it does not cut them.
  • Grandiloquent and English titles (Plénipotentiaire, Trader, Head of): too abstract or not plain French.
  • Grand Maître and Directeur: too grand, faceless Direction; gender agreement.
  • Pioneer of a Legendary as a feat: nearly everyone has it.

Weak points that survived

  • 33 members: top percentiles are single players; Bourse and Stratégie thresholds are guesses until volume exists.
  • Half the active members show no title at launch.
  • The 12-week evolution needs a new weekly snapshot.
  • CAP-22 and brotherhood-tone.md must change; English titles are not written yet.
