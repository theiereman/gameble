# Live duels between friends

## Description

Two friends who are both on gameble duel live with their decks. A player challenges a reachable friend with a complete deck; the friend has 60 seconds to accept with one of theirs, or refuses. The duel is ten rounds: the caller locks a card and a stat with its direction, the card turns face up, the answerer picks a card without knowing the call, then all is revealed. Both decks stay visible, hands stay hidden, and the server holds everything: a frozen copy of both decks and their values, the rules, the clock, and what each seat may see. Timers and a reconnection window keep a duel moving; past them the game plays the move, and no duel is won by forfeit. The duel capsule, the record and the duel achievements follow in epic 44.

## Outcome

For the players: a duel with a friend where each round is a real read of the other, on the phone or the desktop, that survives a reload or a dropped connection. The signal is the release's: 30 days after release 1.4, at least 10 players have finished a live duel with no automatic move of theirs, and half of them duel again in a later week (spec, Success signal).

## Requirements

- R1 — A player challenges a reachable friend with one of their complete decks, from "DÉFIER UN AMI" on Duels, "DÉFIER" on an EN LIGNE row, or "DÉFIER EN DUEL" on the friend's row, friend profile or public profile. The friend has 60 seconds to accept with one of their complete decks, or refuses; otherwise the challenge lapses. A player has at most one duel in progress and one challenge received. A private player cannot duel; their friends see duels closed while the profile is private. (CAP-41; `duel-rules.md`, Starting a duel; AD-13, AD-54)
- R2 — Whether a player can receive a challenge is `reachable_until` on their `Accounts` row: `PlayerStreamChannel`, subscribed on every page, sets it 45 s ahead on subscribe and every 30 s while subscribed, through `Accounts::NoteReachable`, a housekeeping write with no event; unsubscribing clears nothing. One query, `Game::ReachableFriends`, feeds every "DÉFIER" and `ChallengeFriend`. "Online" elsewhere still reads `last_seen_at`. (CAP-41; AD-24, AD-55)
- R3 — `game_duels` holds the token, the state (`challenged`, `cancelled`, `refused`, `lapsed`, `active`, `finished`), both decks' snapshot, both draw orders, the first caller, `move_number`, `move_deadline_at`, the scores, the margins, the result and the times. `game_duel_seats` holds per seat the duel, challenger or opponent, the player (nulled once a deleted player's duel has finished), the deck token, the seat's stream key, `present_until` and a status (`invited`, `seated`, `left`). `game_duel_rounds` holds per played round the caller, both card-seasons, the stat, the direction, both values, the outcome and which moves were automatic. A player has at most one `seated` and one `invited` seat, each held by a partial unique index; every end of a challenge or duel sets the seats `left`; the last used deck is read from the player's latest seat. (CAP-41; AD-54)
- R4 — `AcceptChallenge` re-checks that both decks are complete and owned (else the challenge lapses, `:deck_not_ready`), copies both 12-card decks with every value, percentile and median distance of `game_duel_stats` into the duel in a fixed order (season, then card number), and draws a draw order per deck and the first caller with `SecureRandom`, in one transaction. After that a duel reads nothing from decks, holdings or the stats; a trade, sale, Monday run or deck edit never changes it. (CAP-41; `duel-rules.md`, A duel; AD-54)
- R5 — Every move is an operation: `ChallengeFriend`, `CancelChallenge`, `AcceptChallenge`, `RefuseChallenge`, `LapseChallenge`, `CallRound`, `AnswerRound`, `PlayAutomaticMove`. Each takes both seated players' bank locks (a removed player's skipped), then the duel row, then its seats; checks the state, `move_number` and whose move it is; refuses anything else (`:not_your_move`, `:stat_already_called`, `:card_not_in_hand`, `:friend_busy`, `:deck_not_ready`); raises `move_number`; publishes one event. The answer of round 10 finishes the duel and publishes `Game::DuelFinished` in place of its round event. Only `ChallengeFriend` and `AcceptChallenge` check friendship and `open_to_friends?`; later moves act for a seat whose player may since have been unfriended, made private, banned or deleted. (CAP-41; AD-10, AD-13, AD-54)
- R6 — `Game::DuelRules`, one pure object, holds the rules: calls alternate from the first caller, who also calls round 10 (A B A B A B A B B A); each player calls each of the five stats once, in either direction; a hand of 3 from the draw order and the played cards, one card drawn after each round, so round 10 is played with open hands; a round goes to the better percentile in the called direction, equal percentiles compare the exact value, still equal the round scores nothing; one point per round won; the duel goes to the higher score, equal scores to the larger total percentile margin over the rounds won, still equal a draw. The automatic move: a caller's strongest card and its most extreme call among the stats it has left; an answerer's hand card strongest on the shown card's most extreme stat among the caller's stats left; between equal choices, the larger distance of the exact value from the median, then the stat order of `duel-rules.md`, then the lower card number. Operations and tests use it; the client computes nothing. (CAP-41; `duel-rules.md`, A duel and Time and absence; AD-54)
- R7 — `Game::DuelView.for(duel, seat)` is the only way a duel becomes what a player sees: the seat's hand and locked call, the other's card once called, played cards, scores, margins, the calls each player has left counted from revealed rounds only, both decks in their fixed order. It never holds the other seat's hand, an unrevealed call or a draw order, and in state `challenged` nothing of the challenger's deck. Pages and broadcasts render from it alone and read the primary; a policy refuses a viewer who holds no seat; opponent names go through the one component that names a player. (CAP-41; `duel-rules.md`, A duel; AD-49, AD-54)
- R8 — A challenge, its cancellation, refusal, lapse and acceptance reach both players on their private streams. Each seat has its own Turbo Stream, named by the seat's opaque key and the locale, subscribed only by that seat's player; after commit a job on the `duels` queue renders `DuelView.for(duel, seat)` for each seat and pushes it to that seat's stream only. Each view carries `move_number` and the screen drops an older one. Every page load renders the same view, so a reload shows the current state. (CAP-41; AD-13, AD-55)
- R9 — The duel row stores `move_deadline_at` for the move awaited: 60 s to accept, 60 s to call, 45 s to answer. A seat's reconnection deadline is its `present_until` plus 60 s; the effective deadline is the earlier of the two for the seat that must act, and a move handed to a seat already past its reconnection deadline gets a deadline of now. An operation that sets a deadline enqueues after commit `Game::DuelDeadlineJob`, on the `duels` queue, for the deadline plus 2 s with the duel token and the `move_number`; only this job and `Game::SweepDuelDeadlinesJob`, every 10 s in both blocks of `config/recurring.yml`, run `PlayAutomaticMove` or `LapseChallenge`, which act only when, under the duel lock, the `move_number` still matches and the effective deadline has passed. An automatic move is marked on its round. The `duels` queue has its own worker in `config/queue.yml`. The countdown on screen reads the stored times and the server time. No duel is won by forfeit. (CAP-41; `duel-rules.md`, Time and absence; AD-56)
- R10 — Presence on the duel screen is a lease: the seat stream's channel sets `present_until` 20 s ahead on subscribe and every 10 s while subscribed, a housekeeping write with no event; unsubscribing changes nothing; a seat is absent once its lease has lapsed. Leaving the duel screen (another screen, closing the page, losing the connection, a native tab) counts as a disconnection. (CAP-41; AD-55; EXPERIENCE.md, Duels, Duel; Native apps)
- R11 — Bans, deletions and going private write no duel row: a removed player's lease lapses and their duel finishes by automatic play. The finishing move nulls a deleted seat's player; `Game::ReleasePlayer` nulls the player on the seats of duels already finished; a banned or deleted opponent reads "anonyme" in the duel, its result and the history. The export lists the player's duels with scores and rounds, the opponent unnamed. ## Done when

1. On staging, two friends on two devices challenge each other, accept and play a whole duel to its result, once on phones and once on desktops; a refused, a cancelled and a lapsed challenge each tell both players.
2. A timer run out plays the move and marks it "Coup joué automatiquement"; a duel one player leaves finishes by automatic play, never by forfeit.
3. Reloading the duel page, or dropping the connection and returning within 60 s, shows the duel where it stands, and no move is played twice.
4. Nothing a player's device receives, page, stream or response, holds the other seat's hand, an unrevealed call or a draw order; tests prove it for every state of a duel and every broadcast.
5. A banned, deleted or newly private opponent's duel finishes by automatic play, and a deleted opponent reads "anonyme" in the result and the history.

## Boundaries

Live duels between friends, from the challenge to the result. Not the duel capsule, the record, DERNIERS DUELS, Comment ça marche's duels section or the duel achievements (epic 44); not decks or stats (epic 42); no ranked or deferred duel, no stake (spec Non-goals and Constraints, Duels move nothing between players).
