# Digest: Steam cover-art precedents, round 1 (accessed 2026-11-02)
Most claims rest on search snippets; only the four pages marked "fetched" were read.

1. Steam Web API Terms (fetched, "Last updated July 2010"): Steam Data may be distributed "to end users for their personal use via your Application"; no endorsement/affiliation; silent on game images, mobile apps, commercial use. https://steamcommunity.com/dev/apiterms (Valve) high.
2. Steam branding page (fetched, undated): logo may not imply endorsement; not a prominent feature; silent on game art. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/branding high.
3. Graphical Asset Rules (fetched, 2022): covers what may appear on capsules only; silent on third-party reuse. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/rules high.
4. Live App Store apps showing Steam data: Gamery (fetched; imports Steam/PSN/Xbox/GOG/Epic; updated 2026-10-24; no Valve disclaimer; "Sign in with Steam" mentioned in a version note) https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1607763458 high; STM Deals (id1451434854), DeckFilter (id6474423599), Honoo (id6764015849): snippets only, medium-low. Google Play not searched.
5. Valve legal actions found are about CS:GO skin-gambling sites (2016, OpenID/commercial exploitation), mods, hardware, a Steam-client installer; none about capsule art. Snippets, medium. https://www.geekwire.com/2016/valve-cracks-third-party-gambling-sites-using-steam/
6. Apple rejected Valve's own Steam Link in 2018 (store-within-a-store), approved 2019 without purchasing. Unrelated to art. https://www.techspot.com/news/75114-steam-link-ios-ditches-app-game-purchasing-bid.html medium.
7. SteamGridDB got Nintendo DMCA takedowns in Nov 2022 (Nintendo titles). Shows publishers DMCA third-party art databases. https://pcgamer.com/nintendo-dmcas-third-party-steam-image-site-users-upload-even-more-artwork-in-response medium.
8. IGDB licensing unclear (forum 2020, aggregator 2026), low. Press kits: made for coverage, no evidence of commercial-app reuse license, low.

NOT FOUND: any Valve C&D/DMCA over capsule art or store API data; any store rejection of a Steam companion app for Steam art; any Valve statement on third-party store-art reuse; any press-kit license allowing commercial reuse; Google Play listings. Absence is not safety (US-only search, takedowns often private).
