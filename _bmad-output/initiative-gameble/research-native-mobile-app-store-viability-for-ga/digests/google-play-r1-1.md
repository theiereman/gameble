# Digest: Google Play policies, round 1 (accessed 2026-11-02)
Pages undated (only "(c) 2026 Google"); primary text fetched via summarising tool unless marked S.
1. Real-Money Gambling policy concerns real-money wagering; simulated gambling appears only in gambling-ads rules. A free app with no real money and no gambling ads is outside as written. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032 medium-high.
2. IARC: simulated gambling allowed at ESRB Teen / PEGI 12 / IARC 12+; real-currency gambling only Adults Only. Nothing on loot boxes or free random packs. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859655 medium.
3. Payments policy s.7: randomized items for purchase must disclose odds before purchase; tied to purchase. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738 medium-high.
4. IP policy lists "album and game cover art" among common copyright violations; use only content you created or have license/written permission for; Google may ask for proof; contact Play team before submission if you hold permission; no fair-use/game-database exception. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888072 high.
5. Trademark: confusing use can lead to suspension; DMCA / trademark complaint processes. Same page. Steam mark not addressed; Impersonation policy not fetched.
6. Account deletion: in-app path plus working web deletion resource; Data safety answers; enforced after 2024-05-31. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111 high.
7. No mandated alternative sign-in rule found (low-medium, absence). Reviewer access via App access instructions not retrieved (lead).
8. WebView JS-interface rule (search summary, medium-low): restrict bridge to own origin. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10768383
9. Personal developer accounts created after 2023-11-13: closed test with >=12 testers for >=14 continuous days before production; review usually <=7 days. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465 high. Org accounts need D-U-N-S (30+ days); personal accounts identity verification. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312 high.
10. Target API: new apps/updates must target Android 16 (API 36) by 2026-08-31 (ext. to 2026-11-01) (search summary, medium); a 2026-10-29 policy announcement exists, unread (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18100766). Android developer verification enforced from 2026-09-30 in BR/ID/SG/TH, global 2027 (search summary, medium) https://developer.android.com/developer-verification
11. Spam policy bans web-view apps made without the site owner's permission: a wrapper of your own site is not caught on its face. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9899034 high. Minimum Functionality: no "static" limited apps; wrapped-app specifics not stated (mitigation via native features is inference).
NOT FOUND: IARC handling of free random packs; explicit exemption of free random items from odds disclosure; Play enforcement against wrapped apps; registration fee detail; Families policy (not researched).
