# Digest: Apple login, 4.2, IP, round 1 (accessed 2026-11-02)
Guidelines page: "Last Updated: October 16, 2026" https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ (first ~100k chars read). Revisions listed: 2026-10-16, 2026-06-08, 2026-02-06, 2025-11-13 (https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=guidelines).
1. 4.8 verbatim: apps that "use a third-party or social login service (such as Facebook Login, Google Sign-In, Log in with X, Sign In with LinkedIn, Login with Amazon, or WeChat Login) to set up or authenticate the user's primary account" must offer an equivalent option limiting data to name and email, allowing a private email, and not collecting interactions for advertising without consent. Exemptions: own account system exclusively; alternative marketplace; education/enterprise accounts; government ID; "a client for a specific third-party service and users are required to sign in to their ... third-party account directly to access their content". High (P).
2. The reported "January 2026 change" is NOT verified: current text has no "exclusively" qualifier already; Apple's news lists no January 2026 revision (iClarified claim unconfirmed, a page fetch returned an unrelated article).
3. Steam OpenID would likely count (inference, medium): Steam is a third-party service as sole way to set up the primary account; "such as" list non-exhaustive; the client-of-a-service exemption fits poorly (gameble has its own state). No Apple statement on Steam/OpenID; no developer report found.
4. Sign in with Apple satisfies 4.8 (forum rejection quote, thread 765145, F). Practical: add Sign in with Apple as equivalent option and create/link a gameble account from it.
5. 4.2 verbatim: "include features, content, and UI that elevate it beyond a repackaged website"; 4.2.2 no content aggregators. No Apple list of qualifying native features; Masilotti suggests tab bar, push, native menu (B, 2023). Forum threads 835821, 825080 report repeated 4.2 rejections even with native features (F, snippets).
6. 5.2: "only includes content that you created or that you have a license to use"; 5.2.1 no protected third-party material (trademarks, copyrighted works) without permission; 5.2.2 if the app "displays content from a third-party service, ensure that you are specifically permitted ... under the service's terms of use. Authorization must be provided upon request." Cover art + "Steam" name fall here (inference). 4.1(c) (added 2025-11-13): no other developer's icon, brand or product name in icon/name without approval.
7. 2.1(a): demo account (or approved demo mode) required for logins; Steam-only sign-in raises reviewer-access question (Steam Guard); Sign in with Apple would ease it (inference). 5.1.1(v): in-app account deletion required; "core app functionality not related to a specific social network ... must provide access without a login or via another mechanism" (unclear whether Steam is a social network; medium inference). 5.1.1(i) privacy policy required.
NOT FOUND: Apple statement on Steam/OpenID; Steam+4.8 reports; verified Jan 2026 change; 4.2 qualifying feature list; privacy label requirements; 5.2 documentation format.
