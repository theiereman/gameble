# Digest: Apple randomized items / gambling / age rating, round 1 (accessed 2026-11-02)
Guidelines page "Last Updated: October 16, 2026" (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), ~100k chars read.

1. 3.1.1 loot boxes: "...randomized virtual items for purchase must disclose the odds... prior to purchase." Scoped to purchase; with nothing purchasable the literal text does not apply (reading, medium).
2. 3.1.1 gifting concerns purchasable items only.
3. 5.3 Gaming, Gambling, Lotteries: every sub-rule (5.3.1-5.3.4) concerns real money, sweepstakes or lotteries. Free, no cash-out, no prize: outside on the text (high on text). 5.3.4: lotteries need consideration, chance and a prize.
4. No Apple rule found on non-monetary player-to-player trading (medium; middle of page read via summary).
5. Age-rating definitions (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions): Loot Boxes = "randomized virtual items for purchase"; Simulated Gambling = "betting or wagering without using real money..."; tiers 4+/9+/13+/16+/18+; loot boxes -> 9+, infrequent simulated gambling -> 13+, frequent -> 18+. Australia: loot boxes 16+, simulated gambling R18+. Brazil: gambling or loot boxes declared -> A18. Probable rating 4+ or 9+ (inference, medium).
6. Apple news (items 2026-05-08..10-29): Australia 15+ removed from 2026-06-18; questionnaire asks about social media capabilities from 2026-07-09 (required from Sept 2026): relevant to profiles/trading. https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=ks775gmo
7. 2025 overhaul details: secondary only (announced 2025-07-24), medium.
8. Comparables: Pokemon TCG Pocket 9+ with Loot Boxes (has IAP); Hearthstone Loot Boxes (IAP). Not free-only comparisons. Odds-disclosure UI not retrieved.
9. No evidence of extra scrutiny for a gamble-like name. Forum anecdote (Nov 2023): app with gambling banners but no gambling rejected under 5.3.4 for lacking licenses (https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/742447, low-medium). Mitigation: state in review notes: no real money, no prizes.

NOT FOUND: explicit Apple statement on free randomized items; Apple rule on non-monetary trading; evidence about "gameble" specifically; original 2025 overhaul text; Marvel Snap/Topps ratings.
